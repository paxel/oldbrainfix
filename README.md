<h1 align="center">Old Brain Fix</h1>

<p align="center">
  <b>Capture a thought in one tap. Get nagged until it is sorted or done.</b><br/>
  Android and desktop. Everything stays on your devices — no account, no server, no tracking. Free.
</p>

---

For anyone who forgets things. Capturing a thought takes one tap; the app
keeps asking until every Note is sorted into an Area and Topic, or turned
into a Routine or Reminder that fires until it is done.

Work in progress. The domain language is in [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md), the
decisions in [docs/adr](docs/adr), the plan in the GitHub issues.
