# Fixture corpus

Generated by `packages/oldbrainfix_core/tool/gen_fixtures.dart` from the Dart core; the desktop core loads every `log.jsonl` and must read the same `expected.json`. Do not edit by hand.

- `schema.sql`: the tables and indexes of the store.
- `signing.json`: entries signed by the writer key, with the bytes the signatures cover.
- `question_marks.json`: the background's shapes and their scatter over a region.
- `schedules.json`: RRULEs read, written back or refused, schedules with their Occurrences, days normalised, and the instants of wall-clock times around daylight-saving switches in Central Europe.
- `routines/alarms.json`: the Agenda, alarm plan, Missed Summary and Nudge of the `routines` log at held moments with this Device's settings, on the same clock.
- `pair_codes.json`: pair codes for a QR and for typing, codes as typed, and what is no code.
- `keys.json`: the writer's key record, and another key claiming its device.
- `sync/`: two Devices that met once and wrote more since: their logs (`host.jsonl`, `joiner.jsonl`) with their blobs and the keys they hold (`settings.json`), the host's vector, the joiner's `POST /sync` request (with its proof for the PIN 246810) and the host's answer, both with the Area keys they hand over, and what both read after the joiner took it in (`expected.json`).
- `conflicts/`: two Devices that met once, then edited the same Note text, Area name and Routine schedule apart, wrote the same value on both sides and renamed a Topic on one side only: their logs, the session between them, the Conflicts both find (`expected.json`), the host keeping the older version of each (`picks.jsonl`, at the held clock), and what both read after a second session.
- `crypto.json`: messages sealed with XChaCha20-Poly1305 under given keys, nonces and what they are bound to; a key's id; a blob's name in the shared folder.
- `folder/`: two Devices that met once, so the joiner holds the host's keys; the host wrote more since, a new Area among it, and synced through the shared folder: both logs with their blobs and the keys each holds (`settings.json`), the folder the host wrote (`oldbrainfix-sync/`, nonces counted from 1001 on), and what the joiner reads from it (`expected.json`).
- `bundle/`: two Devices that met once; the host wrote more since, a new Area among it, and a bundle for the joiner: both logs with their blobs, the keys each holds and what each was told the other holds (`settings.json`), the bundle (`host.obfsync`, nonces counted from 2001 on), and what the joiner reads from it (`expected.json`). The desktop core compares the files inside the zip byte for byte, not the zip itself, whose headers its zip library writes its own way.
- `notes`: Notes of every Importance; a text fixed in place; an edit dated back that loses to the text before it; two texts half a millisecond apart written in reverse order; text with quotes, backslashes, control characters, umlauts and an emoji.
- `sections`: Amends; a chain of Merges where the merged Note had an Amend of its own; two Notes merged into each other, kept under the lower id; a Merge into a Note this Device does not hold.
- `places`: The whole Starter Set planted, one starter Area renamed; an Area of the user with a colour and an icon and a Topic; Notes filed into Areas and Topics; a Note whose Topic vanished falls back to Unsorted; a deleted Area takes its Topics with it.
- `trash`: A Note in the trash; one trashed and restored; one trashed long enough ago to be past restoring; a merged Note whose target is in the trash.
- `edits`: What the desktop writes beyond the other scenarios: Areas and Topics made and renamed, an Area coloured and given an icon, another one with both cleared again; Notes captured with each Nudge time, some into a place; Notes filed and filed again; Topics deleted with their Notes sent back to Unsorted (one that spawned a Routine included), into the Area and into the trash, and an empty one; Areas deleted with their Topics, their Notes sent into the trash and back to Unsorted.
- `media`: Photos and voice recordings: Notes captured with photos, recordings or both, with and without text; photos and recordings added later; a photo whose Exif, ICC and comment are stripped; the same photo on two Notes kept once; Photo Texts and Transcripts, empty ones for nothing found; a Note with a Photo Text and one with an untranscribed recording merged into another; a trashed Note whose media waits for nothing; a photo from another Device whose bytes are not here.
- `routines`: Routines and Reminders spawned from Notes and made with their Note behind the scenes: several times a day with its own Re-nag and Escalation, all-day weekends, Reminders on the hour the clock skips and the one it shows twice, every other day, the last of the month rescheduled to two times, a Re-nag set and cleared, an unchanged reschedule that writes nothing; Occurrences Done, Done at a given time and unchecked; a Routine trashed, another trashed and restored; Notes trashed with their Routines and restored, trashed without them, and trashed with them for good; Unsorted Notes for the Nudge.
