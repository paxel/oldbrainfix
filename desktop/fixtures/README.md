# Fixture corpus

Generated by `packages/oldbrainfix_core/tool/gen_fixtures.dart` from the Dart core; the desktop core loads every `log.jsonl` and must read the same `expected.json`, which holds what each Note hands to another app among it. Do not edit by hand.

- `schema.sql`: the tables and indexes of the store, and its version.
- `signing.json`: entries signed by the writer key, with the bytes the signatures cover.
- `question_marks.json`: the background's shapes and their scatter over a region.
- `schedules.json`: RRULEs read, written back or refused, schedules with their Occurrences, days normalised, and the instants of wall-clock times around daylight-saving switches in Central Europe.
- `ics.json`: Routines and Reminders as calendar files at a held stamp, the export of all of them and each shared on its own, with ends by COUNT and UNTIL, timed and all-day events and titles that need escaping and folding; the list of all of them by name, and names as typed in that order.
- `routines/alarms.json`: the Agenda, alarm plan, Missed Summary and Nudge of the `routines` log at held moments with this Device's settings, and of each Note the Occurrence due or Missed of each of its Routines and what its card ticks today, on the same clock.
- `not-today/not_today.json`: what the Routines of the `not-today` log come to (#269): each with what is skipped, by Tomorrow too, and the Occurrences Tomorrow added, and at held moments the Agenda, the alarm plan, the Missed Summary, what each Note asks to check off, what the notifications may close and each Routine's history, its Streaks and its Adherence with the skips beside it.
- `markers/markers.json`: what the values of a later version in the `markers` log come to (ADR 0034): the Routines read and those whose schedule needs a newer version, by Note too, the people of the shared Area with their roles, what each Device may do there and which Areas go to it, the Overrides; grant values, Profile records and schedule fields read as known or as of a later version.
- `frogs/frogs.json`: each Frog of the `frogs` log, the open ones, those Done, the rotation, the Frogs of each Note, and the "Eat a frog" section the day before, on and after the clock held, on the same clock.
- `tallies/tallies.json`: each Tally of the `tallies` log with its fields and what each multiplies, the Tallies in the lists, the entries of each with what each counts for its fields in millionths, everyone's and the user's own, its removed entries, its charts by day, week, month and year on the same clock, and the known units and a typed one converted both ways, shown and read in both systems, numbers kept and read back, and the system of a few regions.
