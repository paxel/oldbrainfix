# Fixture corpus

Generated by `packages/oldbrainfix_core/tool/gen_fixtures.dart` from the Dart core; the desktop core loads every `log.jsonl` and must read the same `expected.json`. Do not edit by hand.

- `schema.sql`: the tables and indexes of the store.
- `signing.json`: entries signed by the writer key, with the bytes the signatures cover.
- `question_marks.json`: the background's shapes and their scatter over a region.
- `notes`: Notes of every Importance; a text fixed in place; an edit dated back that loses to the text before it; two texts half a millisecond apart written in reverse order; text with quotes, backslashes, control characters, umlauts and an emoji.
- `sections`: Amends; a chain of Merges where the merged Note had an Amend of its own; two Notes merged into each other, kept under the lower id; a Merge into a Note this Device does not hold.
- `places`: The whole Starter Set planted, one starter Area renamed; an Area of the user with a colour and an icon and a Topic; Notes filed into Areas and Topics; a Note whose Topic vanished falls back to Unsorted; a deleted Area takes its Topics with it.
- `trash`: A Note in the trash; one trashed and restored; one trashed long enough ago to be past restoring; a merged Note whose target is in the trash.
- `edits`: What the desktop writes beyond the other scenarios: Areas and Topics made and renamed, an Area coloured and given an icon, another one with both cleared again; Notes captured with each Nudge time, some into a place; Notes filed and filed again; Topics deleted with their Notes sent back to Unsorted (one that spawned a Routine included), into the Area and into the trash, and an empty one; Areas deleted with their Topics, their Notes sent into the trash and back to Unsorted.
