# Fixture corpus

Generated by `packages/oldbrainfix_core/tool/gen_fixtures.dart` from the Dart core; the desktop core loads every `log.jsonl` and must read the same `expected.json`, which holds what each Note hands to another app among it. Do not edit by hand.

- `schema.sql`: the tables and indexes of the store.
- `signing.json`: entries signed by the writer key, with the bytes the signatures cover.
- `question_marks.json`: the background's shapes and their scatter over a region.
- `schedules.json`: RRULEs read, written back or refused, schedules with their Occurrences, days normalised, and the instants of wall-clock times around daylight-saving switches in Central Europe.
- `ics.json`: Routines and Reminders as calendar files at a held stamp, the export of all of them and each shared on its own, with ends by COUNT and UNTIL, timed and all-day events and titles that need escaping and folding; the list of all of them by name, and names as typed in that order.
- `routines/alarms.json`: the Agenda, alarm plan, Missed Summary and Nudge of the `routines` log at held moments with this Device's settings, on the same clock.
- `frogs/frogs.json`: each Frog of the `frogs` log, the open ones, those Done, the rotation, the Frogs of each Note, and the "Eat a frog" section the day before, on and after the clock held, on the same clock.
- `tallies/tallies.json`: each Tally of the `tallies` log with its fields, the Tallies in the lists, the entries of each, everyone's and the user's own, its removed entries, its charts by day, week, month and year on the same clock, and the known units and a typed one converted both ways, shown and read in both systems, numbers kept and read back, and the system of a few regions.
- `checklists/checklists.json`: each Checklist of the `checklists` log with its Lines in the order they show, their parents, what shows checked and their keys, the one-pass read of them, which Notes may become one, the text each goes back to as a plain Note, lines of text read as Lines, a text split at its line breaks, and keys made after and between others and told valid or not.
- `checklist-routines/checklists.json`: the same of the `checklist-routines` log, and each Checklist as checked for the Occurrences of its Routines and Reminders with what is Done, the Occurrence each is on and how it shows from the day before the clock held to three days after, on the same clock.
- `shopping-lists/shopping.json`: each Shopping List of the `shopping-lists` log with its Lines in the order they show, their reappear times, which are struck, the day each comes back and the day an open one came back, the text each goes back to as a plain Note, and the lists on the Agenda each way it may show them, read at moments around the starts of the days they come back on the same clock; which Notes are one and may become one, the Routines with what is Done, reappear times read, and names as an added one is compared.
- `sent-checklist/`: Ada sends a Checklist to Ben (ADR 0030): her log (`sender.jsonl`) and Profile settings (`settings.json`), what sending wrote (`sent.jsonl`, ids counted from 0xa01, at held clocks), the file in format 2 (`checklist.oldbrain`), what Ben's accepting wrote (`accepted.jsonl`, from 0xb01); Ben checks a Line and adds one (`receiver.jsonl`, from 0xc01); Ada renames, deletes and adds Lines (`changed.jsonl`, from 0xd01) and sends the update (`update.jsonl`, `update.oldbrain`), which Ben applies (`updated.jsonl`, from 0xe01); Ada turns it back into a plain Note and sends that (`plain.jsonl`, `plain.oldbrain`), which Ben applies (`plained.jsonl`, from 0x1001); what he reads and the changes at each step (`expected.json`).
- `sessions/sessions.json`: each Session of the `sessions` log with its pool, counts and rule, the Tallies it picks from and may pick, its Routine with what is Done, from entries too, and today's Occurrences with their picks, their targets and the entries recorded for them, the day before, on and after the clock held, on the same clock.
- `pair_codes.json`: pair codes for a QR and for typing, codes as typed, and what is no code.
- `keys.json`: the writer's key record, and another key claiming its device.
- `sync/`: two Devices that met once and wrote more since: their logs (`host.jsonl`, `joiner.jsonl`) with their blobs and the keys they hold (`settings.json`), the joiner's host certificate among them (`tls:cert`, `tls:key`, random bytes counted from 6001 on), the host's vector, the joiner's `POST /sync` request (with its proof for the PIN 246810 and its certificate's fingerprint, which the host keeps) and the host's answer, both with the Area keys, the own Profile and its backup key, the backup key each then holds, the host's on both (`backup_keys.json`), the own Profiles both hold after it, the host's named by the user and taken over by the joiner, whose own still had the defaults (`profile.json`), and what both read after the joiner took it in (`expected.json`).
- `conflicts/`: two Devices that met once, then edited the same Note text, Area name, Routine schedule and Checklist Line apart, wrote the same value on both sides, renamed a Topic on one side only and checked and unchecked Lines apart: their logs, the session between them, the Conflicts both find (`expected.json`), the host keeping the older version of each (`picks.jsonl`, at the held clock), and what both read after a second session, the Checklist with the later check or uncheck of each Line among it.
- `crypto.json`: messages sealed with XChaCha20-Poly1305 under given keys, nonces and what they are bound to; a key's id; a blob's name in the shared folder.
- `transcript_prompts.json`: the lead-ins Whisper gets: name lists with the app's words as given, and three stores, as their entries, seen with the English and the German app's words and Starter Set names (`ui`, from the app's ARB files): the whole Starter Set with one Area renamed, one with names too long to all fit, repeats in another case among them, and one with more names than are listed; Transcripts guarded against the prompts Whisper got for names and words as given (`guards`): the lead-in alone, at the start, twice, in another case and punctuation, cut short and after speech; the name list, the app's words, the whole German prompt; one, two and three names alone and amid speech, names of several words, and real speech naming a place, kept as it is.
- `text_marks.json`: status marks in texts: for each text the marks found at the start of its lines, each as written, after spaces, tabs and a list item's bullet, and those that are none, in the middle of a line, after a numbered item, joined into another emoji or another box or tick; every line's mark swapped to each state, ☐ put at the start of every line, one past the last among them, and the line of every offset, counted in code points; with variation selectors, astral characters and line breaks of every kind.
- `text_tags.json`: tags in texts: for each text the tags found, each as written, as searched and where it lies, counted in code points; in many scripts, after spaces of any kind, punctuation, emoji and status marks, with a `-` inside and at the end, and those that are none: numbers alone, headings, `##`, a `#` after a letter, a digit, `/`, `\` or `&`, as in "C#" and web addresses.
- `markdown.json`: formatted text: for each text its lines with their kind, level, number and parts, as read, what it reads as without its marks, whether it uses a format, the text escaped, as HTML, its status marks, those at the start of a table's cell included, its tables with their rows and cells, its tags but those in code and link destinations, the offsets where a typed shortcut ends and the word at every offset; bold, italic, strike-through, code, links, headings, quotes, bulleted and numbered items one level in, tables with ragged rows, escaped pipes, alignment and the line marking the first column as headings, and what is no format: three hashes, code blocks, images, raw HTML, a link inside a link, what is no table. Then the edits: each format put on and taken off at a cursor, on a word, a selection and lines, links put on, changed and taken off, every line format on and off, items in and out, a table's cell set, its rows and columns put in, moved and deleted, its first column marked, a new table put in, with the text and the selection they gave; status marks of cells changed; and which link destinations may be opened. Offsets are counted in code points.
- `folder/`: two Devices that met once, so the joiner holds the host's keys; the host wrote more since, a new Area among it, and synced through the shared folder: both logs with their blobs and the keys each holds (`settings.json`), the folder the host wrote (`oldbrainfix-sync/`, nonces counted from 1001 on; its keys file carries the own Profile, renamed since they met, and its backup key), what the joiner reads from it and the backup key it holds then (`expected.json`) and its own Profile after (`profile.json`).
- `bundle/`: two Devices that met once; the host wrote more since, a new Area among it, and a bundle for the joiner: both logs with their blobs, the keys each holds and what each was told the other holds (`settings.json`), the bundle (`host.obfsync`, nonces counted from 2001 on), what the joiner reads from it (`expected.json`) and its own Profile after, both Profiles named by the user since, so "Which one is you?" waits (`profile.json`).
