## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-31

### Added
- On the phone and the desktop, 30 minutes after the first change that people synced by files have not been sent yet, a notification "Changes for Bob and Carol" comes, repeats like the Nudge and waits out the Quiet Hours until each of them was sent their file, and tapping or clicking it offers each person their file in turn, while a file counts as sent, there and with "Send changes to …", only once an app was picked on the phone's share sheet or the file was saved on the desktop.
- On the phone and the desktop, the first start asks whether the user is new to Old Brain Fix, adds a Device of their own or restores a Backup, and "Add a Device of mine" shows this Device's pair code or takes the other Device's, then syncs before any setup, bringing the Profile, Areas and everything else, so that the phone skips the Starter Set and only offers the speech model and the alarm setup, while a failed or cancelled Pairing returns to the first screen.
- On the phone, text Capture has a Gallery button next to Photo and Voice that picks one or more pictures from the phone into the Note as its photos, each with its Photo Text.
- On the phone, an audio file shared in from another app, such as a WhatsApp voice message, becomes a voice Note with its Transcript, several shared at once one Note with a recording each, and a file the phone cannot decode is kept in the Note with an announcement that it could not be transcribed, whose tap shows the reason.
- On the desktop, "From file…" in the capture field and files dropped onto the window make pictures and sound files, such as a WhatsApp voice message in Ogg Opus, m4a, mp3 or WAV, into one Note with their photos and voice recordings, which wait in the capture until it is saved with what is typed, each with its Photo Text or Transcript, a sound file the desktop cannot decode kept in the Note with an announcement, once the Note is saved, whose click shows the reason; voice Notes in Ogg Opus or MP3 from the phone also play and are transcribed on the desktop.
- On the phone and the desktop, a person can be blocked on their Profile page or with "Decline and block" in the preview of a Sent Item from them, which deletes everything they wrote but their check-offs from all of the user's Devices, keeps and shows nothing more of theirs, refuses their Sent Items, leaves the Shared Areas they manage and lists them in a folded "Blocked" section of People, from where Unblock fetches their writing again.
- On the phone, Sync shows the phone's own pair code as a QR with the typed code under it for another phone, a tablet or the desktop to join, Share shows an invite to a Shared Area the same way, and the phone hosts automatic sync for its paired Devices while the app is open and during its background sync, so phones and tablets pair and sync with each other without a computer, and the desktop's Sync view takes such a code typed or pasted in to join a phone or tablet.
- On the phone and the desktop, a person can be added from their Profile card: on the phone "Send my Profile" on the Profile page sends a link through any messenger and "Show my QR" shows it as a QR, "Add a person" in People takes a link pasted, a QR scanned with the camera, which marks the person verified at once, or a QR in a picture, and a card link tapped in another app opens in the app; on the desktop "Copy my Profile link" and "Show my QR" on the Profile page do the same and "Add a person from a link" in People takes a pasted link; a person new to the user is offered "Send yours back" on their Profile page.
- On the phone and the desktop, "Share with…" on the Share page of an Area shares it with a person in People who is not nearby, as Manager or Member, asks once whether changes travel with them through a shared folder or as files, and sends the offer as a file through any messenger, which the desktop saves where the user says; the other phone or desktop, given the file opened or dropped onto the window, shows "Ann shares Household with you as Member" with Accept, Compare codes, Decline and "Decline and block", keeps nothing before Accept, lists undecided offers on the Agenda, and the person's Profile page offers "Send changes to Ann" when changes travel as files.
- On the phone and the desktop, "Replace Note text" and "Append to Note text" under each Transcript and Photo Text in the Note editor, also in a merged Note's Section, take the generated text over into the Note's text, "Remove generated text" removes it after asking once while the recording or photo stays, and "Transcribe again" and "Read the text again" make it anew, also after it was removed.
- On the phone and the desktop, Routines and Reminders ring with a chime of Old Brain Fix's own instead of the notification sound, and "Alarm sound" in Settings picks another sound of the phone or, on the desktop, a sound file that the app plays itself while its notifications stay silent, with the own chime ringing in place of a file that is gone or cannot be played, while Escalation still rings with the system's alarm sound.
- On the desktop, "Open the AI port" in the Settings lets an AI model of the user's choice, local or in the cloud, work on their Notes, Areas, Topics, Routines and Reminders for one task through an MCP server reachable only from this computer and only with its token, with the client config copied for Claude Code, Claude Desktop or another client, while sync pauses and every change waits until "Done, review" lists them by kind with before and after for "Accept all" or "Reject all", a review left open waiting for the next start.
- On the phone and the desktop, "Routine, Reminder, Frog or Session" on a Note's card, or "Frog" in the desktop's Note editor, makes a Frog, a job with no time done once, and the Agenda offers two Frogs a day in an "Eat a frog" section under today's Occurrences, the same on all of the user's own Devices, where "Another one" swaps a Frog, checking one off hides the other for the day, and the heading, on the phone "Frogs" on the Areas list's bar and on the desktop "Frogs" on the left beside Agenda, All Notes and Unsorted, open the Frog view with the open Frogs and a folded "Done" section, while a long press, or a right-click on the desktop, deletes a Frog made by mistake and the trash restores it.
- On the phone and the desktop, "Make it a Tally", in a Note's ⋮ on the phone and in its editor on the desktop, gives the Note fields, each a number with a unit from a known list, shown in metric or imperial as set in Settings or by the region, or typed freely, an optional target and whether a day counts the sum or the largest, and "Add entry" records the fields prefilled with their targets, a date and a line of text, which the Note shows as a list of entries with their authors, by default only the user's own in a Shared Area, and as one chart per field by day, week, month or year, while a removed entry or a hidden field can be restored.
- On the phone and the desktop, "Session" in "Routine, Reminder, Frog or Session" on a Note's card, or "Session" in the desktop's Note editor, makes a Routine that picks Tallies from a pool for each Occurrence, as many as its count or a weekday's own count says, by Random, Rolling or Least selected, and the Agenda lists today's picks with their targets under it, the same on all of the user's own Devices and for everyone in a Shared Area, where tapping or clicking a pick records an entry prefilled with its targets, "Another one" swaps a pick, and the Occurrence is Done once every pick has an entry.
- On the phone and the desktop, Share in the ⋮ of an Occurrence on the Agenda, on the desktop also after a right-click, shares its Routine as the whole series with its end, or its Reminder, as a calendar file, which the desktop saves where the user chooses, as it does with Export.
- On the desktop, the All Routines and Reminders page lists every Routine and Reminder alphabetically by name, opens one's editor with a click and writes one calendar file of all of them with Export.
- On the desktop, a Routine's History, with its Streak now and longest, the last 7 days, its Adherence this week, this month and over the last 30 days and the log of every Occurrence, Done early, on time or late, Missed, due or still to come, with the snoozes this computer recorded, opens from the menu of its Occurrence on the Agenda and from the History button of its editor, and "Show Streaks on this computer" in Settings hides the Streaks there.
- On the phone, "Show my calendar's appointments on the Agenda" under "Read from your calendar" on the Calendar page asks for calendar access and opens the list of the phone's calendars, none picked at first, and the Agenda then shows the appointments of the calendars picked beside the Occurrences of every day, read only, marked with their calendar's icon, colour and name, without the calendar mirror's own entries and without copying or syncing anything, while a tap shows an appointment's place and description and "Copy into a Note" in its ⋮ or after a long press makes a new Note of its title, time, place and description.
- On the phone, the Note editor and "Add to Note" type in two rows: the whole text on top, where a tap puts the cursor at the nearest word boundary, and above the keyboard one line of the text around the cursor whose ◀ and ▶ buttons move it one character, repeating faster while held, and whose long press starts selecting with those buttons; Settings of this Device can switch it off.
- On the phone and the desktop, "Make it a Checklist" on a Note's card and in its editor turns every line of the Note but the first into a Line with a checkbox, in formatted text without the mark of a bulleted or numbered item or a quote, a line starting with a space or a tab going under the line above into a group drawn as one frame under it, where a checked Line stays in place struck through, checking every Line of a group checks its parent and checking the parent checks the group, the editor adds, renames, moves, indents and deletes Lines, full cards are checkable and compact ones show "4 of 9 checked", "Uncheck all" readies the list for the next use and "Back to a plain Note" writes every Line back into the text with "✓ " before the checked ones, while in a Shared Area every Member checks and adds Lines and only the author and the Managers change the rest, two renames of one Line made apart become a Conflict, Sent Items, Bundles and Backups carry Checklists, and a Routine or Reminder spawned from a Checklist starts each Occurrence with every Line unchecked, on all Devices and for everyone in a Shared Area, and counts the Occurrence Done once every Line is checked, by anyone, while checking it off by hand still works.
- On the phone and the desktop, "Make it a Shopping List" on a Note's card and in its editor turns every line of the Note but the first into a Line with a checkbox and no groups, where checking a Line strikes it through and moves it to the end with the day it comes back, such as "back Thu, Nov 12", which is the same weekday one to four weeks on as chosen under "Reappear time" in the Line's ⋮ beside "Delete", one week for a new Line, and from the start of that day the Line is open again at the top, while a Line set to Once is gone once checked, adding a name the list already has brings its struck or gone Line back at the top instead of adding it twice, making it, "Back to a plain Note", the cards, Shared Areas and Conflicts work as for a Checklist, a Sent Item carries its Lines with their reappear times, all of them open on arrival and its preview showing each Line's reappear time, an update that makes a Shopping List of a Note says so, and its updates add, rename and remove the sender's Lines as for a Checklist, and Settings of this Device choose how Shopping Lists show under today on the Agenda: each Line that came back today on its own row to check off there, one row per list on a day when Lines came back such as "Groceries: 3 back" (the default), one row per list on every day it has open Lines, or not at all, and "Add to a Shopping List" in the ⋮ of every capture screen makes each line typed, and each line of a voice recording's Transcript once it is there, a Line of the list picked and keeps no Note, while a photo held in the capture keeps it a Note, drops a list picked and offers none, and a capture kept as a Note instead is announced at the top as done.
- On the phone and the desktop, a ☐, ✅ or ❌ anywhere in the text of a Note or of a Section, also in the middle of a word or in a table's cell but not in inline code, becomes a box when the text is saved, a tap or click on it opens a small menu with Check, Fail and Open, the current state left out, each change is kept beside the text with who made it and when, so the boxes of a Section can be changed too and in a Shared Area everyone with a role there changes any box while adding and removing boxes stays with the Note's author and the Managers, copies, shares and sent Notes show each box in its state now, Transcripts and Photo Texts stay as they are, the editor puts ☐ in at the cursor, also in the middle of a word, "Show ticks" in the ⋮ of a Note's card and of its editor shows after each box who changed it last and when, kept for that Note on that Device, and a box's menu has "History" with every change, who made it and when.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-10-27

### Added
- On the phone, the speech model download is offered once after the Starter Set.
- The phone's Routine editor builds a day of the month, the first to fourth or last weekday of the month, and an end (never, on a day or after a number of times) without typing a repeat rule.
- A big red button on the phone's About page.
- On the phone, a swipe left on the Agenda opens the Areas list and a swipe right from the Areas list returns to the Agenda, and every swipe between Agenda, Areas list, Area and Topic moves the pages with the finger, completing past the middle or with a fling and springing back otherwise.
- A voice recording with a Transcript or a photo with a Photo Text can be removed in the Note editor (on the phone also in the card's ⋮ menu), from every Device and the Shared Area, while the text stays on the Note and in search.
- On the phone and the desktop, a label beside every play button shows the playback speed of voice recordings and opens a menu with 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5 and 2 times normal speed; the choice applies at once and stays on this Device.
- On the phone and the desktop, the speech model settings offer Whisper's small model (488 MB) beside the base model (148 MB), better with English words in German speech but slower; choosing one downloads it, and only the chosen model is kept.

### Changed
- The phone's Agenda shows the whole week: after tomorrow come the five following days, each under its weekday and date, an empty day as "Nothing planned".
- On the phone, an Area lists only its Notes without a Topic; a search in the Area still finds the Notes of its Topics.
- The phone's Capture page has large Photo and Voice buttons above the reminder choices instead of icons in the top bar.
- On the phone, every single choice is a column of radio buttons, and every switch, button or list sits on its own line below its text.
- A Note card on the phone shows, top to bottom, the Profile of another person who wrote it, the date with the location, its Area and Topic or Unsorted where the screen does not already name them, the Note, and one row of icons for File into, Routine or Reminder, Merge and ⋮, which holds Importance, Send to a person, the calendar, Add to Note and Delete.
- On the phone and the desktop, Whisper is given the Area and Topic names, and for German speech the app's own words and common English words such as Meeting or Update, so Transcripts spell them as they are written.

### Fixed
- With three-button navigation, the end of the phone's pages no longer lies under the navigation bar.
- On the phone, the Unsorted list, the Nudge screen, the Areas list, an Area, a Topic, All Notes and the trash no longer show Notes as they were before a page opened on top of them, such as the Nudge screen from its notification, changed them.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added
- Capture a thought as a text Note with one tap on +; leaving by Back or switching apps keeps it.
- Take a photo as a Note, optionally with a few words; kept photos carry no location data.
- Record a voice Note that starts at once and ends with one big stop button; the recording is kept to play back.
- Transcripts of voice Notes, written on the phone by Whisper in English or German, after a one-time speech model download on request or from a file of your own.
- Text in photos and screenshots is recognised on the phone by ocrs, offline, and kept with the photo.
- Share images, text and links from other apps into a new Unsorted Note.
- App shortcuts, Quick Settings tiles and home-screen widgets that open voice, text or photo capture directly, also for hardware buttons.
- An Agenda home-screen widget that lists today's Routines and Reminders to check off, with the Unsorted count.
- English and German.
- Light teal and anthracite dark themes that follow the system or a choice of your own.
- Notes as embossed cards over a background of hand-drawn question marks that drift slowly while scrolling.
- Areas and Topics of your own, two levels deep, with an optional colour and icon per Area.
- A Starter Set of Areas and Topics (Household, Work, Car, Health) to pick from on first start.
- Unsorted: a Note that is not filed, turned into a Routine or Reminder, merged or deleted.
- Deleting an Area or Topic asks whether its Notes go back to Unsorted, into the Area, or into the trash.
- A notes browser with search across Note text, Transcripts and text in photos, swipe navigation through Areas and Topics, and a diary of cards per Area and Topic.
- A Note editor to fix text in place, add dated parts, set Importance and file the Note.
- Importance: important cards stand out, unimportant ones are dimmed and can be hidden.
- A trash that keeps deleted Notes, Routines and Reminders for 30 days.
- Merge a Note into another; it becomes a dated part of that Note.
- Typed text in the editor survives the app being closed or killed.
- Every Note and every added part records when and where it was captured, the calendar event and Bluetooth device at the time, and for shares the app they came from.
- Named Places that show "Home" or "Work" instead of coordinates, matched offline.
- The Agenda as home screen: today's Routines and Reminders, then tomorrow's, dimmed.
- Routines and Reminders made from any Note, each linking back to it.
- Schedules from once to iCalendar repeat rules, with several times per day, fixed across early or late ticks and daylight-saving changes.
- Alarms on the minute with Done and Snooze (10 min, 30 min, 1 h), even from the lock screen and after a restart.
- A step-by-step permission setup and a red Agenda banner while a needed permission is missing.
- Re-nag: an ignored alarm rings again at an interval, per phone or per Routine, until Done, snoozed or Missed.
- A Missed card on the Agenda that lists what was Missed until you tap "Seen".
- One catch-up notification instead of a burst of old alarms after the phone was off.
- Escalation: an ignored Routine can turn into a full-screen alarm with sound.
- A history per Routine with each Done, Missed and snoozed time, its Streak and its Adherence, with Streaks that can be hidden.
- Nudges that keep reminding you of Unsorted Notes, with a nudge time picked at capture.
- An "x Unsorted" banner on the Agenda.
- Quiet Hours that hold Nudges back at night while Routines still ring.
- Deleting a Note with Routines or Reminders asks whether they go to the trash too.
- Add any Note, Routine or Reminder to your calendar app, filled in, without a calendar permission.
- Export all or picked Routines and Reminders as a shareable calendar file with their repeat rules and alarms.
- Optionally keep all Routines and Reminders in a device calendar of your choice, updated as they change and removed when turned off.
- Settings that belong to each phone and are never synced.
- Sync the phone with the desktop over the home network by scanning or typing the code the desktop shows; the two stay paired.
- Paired phone and desktop find each other on the home network and sync on their own, at once while the app is open and about every 15 minutes in the background.
- Things changed on two Devices before a sync show up as conflicts on phone and desktop, and the version you pick stays on every Device.
- Phone and desktop also sync through a shared folder such as Syncthing, Nextcloud or a USB stick, on their own and in the background, with everything in it end-to-end encrypted per Area.
- Send what is new as an encrypted sync bundle file through any messenger and import it on the other paired Device.
- Share a single Area with another person as Manager or Member after confirming their Profile, with their picture on the Notes they write there; every other Area stays private.
- Send a Note with its Routines and Reminders as a signed file carrying your Profile to another Old Brain Fix user, encrypted when you know them, who previews it with your name, picture and short code, can compare that code with you in person, and accepts it into Unsorted with its alarms on; later updates or cancellations from the same sender change it or move it to the trash.
- Encrypted Backups of all Areas, Notes, photos, voice recordings, Routines and their history, by hand or weekly into a folder you choose, restored on a new install with your passphrase or the recovery key on a printable Recovery Sheet.
- One passphrase and one Recovery Sheet open the Backups of all your Devices, a restored Backup brings your Profile back, and an opt-in Google backup keeps only the newest encrypted Backup, offered with the passphrase or recovery key on a new phone.
- An optional App Lock with a PIN, fingerprint or screen lock that guards your Notes while capturing and alarms stay open.
- Optional Area Locks that keep single Areas and their Notes hidden until unlocked, with their alarms, Agenda rows, widget and calendar entries shown as "Private Routine" or in full, still checkable.
- An About page with the version, documentation, source, a way to report problems or write to the developer, and a donation link; it and the desktop's About list every third-party component and its licence.
- A documentation site in English and German with a glossary, what's new and a privacy policy, linked from About on the phone and the desktop.
- A desktop app for Linux, Windows and macOS in the phone's look that captures, edits and sorts Notes, manages Areas, Topics, Routines and Reminders, shows the Agenda like the phone, records and plays voice Notes, keeps pasted pictures as photo Notes, writes Transcripts with Whisper and Photo Texts with ocrs, and finds Notes by their text, Transcripts and Photo Texts, with drag-and-drop filing, keys in place of swipes, notifications with Re-nag from a tray icon that starts at login unless switched off, and a small capture popup that a global hotkey, the tray or the `oldbrainfix capture` command opens, the command also keeping a given text as a Note without opening anything; it comes as .deb, AppImage and Flatpak, from Homebrew and Scoop, and sits in the application menu with its own icon.
- A Profile with your name, picture and short code, taken from the phone's name or the computer login without asking and shared by all of your Devices, where pairing two Devices that each have a named Profile asks once which one is you; its page gathers Backups, your Devices, the People you know and the settings, and every other person's Profile shows their short code, a way to compare it, a name and picture of your own for them that only your Devices see, and opens their Notes; the notes browser can show only the Notes of the people you pick, yourself included.
- On the phone, a crash shows what happened with buttons to restart the app and to mail a report to the developer, and an unexpected end is offered as a report at the next start.
