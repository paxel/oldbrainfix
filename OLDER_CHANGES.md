## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-07

### Added

- An iPhone version of Old Brain Fix, for iOS 26 and newer, which goes to testers through TestFlight once the Apple account is connected; it is not in the App Store yet. The user guide and the privacy policy say where the iPhone differs from Android.
- On the desktop, a right-click in a text field opens a menu at the pointer with Cut, Copy, Paste and Select all, Cut and Copy greyed while nothing is selected. With words selected in a formatted text, and in the quick note and Add to Note while their format bar shows, it also offers the format bar's Bold, Italic, Code, Link and Code block for them. A right-click on the selection keeps it selected.
- On the desktop, every text the Note dialog only shows can be selected and copied: another person's text, a Note's in the Trash, Sections, Transcripts, Photo Texts and the History. Dragging the mouse selects, also over formatted lines, and Ctrl+C copies; a right-click opens Copy and Select all. Links, boxes and tags in them still work with a click.
- On the desktop, a selection being dragged scrolls: while the mouse button is held, the wheel scrolls and the selection follows, and dragging past the top or bottom edge scrolls by itself, faster the further out the pointer is. This works in every text field and in the texts the Note dialog only shows.
- In a formatted text, a Markdown task list item such as `- [ ] milk` or `- [x] bread` becomes a box, ☐ or ✅, on the phone and the desktop: as the space after `]` is typed, when it is pasted and when a Markdown file is imported. It can then be ticked like any box. In code, a code block, a Code text or a plain text it stays as written. Copy as text, Share and Save as Markdown write such a box back as `- [ ]` or `- [x]`, a failed one as `- [x]` with its words struck through, so other Markdown apps show a checkbox.
- A Note with a Frog shows it as a Frog badge in the Note editor, on the phone and the desktop: Open, then Eaten today or Eaten yesterday. A tap offers Eat now while it is open, and Remove the Frog, which asks once and moves it to the Trash. The "+" no longer offers a Frog while the Note has an open one.
- Links to places in the app, on the phone and the desktop: a Markdown link such as `[Backups](oldbrainfix://settings/backups)` opens a Note, an Area or Topic, the Agenda, Unsorted, the Settings or one of their sections, All Routines and Reminders, Backups, Sync, the Profile, the Frogs, New Session or a page of the user guide. "Link to…" in the format bar picks the target from a list with a search, so nobody types an address. A link works on every Device of a Shared Area; where its target cannot be seen, it says "Not available here", and other Markdown apps show its words. A link can also open a file of the shared folder, on the desktop the synced folder, with the system's app, after one question that names the file and the app; files elsewhere never open. The help Notes use these links: the Quick start's description links each step to its place, and "What the icons mean" links to the user guide.

### Changed

- On the phone and the desktop, a table in a formatted text, a pasted one too, is edited right in the Note editor, cell by cell, instead of as its `|` lines or full screen. A tap or click on a cell edits it in place; Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter and the arrow keys move between the cells and into and out of the table. "+" adds a row or a column, and a row's number or a column's letter opens a menu to insert, move and delete it and to align a column left, centred or right. On the desktop the columns' widths can be dragged; they are not stored. Every change is written back as the table's Markdown lines, and "Show as lines" shows them for editing as text until "Show as table".
- The help Checklist "Quick start" says where and how for every step: each Line names the place on the phone and what to tap, such as "Tap the round + at the bottom right of the Agenda, then Text, type a thought and tap 1 h." Its description, under its name, gives the desktop's way for each step: the menus, keys, drag and right-click. A Quick start made by 0.4.0 keeps its words.
- Deleting a Note always moves its Routines and Reminders into the Trash with it, and restoring the Note brings them back, on the phone and the desktop, also when an Area or Topic sends its Notes to the Trash. The question names them, such as "Delete this Note and its 1 Routine?", and offers only Cancel and Delete; "Delete only the Note" is gone. A Routine left without its Note before shows "The Note is in the trash" or "The Note was deleted" in All Routines and Reminders.
- Tallies count in sets instead of multipliers, on the phone and the desktop. "Counts in sets" in a Tally's setup gives its entry form "Add set", which adds a set that starts with the values of the set before, such as 60 kg × 10 and then 65 kg × 8; each field totals the sets by its rule, reps by their sum, a weight by the largest. A Tally with a multiplier counts in sets, so 3 Rounds × 20 Push-ups shows as three sets of 20. A new kind of field, the Check field, is a box in each entry whose ticks the chart counts, such as one Tally per room for cleaning.
- A Tally's bar length, a day, a week, a month or a year, is set in its setup and synced with the Note instead of picked on its card, and its targets count per bar. The charts show slim bars with gaps, the newest on the right, as many as fit; a single bar no longer fills the chart. On the desktop the AI port reads sets and Check fields and sets them up.
- A Session's card and its page list its past runs, newest first: the date and each picked Tally, done or not done, on the phone and the desktop.

### Fixed

- On the phone, the swipe between the Agenda and the Areas list reacts at once to a quick short flick and no longer stutters: the two pages lie side by side and the Areas list is ready before the swipe starts. Back and the folder go between them as before. Tapping the version under About Old Brain Fix seven times shows how long the last frames took, at the bottom left, for reporting from the phone; seven more taps hide it.
- The desktop draws arrows, ticks and other symbols that showed as empty squares, such as the → in Notes, with the fonts Noto Sans Symbols 2 and Noto Sans Math.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-11-04

### Added

- On the phone and the desktop, a field of a Tally can multiply another field of the same Tally, such as Rounds of Push-ups: its page has "Multiplies" with Nothing and the other fields, and the setup shows it as a badge. In each entry the other field counts as the two values multiplied, so 3 rounds of 10 push-ups count as 30 in the totals, targets and charts, and the entry shows "Push-ups 3 × 10 times"; an entry without a value for the multiplier counts as it is. The multiplier has no total, chart or target of its own. It is read from the entries as they are, so older entries count the same; hiding the field it multiplies makes it a field of its own again. On the desktop the AI port sets it up and reads entries with the product.
- On the phone and the desktop, Settings has an Agenda section with "Hide what's checked on the Agenda", off by default and kept for each Device. Switched on, a Done Occurrence, a Checklist Routine's checked Line and a Session's pick with its entry leave the Agenda at once instead of staying struck through, and a group with nothing left goes with them, "Eat a frog" too once today's Frog is eaten; the phone's widget follows. On the desktop the AI port reads the setting.
- On the phone and the desktop, a Routine or Reminder is checked off from its Note: the Note editor shows at the top the Occurrence that is due, such as "Due 15:00", or else the latest Missed one with its day, with Done, and for a one-time Reminder, the Note's only one, also "Done and move to trash", which moves the Note with the Reminder to the trash and closes the editor. A Note's card, compact or detailed, has a box for today's Occurrence of each of its Routines and Reminders, which checks it off and unchecks it as the Agenda's box does; a long press on the card still opens the editor. Snooze stays in the notification.
- On the phone and the desktop, the pool of a Session, on its Note's page, the New Session page and the Routine editor, shows the #tags found in its Tallies' texts above the list, such as #sport and #cleaning, those most Tallies carry first. Picking one or more shows only the Tallies carrying any of them; ticked Tallies the filter hides stay ticked, and a line says how many. New Tally from a filtered list starts the new Tally's name with the picked tags. The filter is not saved and lasts while the page is open; Rolling's order is changed only in the whole list.
- On the phone and the desktop, Settings ▸ Agenda has "Move a Note to the Trash when its one-time Reminder is done", off by default. It is a setting of the person, the same on all of their own Devices, and never reaches anyone else. Switched on, Done on a one-time Reminder that is a plain Note's only Routine or Reminder moves the Note with it to the trash, wherever it is checked off: the Agenda, the phone's widget and full-screen alarm, a notification, the Note editor or dialog, which closes, and the card. Checklists, Shopping Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Notes with a Routine or a second Reminder stay. Unchecking does not bring the Note back; it comes back from the trash, still Done. On the desktop the AI port reads the setting.
- On the phone and the desktop, a formatted text holds code blocks: lines between two fences of three backticks or tildes show in a fixed-width font on a grey background, exactly as written, with no format, table, #tag or box read inside. A word after the opening fence, such as "```bash", names the language and is kept without showing. As in CommonMark, a block ends only at a fence of the same character at least as long as the opening one, so a block opened with four backticks keeps a line of three, and a block without a closing fence runs to the end of the text. "Code block" in the format bar puts the lines of the selection into a block, or takes a block's fences off. Before, fenced code showed as written.
- On the phone and the desktop, a plain Note's whole text can be Code: it shows exactly as written in a fixed-width font on a grey background, without line wrapping, and nothing in it is read as a format, a #tag or a box. In the Note editor it scrolls sideways; on a card it is cut off at the edge. The editor's new "Text format" badge switches between Plain text, Formatted text and Code; a formatted text can become Code but still never plain again. When a pasted text looks like a script or code, such as one starting with "#!/" or lines ending in ";" or "{", the editor offers "Keep as code" under the text, and the paste does not format the text. Search finds the words of a Code text, and sharing and copying give it as written. A Note with a Code text can't be made a Checklist, Shopping List, Tally or Session until its text format is changed back. Older versions of the app show a Code text read-only. On the desktop the AI port reads the format of a Note's text and sets it.
- On the phone and the desktop, every Note has a History, on the Note editor's bar on the phone and under Note ▸ History on the desktop. It lists every change, the newest first, with who made it and when: the text before and after, Amends added and changed, Lines added, renamed, checked, unchecked, deleted and restored, the Importance, the Area and Topic, the kind, the text format, the trash, and photos and recordings. "Use this version" brings an older text of the Note or of an Amend back as a new change; nothing is deleted. On the desktop the AI port reads it.
- On the phone and the desktop, a web address written into a text, starting with "http://" or "https://", is a link: a tap or click on it opens it in the browser, in plain and formatted texts, on cards, in Sections and in the read views of the Note editor and dialog. A tap beside it still switches the card, and a long press still opens the editor. Punctuation right after the address, such as a full stop or a closing bracket, is not part of it. An address inside code, a code block, a Code text or a link stays as it is, and a #tag or a box inside an address is none. The text itself is not changed.
- One switch, Simple or Advanced, on the phone and the desktop. The first start asks it on the Starter Set page, Simple picked; Settings changes it any time. It is a setting of the person, the same on all of their own Devices. Simple offers making Notes, Checklists, Shopping Lists, Reminders and Routines; Advanced adds Tallies, Sessions, Frogs, Topics, the format bar and roles in Shared Areas. What was made before stays visible and usable in Simple. Someone who never chose, as everyone updating from 0.3.1, has Advanced. On the desktop the AI port reads the choice.
- On the phone, Settings ▸ Look has "Fat fingers", off by default and kept for each Device. Switched on, text shows 1.3 times its size on top of the system's font size, and icons, buttons and tap targets 1.3 times theirs; the Agenda's rows grow with them, and so does the text of the home-screen widget. Actions that no longer fit on a page's bar, such as some of the Note editor's, go into ⋮ at its end. On the desktop, Settings ▸ This computer has "Bigger text and icons", off by default, which zooms the window to 1.3 times. The AI port reads the setting.
- On the phone and the desktop, the app explains itself with its own Notes, in their own Area "Old Brain Fix" with a brain icon: "Quick start", a Checklist of steps to try that shows on the Agenda as one row with its count, and "What the icons mean", with pictures of the icons and their names and meanings in its text. An update also brings a "What's new in X.Y.Z" Note with that release's notes, one for each version passed. Each Note is made once per person, in the language of the Device that makes it first; the user's other Devices get it by sync and make none, and a deleted one does not come back. The Area icon "brain" can be picked for any Area.
- On the phone, "Export as Pack…" in the menu of an Area, a Topic and a Note, on a card's ⋮ and on the Note editor's bar, makes a Pack: a file of copies of Notes for another Old Brain Fix user, shared through any messenger, mail or files. A tree shows what it holds, every Note ticked with its Routines, Photo Texts and Transcripts, while Reminders, photos and recordings start unticked; "Tick all", "Untick all" and the size update as you tick. A Note brings what it is, its text, Amends, kind, Lines unticked, Tally fields, Session pool, Routines and an open Frog, never Tally entries, ticks, check-offs, its Capture Context or its history; ticking a Session ticks the Tallies it picks from, and a Session without one stays out. "Add my Profile" signs the Pack with your Profile. Opened with the app, a Pack shows the same tree, all ticked, and "Add" puts the copies into new Areas and Topics, "Health (Pack)" where a Health exists, Routines starting that day and Reminders already past left out. With a Profile it says who it is from, offers to add them to People, and "Claim as mine" unticked leaves each Note a "From Ann" badge until you remove it; a changed Pack and one from a person you blocked are refused. On the desktop, "Export as Pack…" is in the right-click menu of an Area, a Topic and a card, on the card's ⋮, in the Note menu and in the Note dialog; the tree opens as a dialog and "Save…" saves the file where you choose. "File ▸ Open Pack…" and a Pack dropped onto the window open it in the same tree, "From Ann" shows on the card and as a badge in the Note dialog, and the AI port reads who a Note came from.
- On the phone and the desktop, the schedule in the Routine editor starts with a line, "When", that shows it as a sentence in the app's language, such as "Weekdays Mon, Thu at 08:00, 15:00, until 12/31". Typing in it sets the repeat, the times, the start and the end as you type, with the next word, weekday names and times offered under it; words it does not understand are marked and change nothing. It covers everything the Simple schedule can do; on an Advanced schedule it shows a summary and "Switch to Simple to type". Only the schedule is saved, not the sentence.
- The guide has a page on syncing through Syncthing or Autosync.
- The guide has a page on calendar apps such as FamilyWall: their appointments on the phone's Agenda through the phone's calendar, and Routines and Reminders exported into them as a calendar file.
- On the phone and the desktop, Settings ▸ Send feedback writes to the developer: a field for what happened or what could be better, ticks for the version, the system and the device and the install method, ticked at first, and for the last crash report (phone) and Notes as a Pack picked in the Pack's tree, not ticked. A preview shows exactly what is sent; Mail opens the mail app with it, and Share hands it with the Pack to any app on the phone, while the desktop saves it as a file and the Pack to attach.
- On the phone and the desktop, the Agenda's bar has New Session, after New Routine or Reminder, in Advanced. It makes a Session as New Session on All Routines and Reminders does. On a narrow phone, and with Fat fingers, the bar's last buttons move into ⋮.

### Fixed

## [0.3.1] - 2026-11-01

### Changed

- The Note card's ⋮ no longer holds the Importance, which is changed through its badge in the Note editor.
