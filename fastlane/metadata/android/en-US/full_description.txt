<b>Old Brain Fix is a memory aid for anyone who forgets things.</b> Capturing a thought takes one tap. The app then keeps asking until every Note is sorted into an Area and Topic, or turned into a Routine or Reminder that rings until it is done.

It is built for brains that drop things: the idea in the shower, the pills at eight, the form that is due on Friday, the thing you meant to tell somebody. Nothing has to be sorted at the moment you think of it, and nothing gets lost because you didn't.

<b>CAPTURE</b>
• <b>One tap.</b> Text, a photo or a voice Note, from the + button, a widget, a Quick Settings tile, an app shortcut or a hardware button. Things shared from other apps land as a Note too.
• <b>Context that makes sense later.</b> Every Note keeps when and where it was captured ("Home" or "Work" instead of coordinates), the calendar event at the time and the Bluetooth device connected, so "buy more sauce" still means something days later.
• <b>Voice and photos become searchable.</b> Voice Notes are transcribed on the phone by Whisper, in English or German, and text in photos and screenshots is read on the phone by ocrs. Nothing is sent anywhere for it.

<b>SORTED OR DONE</b>
• <b>Unsorted until you deal with it.</b> A new Note waits in Unsorted, and Nudges keep reminding you until none are left. Quiet Hours hold them back at night.
• <b>Areas and Topics of your own,</b> two levels deep, with a colour and icon, and a starter set to pick from.
• <b>Routines and Reminders made from any Note:</b> from once to any repeat rule, several times a day, with alarms on the minute, even from the lock screen and after a restart.
• <b>Re-nag:</b> an ignored alarm rings again until it is Done, snoozed or Missed; if you like, it turns into a full-screen alarm with sound.
• <b>The Agenda</b> as home screen: today's Routines and Reminders, what was Missed, and how many Notes are still Unsorted — also as a home-screen widget to tick off.
• A history per Routine with its Streak and Adherence, and your Routines in your calendar app if you want them there.

<b>YOURS, AND PRIVATE</b>
• <b>No account, no server, no tracking, no ads.</b> Everything stays on your devices.
• <b>Phone and desktop sync directly,</b> end-to-end encrypted, over the home network, through a folder you already share (Syncthing, Nextcloud, a USB stick) or as a file through any messenger.
• <b>Share a single Area</b> with another person; every other Area stays private. Send one Note with its Reminders to someone else, who decides whether to accept it.
• An optional App Lock and Area Locks that hide single Areas, while capturing and alarms keep working.
• Encrypted Backups with a printable Recovery Sheet.

<b>ALSO</b>
• A trash that keeps deleted things for 30 days.
• English and German. Light and dark themes.
• A desktop app in the same look.
• Free and open source (Apache-2.0 / MIT).
