Capturing a Note takes one tap and never asks anything, not even for the App Lock. Sorting can happen while capturing or later; until a Note is sorted it is Unsorted, and the Nudge reminds you of it. Each box below shows the steps on the phone and on the desktop.

The desktop app captures in the quick note, a dialog over its window, and in the capture popup that its hotkey opens over all other windows.

Capture a thought

Write, say or photograph a thought before it is gone.

  • Tap + on the Agenda.
  • Tap Text, Voice, Photo, Gallery, Paste or File.
  • Type, speak or take the photo.
  • After speaking, tap Stop.
  • Tap 10 min, 30 min or 1 h.
    •
  • Press Ctrl+N, or click File ▸ New Note.
  • Type what's on your mind.
  • Click 10 min, 30 min or 1 h.
    •

    The Agenda's "+" fans out the ways to capture, upward from the button, each an icon with its word: Text opens the capture screen with the keyboard up, Voice starts recording at once, Photo opens the camera, Gallery opens the system's picture picker, where several pictures can be picked at once, and Paste opens the capture screen filled from the clipboard, and File opens the system's file picker and attaches the files picked (see Attach a file to a Note). Voice, Photo, Gallery, Paste and File show only where the Device has them. While the fan is open, "+" reads as Close; tapping it again, tapping outside or Back closes the fan. A long press on "+" opens the capture screen with the keyboard up at once, as Text does, without the fan; a screen reader names it with the button, and on the iPhone VoiceOver lists it among the button's actions.

    The capture screen, the quick note, holds the text field ("What's on your mind?") at the top, under it "Remind me to sort it in" with 10 min, 30 min and 1 h, and under them the wide button Open in the editor; nothing else, in Simple and Advanced alike. On the phone they sit right under the text and stay there when the keyboard opens. Each of the three saves the Note and sets its Nudge to that time. Everything else, Photo, Voice, Gallery, Paste, File, the Kind, Add to Note…, Remind me…, File into…, the Frog, the Importance and the format bar, is in the Note editor that Open in the editor opens (see Sort while capturing).

    • Voice: the elapsed time runs above one big Stop button. There is no holding a button. A pause is ignored unless Settings ▸ Capture ▸ End a recording after a pause is set on this Device to 2, 3, 5 or 10 s: then, once you have said something and stay silent that long, the recording ends and is kept as Stop keeps it, in the editor too. After Stop you are back on the quick note with the recording above the text and can type. With a speech model, "Transcribing…" and a moving line show under the recording; the Transcript then goes into the text, as a new paragraph after what you typed, once you pause typing for a second, and stays folded under the recording as Transcript. Leaving before it is done saves the Note without it in the text; the recording gets its Transcript all the same. The screen stays on while recording. Locked anyway, the iPhone goes on recording until you stop it or such a pause ends it; on Android, locking the phone or leaving the app ends the recording and keeps it. The first time, the app explains why it needs the microphone and asks for it.
    • Photo, Gallery and File: what was taken or picked shows above the text field, and the text becomes optional ("A few words about it (optional)").
    • Paste: the clipboard is read only on this tap. Its text goes into the field, its picture into the Note as a photo. When it holds neither, a line above the field says so.
    • Open in the editor saves the Note at once, Unsorted with a Nudge in 1 h, and opens it in the Note editor in place of the quick note, with everything the editor offers. Filing it there drops the Nudge, as filing always does. Leaving the editor goes back to where the capture was opened from. It waits until something is typed, recorded or picked, and behind the App Lock it is not offered, as the editor shows the Areas.

    Back, on the iPhone the back arrow at the top left, and the app going to the background save as well; the Nudge is then 1 h. There is no Save at the top. An empty Note is not saved. After saving, the new Note's card shows on the Agenda under "Just captured", in its detailed view with the same options, for what was not done while capturing, merging into another Note among them; a tap switches it to its compact view and back.

    On the desktop, File ▸ New Note, Ctrl+N (Cmd+N on macOS) and "New Note", the "+" at the top of the Agenda and "+ New Note" at the top of Unsorted, open the quick note as the dialog "New Note" over the window, its field reading "What's on your mind?". In All Notes, an Area or a Topic, "+ New Note" opens a menu; its Note opens the quick note. The dialog's cross, Esc, Alt+Left and a click beside it close it and keep what is typed with a Nudge in 1 h. Enter starts a new line; Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on macOS) saves the Note with a Nudge in 1 h, or without one once the Note is sorted, and Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2 and Ctrl+3 save it with a Nudge in 10 min, 30 min or 1 h. Once something is typed or waits in it, "Remind me to sort it in" with 10 min, 30 min and 1 h appears, and under it the wide button Open in the editor with the pencil; nothing else.

    • Open in the editor saves the Note at once, Unsorted with a Nudge in 1 h, and opens it in the Note dialog in place of the quick note, its text ready for typing. The list stays where it was. The badges are there under This Note, Voice, Paste and Attach a file… under Add, and Merge into… and Add to a Shopping List under Tidy.
    • With an Area or Topic shown in the list, the Note is filed there and needs no Nudge: one Save takes the place of the three Nudge buttons, and Open in the editor files it there too.
    • Ctrl+V in the empty field puts the clipboard's text into the field. A pasted picture waits above the field as a "Photo" line and is saved with what is typed as the Note's photo.
    • Files dropped onto the window open the quick note. They wait above the field, a picture as a "Photo" line, a sound file as a "Voice recording" line and any other file as a line with its name, and are saved with what is typed as one Note, the other files as its Attachments (see Attach a file to a Note).

    Text still typed, and pictures and files still waiting, when the window closes are kept as a Note with a Nudge in 1 h.

    On the iPhone the first Photo asks for the camera, and Paste may show iOS's own "Allow Paste" question before the clipboard is read. Both questions come from iOS; the app itself asks nothing while capturing.

    Sort while capturing

    File the Note, or set it up, before you leave it.

  • Tap + ▸ Text and type.
  • Tap Open in the editor.
  • Tap the place badge and pick an Area.
  • Tap Back.
    •
  • Press Ctrl+N and type.
  • Click Open in the editor.
  • Click the place badge and pick an Area.
  • Press Esc.
    •

    The quick note is meant for getting a thought out of the head: it holds the text, the three Nudge buttons and Open in the editor, nothing else. Open in the editor saves the Note, Unsorted with a Nudge in 1 h, and opens it in the Note editor, on the desktop in the Note dialog, where everything else is: under This Note the badges for the Kind, the place (File into…) and the Importance; the format bar; at the end of the page or the dialog, under Tidy, Merge into…, which adds it to a Note you already have as a Section, and under Add "+" with Remind me…, in Advanced also Frog and Session, and the buttons to add more:

    • Photo, Gallery and Paste on the phone, Paste on the desktop: a photo, pictures picked or the clipboard's text and picture go onto the Note.
    • Voice on the phone opens the recording page with its big Stop; Voice in the Note dialog records under the Note's files, with its time and a big Stop over the groups. Stop, Back and closing keep the recording on the Note. On either, a pause ends the recording and keeps it only where Settings ▸ Capture ▸ End a recording after a pause is set to 2, 3, 5 or 10 s on that Device, once you have said something and stay silent that long.
    • Attach a file… picks files of any kind.
    • Add to a Shopping List: each line of a Plain Note becomes a Line of the list picked, and the Note goes. With one list it names it, such as Add to Weekly. It is offered for a Note without photos, files or Sections and with nothing set up from it.

    Filing the Note, or setting up a Routine, Reminder, Frog, Session or Tally, drops its Nudge, as it always does. A capture opened inside an Area goes into that Area, and one Save takes the place of the Nudge buttons.

    A Note with a recording can go into a Shopping List too, with or without the speech model: say "milk, eggs and bread". The recording stays on the list as a Section under a tear line, so you can listen to an item that was misheard, and the typed lines become Lines at once. Once the recording is transcribed, on this Device or another, the items said become Lines as well, split at commas, at "and" and "und" and at pauses. Without the speech model they follow once it is downloaded. They are added once: transcribing again adds nothing.

    Settings, under "Quick note", keeps one switch, Nudge buttons, on unless switched off: off, a single Save takes the place of the three buttons and saves with a Nudge in 1 h, as leaving does. It belongs to this Device. On the phone, Settings is the row under "Settings of this Device" on the Profile page, behind the Profile picture in the Agenda's top corner.

    Capture without opening the app

    Start a Note from the home screen, or from anywhere on the computer.

  • Long-press the Old Brain Fix icon.
  • Tap Voice, Text or Photo.
  • Type, speak or take the photo.
  • After speaking, tap Stop.
  • Tap 10 min, 30 min or 1 h.
    •
  • Press Ctrl+Alt+N anywhere.
  • Type what's on your mind.
  • Press Enter.
    •

    On Android the quick entry points open their capture screen directly, without the fan and without a screen in between: Text with the keyboard up, Voice recording, Photo with the camera open.

    • Widgets: "Voice note", "Text note" and "Photo note", one button each, and "Voice, text, photo" with the three side by side. The "Agenda" widget shows the days the Agenda shows, with boxes to check off today's rows, and the Unsorted count, which opens the Unsorted Notes (see Routines, Reminders and the Agenda).
    • Quick Settings tiles: "Voice note", "Text note" and "Photo note". Add them by editing the tiles in the notification shade. On the phone's lock screen, Android asks to unlock first.
    • App shortcuts: long-press the Old Brain Fix icon for Voice, Text and Photo.
    • Hardware button: the app does not read keys itself. A phone's own key or gesture setting, or a button-mapper app, can start the shortcuts "Voice note", "Text note" or "Photo note". Where the setting sits on many phones is described in Capture with a hardware button.
    • Sharing: text, pictures and sound files shared from another app open the capture screen filled in, sound files as voice recordings. The app they came from is recorded with the Note.

    While the App Lock is on, its lock screen has its own "+" with the same fan and the same long press, and every quick entry point opens Capture without unlocking.

    On the desktop, the hotkey, Ctrl+Alt+N unless changed, opens the popup "Capture a Note" over all other windows; File ▸ Quick note opens it too. It works while Old Brain Fix runs; where there is a tray, the app keeps running there after its window is closed. The popup holds a field, the Nudge buttons and under them Open in the editor, nothing else. Enter saves the Note with a Nudge in 1 h, or without one once the Note is sorted, and Shift+Enter starts a new line; Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2 and Ctrl+3 (Cmd on macOS) save it with a Nudge in 10 min, 30 min or 1 h, as the Nudge buttons do. Esc and the close button keep what is typed the same way, and an empty popup just closes. Open in the editor saves the Note the same way, closes the popup and opens the Note in the window's Note dialog, bringing the window back from the tray; it waits until something is typed.

    The key is set in Settings under "Quick capture": Record keys takes the keys pressed next, and the field "Hotkey" takes them typed out; it needs Ctrl, Alt or Super. On X11, Windows and macOS the app takes the key itself. On Wayland the desktop manages it: it asks once and lets you change the key in its own keyboard settings. Where the key is taken or no hotkey is possible, the line under the field says so. The tray's menu also has Quick note.

    The command line:

    • oldbrainfix capture opens the popup.
    • oldbrainfix capture buy milk keeps "buy milk" at once as an Unsorted Note with a Nudge in 1 h, without a window.
    • oldbrainfix capture - keeps what comes in on standard input.

    Launchers, key-binding tools and macro pads can run these commands, also where the desktop offers no hotkey.

    On the iPhone a long press on the icon offers no Voice, Text or Photo. Instead:

    • The Capture widget, on the home screen and the lock screen: its button opens voice, text or photo, as chosen when the widget is edited; text until then.
    • Control Center: the controls "Voice note", "Text note" and "Photo note".
    • Sharing: "Old Brain Fix" in the share sheet takes text, links, pictures and the app's own files, no sound files. iOS lets it not open the app: a small card says it was kept, and it opens in Capture the next time the app opens or comes back. The app it came from is not recorded.

    Sort what is Unsorted

    File the Notes you captured without sorting them.

  • Tap the Nudge, or x Unsorted on the Agenda.
  • Tap Select and tick the Notes.
  • Tap the folder, File into…, in the bar.
  • Pick the Area or Topic.
    •
  • Click the Nudge, or Unsorted in the sidebar.
  • Ctrl-click the cards of the Notes.
  • Right-click one of them ▸ This Note ▸ File into….
  • Pick the Area or Topic.
    •

    While Notes are Unsorted, the Agenda shows "x Unsorted" on top, which opens them. The Nudge is one notification for all of them, such as "3 Notes are Unsorted", first at the earliest time picked while capturing. It offers 10 min, 30 min and 1 h to snooze; tapping it opens "Waiting to be sorted" with the three oldest Unsorted Notes and "+x" for all of them.

    An ignored Nudge comes again until no Note is left Unsorted, after the time set in Settings under "Nudge again after" (1 h unless changed). In the Quiet Hours, 22:00 to 08:00 unless changed, Nudges wait until they end; Routines and Reminders still come on time, but without sound unless Escalation is on, and only the first ring of an all-day one waits too (see Routines, Reminders and the Agenda).

    On the desktop the Nudge shows as a notification, such as "3 Notes are Unsorted", "Click to sort them.". On Linux and Windows it has the buttons 10 min, 30 min and 1 h to snooze, and "Open Old Brain Fix"; on macOS it shows without buttons. Clicking it opens the window on Unsorted. The switch "Notifications on this computer" in Settings turns notifications off. The Agenda shows "x Unsorted" as well, which opens Unsorted.

    On the desktop, File into… is also under This Note in a card's right-click menu and its ⋮, Ctrl+M opens it, and the badge "Area and Topic" in the Note dialog opens it. A card can be dragged onto an Area or Topic in the sidebar. Ctrl-click and Shift-click select several cards; File into… then files all of them. A selected card's right-click also offers Importance under This Note, Merge into… and Move to trash under Tidy for all of them, and in the Trash Restore; Ctrl+Z takes back the trash or the Merge of all of them at once.

    On the card of an Unsorted Note, on the phone and on the desktop, Merge into… merges the Note into the one picked, which it then shows as a Section, the Notes added to last at the top; it has the same name as on a filed Note's card and in the Note editor.

    A Note opened from Unsorted, or on the phone from "Waiting to be sorted", moves on once it is sorted: filed, merged, added to a Note, made a Checklist, Shopping List or Tally, or given a Routine, Reminder, Frog or Session. The Note editor, on the desktop the Note dialog, then shows the next Unsorted Note, and once none is left the Agenda shows. Sorted from the list itself, the list stays; the desktop selects its first card. A Note opened from an Area, the search or the Agenda stays open, and a Note moved to the trash moves nothing on. On the desktop Ctrl+Z brings a sorted Note back into Unsorted and shows it there again.

    Without the mouse, Down selects the first card in Unsorted, Ctrl+M opens File into…, a few letters of the Area or Topic and Enter file it, and the next card is selected by itself. Unsorted has a search field of its own, and Down there goes to the first card found (see The desktop app for all keys).

    On the phone, every list of Notes has Select in its bar: Unsorted, an Area, a Topic, All Notes, the search's hits and the Trash. It puts a box on each card you may change; only the box ticks, a tap on the card still switches its view and a long press opens the editor. The bar then says how many are ticked and offers File into…, Merge into…, which merges all of them into the Note picked, a ticked one included, Importance and Move to trash, which asks nothing: the announcement "Moved to the trash" at the top brings all of them back with one tap. In the Trash it offers Restore. The card's own File into… still files that Note alone. Back or Stop selecting ends the selection without changing anything.

    File into… starts with a filter: typing shows the Areas and Topics whose names hold the text. Above the Areas, "Filed into recently" lists the last three Areas or Topics this Device filed into. The Areas stand in your own order, as on the Areas list. New Topic… under an Area and New Area… at the end ask for a name, New Area… with the typed text filled in, then make the Topic or Area and file the Note into it. New Topic… is there in Advanced only, and in a Shared Area only for a Manager. On the phone, a locked Area that is not open shows no Topics, and New Topic… is not offered there.

    On the desktop the filter takes the keyboard as File into… opens. Enter files into the first Area or Topic whose name matches, or, while nothing is typed, the one filed into last; when nothing matches, it asks the name of a New Area. Esc closes File into… and files nothing. A card dragged onto the sidebar counts as filed into, too.

    Change a Note

    Change a Note's text and its settings.

  • Long-press the Note's card.
  • Change the text.
  • Tap a badge to change its setting.
  • Tap Back.
    •
  • Double-click the Note's card.
  • Change the text.
  • Click a badge to change its setting.
  • Press Esc.
    •

    A long press on a Note's card, the pencil at the bottom of its detailed view, or Edit in its ⋮, opens the Note editor. The text, the Lines of a list, the photos and recordings and the Sections fill the page. A Checklist, a Shopping List, a Tally or a Session starts with its name and its description, then its Lines, its Tally or its Session (see "A Note's kind" in Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Frogs). The Sections, each an Amend or a Note merged in, have no box of their own: each follows under a dashed tear line, in a Plain Note right under its text, in the other kinds at the end of the page. The text is saved on leaving. For a Note with a Routine or Reminder, the top of the editor shows the Occurrence that is due, such as "Due 15:00", or else the latest Missed one, with Done; a one-time Reminder that is the Note's only one also has Done and move to trash (see Routines, Reminders and the Agenda).

    The Note's settings sit under the text and its Sections, or under the Lines or the Tally, in one row of badges under This Note. Each starts with the setting's icon, the same whatever its value, then shows the value: as an icon where the setting has a few fixed values, such as the Importance, Show on the Agenda and Show ticks, and as short text otherwise, such as "Household" or "Every day · 08:00". The kind comes first and shows its name in words:

    • The kind: Plain Note, Checklist, Shopping List, Tally or Session, with ▾ after it while a tap can change it. A tap offers the kinds the Note can become, as choice chips; Tally and Session only in Advanced. A Tally's badge only shows it, and so does the badge of a Note whose text is Code.
    • Importance: Unimportant, Normal or Important. It is changed here, not in the card's ⋮; on the desktop a card's right-click offers it too.
    • Area and Topic: where the Note is filed, or Unsorted, or "Not filed" for a Note with a Routine or the like and no Area. A tap opens File into….
    • Captured: when the Note was captured; its editor also shows the place, the calendar event, the Bluetooth device and the app it was shared from, as far as they were recorded. Each of these but the time has Remove beside it, which asks once and then takes the detail off the Note on every Device; the time it was captured stays. Under its text, each Section shows its own in one small grey line, such as "12:40 · Home · Dentist", its time alone when it was captured the same day as the part before it; a tap on the line opens the same details, with the same Remove. In a Shared Area, the Note's author and the Area's Managers may remove them.
    • Each Routine and Reminder of the Note with its schedule, such as "Every day · 08:00". A tap opens its editor.
    • Frog, while the Note has an open Frog or one eaten today or yesterday: Open, Eaten today or Eaten yesterday. A tap offers Eat now while it is open and Move to trash (see Eat a frog).
    • Text format, for a Plain Note: Plain text, Formatted text or Code, as choice chips; a formatted text does not become plain again (see Formatted text).
    • Show on the Agenda of a Checklist without a Routine or Reminder, and of a Shopping List: Off, One row with the number of open Lines, or Each open Line on its own row, with radio buttons in its editor.
    • Show ticks, while the Note has a box: On or Off, with a switch in its editor.
    • Hidden from AI, outside a Shared Area: On or Off, with a switch in its editor, or "Off, hidden by its Area" while only its Area is marked (see Hide a Note from the AI).

    A tap on a badge opens its small editor, which names the setting beside the same icon, shows its values in words and changes it at once; the badge then shows the new value. A setting that does not apply has no badge, and where you may not change a setting, as on another person's Note in a Shared Area, its badge only shows it. +, first under Add, is there where you may set things up; it offers Remind me…, Frog and Session…, as the alarm clock on the card does, in Simple only Remind me…, no Frog while the Note has an open one, and no Session on a Session or a Code text. A screen reader reads each badge as its setting and value, such as "Importance: Important".

    On the phone, the Note's actions follow at the end of the page in three more groups, as in the Note dialog on the desktop: Add with "+", Voice, Photo, Gallery, Paste, Attach a file… and Add a part; Share with Send to a person, Share to an app and Export as Pack…; Tidy with Merge into…, Add to a Shopping List and Move to trash. Voice, Photo, Gallery, Paste and Attach a file… show where the phone has them, and a group shows only what you may do with the Note. The top bar keeps Undo, Redo, Find in Note, the calendar, History and Revert all changes.

    In the Note dialog on the desktop, and in a Note's own window, the same three groups follow the Note's files, Sections and Linked from, side by side where the dialog is wide enough: Add with "+", Voice, Paste, Attach a file… and Add a part; Share with Send to a person, Copy text, Save as Markdown… and Export as Pack…; Tidy with Merge into…, Add to a Shopping List and Move to trash. The desktop takes no photos; a picture comes in by Paste, Attach a file… or a drop.

    The text of each Section, an Amend or a Note merged in, is edited in place like the Note's own text, in the same field with the same format bar, and saved on leaving; in Simple the editor has no format bar. It keeps its place and the time it was captured. Its author may edit it, and in a Shared Area also the Area's Managers; for anyone else it is only read. Once edited, a Section says under its capture line when it was last edited and by whom, such as "Edited 11/1/2026 14:28 by Ann". The text before every edit stays in the log.

    On the phone, Add a part under Add at the end of the page adds a Section: it opens Add a part with "What's new?", and Add puts what was typed under the Note with its own time and place. The editor shows no field for it until then. A Note just made, in the editor opened by Open in the editor or by a list's "+", has no Add a part; it is there once the Note is opened again.

    Copy text, under Share in the ⋮ of a Note's card, puts the text of the Note and of each of its Sections on the clipboard, as they show, oldest first, with a blank line between them; a Checklist's or Shopping List's Lines come after its text as Markdown tasks. In the editor, a text that is only read, such as another person's Section, is selected with a long press; the toolbar just above it offers Copy and Find in Note.

    On the phone, Undo and Redo come first in the editor's top bar. They take back, and make again, one step at a time, what changed since the editor opened: the text, a table's cells, the Lines of a list and the badges. Typing is one step until a pause of a second or a new word. Revert all changes, on the bar after History, in ⋮ where the bar is full, asks once and then brings the Note back to how it was when it was opened; Redo brings the changes back one by one. Undo does not make a formatted text plain again, nor a Tally or a Session a Plain Note.

    On the desktop, Undo, Redo and ⋮ stand in the Note dialog's bar, before "Open in its own window" and the cross, and in a Note's own window before "Reading view". They go through the same steps; outside a text field Ctrl+Z is Undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z and Ctrl+Y are Redo (Cmd on macOS). Typing is one step until a pause of a second, a new word, leaving the field or Save; a table's row or column menu is a step of its own. In a text field Ctrl+Z still undoes only that field's typing. Revert all changes in ⋮ asks once, as on the phone.

    On the desktop, a double-click on a card, its pencil or Edit in its ⋮ opens the Note dialog; a single click only selects the card. The dialog shows the same badges and the same Done at the top. Esc, its cross or a click beside it close it and keep the text. Copy text, in the dialog and under Share in the card's ⋮, copies the Note's text, a list's Lines as Markdown tasks; text that is only read can be selected and copied.

    See what changed in a Note

    See every change of a Note and bring an older text back.

  • Long-press the Note's card.
  • Tap History, the clock with an arrow.
  • Tap Use this version under an older text.
    •
  • Double-click the Note's card.
  • Click ⋮ ▸ History at the top of the Note dialog.
  • Click Use this version under an older text.
    •

    History, the clock with an arrow in the editor's bar, lists every change of the Note, the newest first: its text before and after, its Amends added and changed, the Lines of a list added, renamed, checked, unchecked, deleted and restored, its Importance, Area and Topic, kind and text format, the trash, and photos and recordings added and removed. Each change says who made it and when, in a Shared Area by the person's Profile name. An Area or Topic in a locked Area that is not open shows as "A locked Area". Use this version under an older text of the Note, or of an Amend you may edit, brings that text back as a new change; nothing is deleted, and the History keeps every version.

    On the desktop, History in the ⋮ at the top of the Note dialog opens the same list as a dialog for the Note open in it.
