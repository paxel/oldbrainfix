Capturing a Note takes one tap and never asks anything, not even for the App Lock. Sorting can happen while capturing or later; until a Note is sorted it is Unsorted, and the Nudge reminds you of it. Each box below shows the steps on the phone and on the desktop.

The desktop app captures in the quick note, a dialog over its window, and in the capture popup that its hotkey opens over all other windows.

Capture a thought

Write, say or photograph a thought before it is gone.

  • Tap + on the Agenda.
  • Tap Text, Voice, Photo, Gallery or Paste.
  • Type, speak or take the photo.
  • After speaking, tap Stop.
  • Tap 10 min, 30 min or 1 h.
    •
  • Press Ctrl+N, or click File ▸ New Note.
  • Type, or click Record voice, Paste or From file….
  • After speaking, click Stop.
  • Click 10 min, 30 min or 1 h.
    •

    The Agenda's "+" fans out the ways to capture, upward from the button, each an icon with its word: Text opens the capture screen with the keyboard up, Voice starts recording at once, Photo opens the camera, Gallery opens the system's picture picker, where several pictures can be picked at once, and Paste opens the capture screen filled from the clipboard. Voice, Photo, Gallery and Paste show only where the Device has them. While the fan is open, "+" reads as Close; tapping it again, tapping outside or Back closes the fan.

    The capture screen, the quick note, holds the text field ("What's on your mind?"), the buttons Photo, Voice, Gallery and Paste, To full editor, and at the bottom "Remind me to sort it in" with 10 min, 30 min and 1 h. Each of the three saves the Note and sets its Nudge to that time. Nothing on the screen sorts the Note unless it is switched on in Settings under "Quick note" (see Sort while capturing).

    • Voice: the elapsed time runs above one big Stop button. There is no holding a button and no stop on silence. After Stop you are back on the capture screen with the recording and can type or add more. The first time, the app explains why it needs the microphone and asks for it.
    • Photo and Gallery: the photos show above the text field, and the text becomes optional ("A few words about it (optional)").
    • Paste: the clipboard is read only on this tap. Its text goes into the field at the cursor, its picture into the Note as a photo. When it holds neither, a line above the field says so.
    • To full editor saves the Note at once, Unsorted with a Nudge in 1 h, and opens it in the Note editor with everything the editor offers. Filing it there drops the Nudge, as filing always does. Leaving the editor goes back to where the capture was opened from. The button waits until something is typed, recorded or picked, and behind the App Lock it is not offered, as the editor shows the Areas.

    Save in the top bar, Back and the app going to the background save as well; the Nudge is then 1 h. An empty Note is not saved. After saving, the new Note's card shows on the Agenda under "Just captured", in its detailed view with the same options, for what was not done while capturing, merging into another Note among them; a tap switches it to its compact view and back.

    On the desktop, File ▸ New Note, Ctrl+N (Cmd+N on macOS) and "New Note", the "+" at the top of the Agenda and of Unsorted, open the quick note as the dialog "New Note" over the window, its field reading "What's on your mind?". In All Notes, an Area or a Topic, the "+" is "Add here"; its Note opens the quick note. The dialog's cross, Esc, Alt+Left and a click beside it close it and keep what is typed with a Nudge in 1 h. Enter starts a new line; Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on macOS) saves the Note with a Nudge in 1 h, or without one once the Note is sorted. Once something is typed or recorded, To full editor and "Remind me to sort it in" with 10 min, 30 min and 1 h appear. Like the phone's quick note, it shows nothing that sorts the Note unless it is switched on in Settings under "Quick note".

    • To full editor saves the Note at once, Unsorted with a Nudge in 1 h, and opens it in the Note dialog in place of the quick note, its text ready for typing. The list stays where it was.
    • With an Area or Topic shown in the list, the Note is filed there and needs no Nudge: one Save takes the place of the three Nudge buttons, and To full editor files it there too.
    • Record voice starts recording at once; Stop ends it, the recording waits under the field as a "Voice recording" line and is saved with the Note.
    • Paste, or Ctrl+V in the empty field, puts the clipboard's text into the field. A pasted picture waits under the field as a "Photo" line and is saved with what is typed as the Note's photo.
    • From file… picks pictures and sound files; files dropped onto the window open the quick note. They wait under the field, a picture as a "Photo" line and a sound file as a "Voice recording" line, and are saved with what is typed as one Note.

    Text still typed, and pictures and files still waiting, when the window closes are kept as a Note with a Nudge in 1 h.

    Sort while capturing

    File the Note, or set it up, before it is saved.

  • Tap your Profile picture, then Settings.
  • Switch on File into… under "Quick note".
  • Tap + ▸ Text and type.
  • Tap the folder, File into…, and pick an Area.
  • Tap Save.
    •
  • Click Edit ▸ Settings….
  • Tick File into… under "Quick note".
  • Press Ctrl+N and type.
  • Click File into… and pick an Area.
  • Click Save.
    •

    The quick note is meant for getting a thought out of the head; sorting happens later on the card or in the editor. Settings, under "Quick note", has one switch each for what the quick note shows besides, all off unless switched on; they belong to this phone. On the phone, Settings is the row under "Settings of this Device" on the Profile page, behind the Profile picture in the Agenda's top corner.

    Frog, Session, Make it a Tally and the format bar are there in Advanced only; in Simple the alarm clock offers Routine and Reminder, and the format bar's switch does not show.

    • File into… (the folder) picks the Area or Topic.
    • Routine, Reminder, Frog, Session or Tally: the alarm clock offers the first four, and ⋮ offers Make it a Tally. Routine, Reminder and Session open their usual editor, Make it a Tally the page for the Tally's first field; the Frog needs nothing more.
    • Add to a Shopping List, in ⋮.
    • Importance, in ⋮.
    • Format bar: the format bar sits on the keyboard, as in the Note editor but without ☐ (see Formatted text). On a small screen the buttons under the text then scroll, and the text keeps two lines.
    • Nudge buttons, on unless switched off: off, a single Save takes the place of the three buttons and saves with a Nudge in 1 h, as leaving does.

    What is switched on shows beside To full editor, above the Nudge buttons. Each choice comes back to the capture screen and shows at the top, such as "→ Household", most of them with a button that drops it again. Nothing of it is saved before the Note. Once the Note has an Area or Topic, or a Routine, Reminder, Frog, Session or Tally, it needs no Nudge: the three buttons become one Save. A capture opened inside an Area goes into that Area and shows it at the top.

    Behind the App Lock the row offers neither File into… nor the Session, which would show the Areas and the Tallies. In a Shared Area where you are a Member, only a Manager sets up Routines, Reminders and Frogs, so the alarm clock is not offered there.

    Add to a Shopping List makes each line of the capture a Line of the Shopping List you pick, and keeps no Note. While a list is picked, the other choices and To full editor wait unshown, and one Save takes the place of the Nudge buttons. It is offered only while there is a list to add to and the capture holds no photo; a photo taken or pasted afterwards drops it, and it is not offered behind the App Lock. A recording gives the lines of its Transcript, so with a recording it is offered only where the speech model is in place. Until the Transcript is there the Note waits Unsorted, then goes into the list; without a Transcript it stays a Note, and a line at the top says so.

    On the desktop, Settings, under "Quick note", has the same switches for the quick note in the window and the popup, all off unless switched on; they belong to this computer:

    • File into… picks the Area or Topic.
    • Routine, Reminder, Frog, Session or Tally shows Routine, Reminder, Frog and Session, and Make it a Tally in More; in Simple only Routine and Reminder.
    • Add to a Shopping List, in More. With a list picked, the lines go into that list and no Note is made; only More stays in the row, To full editor goes, and one Save takes the place of the Nudge buttons.
    • Importance, in More.
    • Format bar: the format bar over the field, without ☐, link and table, which the Note editor adds. In Simple the switch does not show and the bar stays off.
    • Nudge buttons, on unless switched off: off, a single Save takes the place of the three buttons and saves with a Nudge in 1 h.

    What is switched on shows beside To full editor. A place picked with File into… shows at the top, such as "Into: Household", with a button that removes it. A Note with an Area or Topic, or with a Routine, Reminder, Frog, Session or Tally set up, gets one Save instead of the three Nudge buttons.

    Capture without opening the app

    Start a Note from the home screen, or from anywhere on the computer.

  • Long-press the Old Brain Fix icon.
  • Tap Voice, Text or Photo.
  • Type, speak or take the photo.
  • After speaking, tap Stop.
  • Tap 10 min, 30 min or 1 h.
    •
  • Press Ctrl+Alt+N anywhere.
  • Type what's on your mind.
  • Press Enter.
    •

    On Android the quick entry points open their capture screen directly, without the fan and without a screen in between: Text with the keyboard up, Voice recording, Photo with the camera open.

    • Widgets: "Voice note", "Text note" and "Photo note", one button each, and "Voice, text, photo" with the three side by side. The "Agenda" widget shows what the Agenda shows for today, its boxes to check off, and the Unsorted count, which opens the Unsorted Notes (see Routines, Reminders and the Agenda).
    • Quick Settings tiles: "Voice note", "Text note" and "Photo note". Add them by editing the tiles in the notification shade. On the phone's lock screen, Android asks to unlock first.
    • App shortcuts: long-press the Old Brain Fix icon for Voice, Text and Photo.
    • Hardware button: the app does not read keys itself. A phone's own key or gesture setting, or a button-mapper app, can start the shortcuts "Voice note", "Text note" or "Photo note". Where the setting sits on many phones is described in Capture with a hardware button.
    • Sharing: text, pictures and sound files shared from another app open the capture screen filled in, sound files as voice recordings. The app they came from is recorded with the Note.

    While the App Lock is on, its lock screen has its own "+" with the same fan, and every quick entry point opens Capture without unlocking.

    On the desktop, the hotkey, Ctrl+Alt+N unless changed, opens the popup "Capture a Note" over all other windows; File ▸ Quick note opens it too. It works while Old Brain Fix runs; where there is a tray, the app keeps running there after its window is closed. The popup holds a one-line field, Record voice, Paste, To full editor, what Settings under "Quick note" switched on, and the Nudge buttons. Enter saves the Note with a Nudge in 1 h, or without one once the Note is sorted; Esc and the close button keep what is typed the same way, and an empty popup just closes. To full editor saves the Note the same way, closes the popup and opens the Note in the window's Note editor, bringing the window back from the tray; it and the Nudge buttons wait until something is typed or recorded.

    The key is set in Settings under "Quick capture", in the field "Hotkey"; it needs Ctrl, Alt or Super. On X11, Windows and macOS the app takes the key itself. On Wayland the desktop manages it: it asks once and lets you change the key in its own keyboard settings. Where the key is taken or no hotkey is possible, the line under the field says so. The tray's menu also has Capture a Note.

    The command line:

    • oldbrainfix capture opens the popup.
    • oldbrainfix capture buy milk keeps "buy milk" at once as an Unsorted Note with a Nudge in 1 h, without a window.
    • oldbrainfix capture - keeps what comes in on standard input.

    Launchers, key-binding tools and macro pads can run these commands, also where the desktop offers no hotkey.

    Sort what is Unsorted

    File the Notes you captured without sorting them.

  • Tap the Nudge, or x Unsorted on the Agenda.
  • Tap the folder, File into…, on a Note's card.
  • Pick the Area or Topic.
    •
  • Click the Nudge, or Unsorted in the sidebar.
  • Click ⋮ ▸ File into… on a Note's card.
  • Pick the Area or Topic.
    •

    While Notes are Unsorted, the Agenda shows "x Unsorted" on top, which opens them. The Nudge is one notification for all of them, such as "3 Notes are Unsorted", first at the earliest time picked while capturing. It offers 10 min, 30 min and 1 h to snooze; tapping it opens "Waiting to be sorted" with the three oldest Unsorted Notes and "+x" for all of them.

    An ignored Nudge comes again until no Note is left Unsorted, after the time set in Settings under "Nudge again after" (1 h unless changed). In the Quiet Hours, 22:00 to 08:00 unless changed, Nudges wait until they end; Routines and Reminders still ring on time, only the first ring of an all-day one waits too (see Routines, Reminders and the Agenda).

    On the desktop the Nudge shows as a notification, such as "3 Notes are Unsorted", "Click to sort them.". On Linux and Windows it has the buttons 10 min, 30 min and 1 h to snooze, and "Open Old Brain Fix"; on macOS it shows without buttons. Clicking it opens the window on Unsorted. The switch "Notifications on this computer" in Settings turns notifications off. The Agenda shows "x Unsorted" as well, which opens Unsorted.

    On the desktop, File into… is also in a card's right-click menu and under Note ▸ File into… (Ctrl+M), and the badge "Area and Topic" in the Note dialog opens it. A card can be dragged onto an Area or Topic in the sidebar. Ctrl-click and Shift-click select several cards; File into… then files all of them.

    Change a Note

    Change a Note's text and its settings.

  • Long-press the Note's card.
  • Change the text.
  • Tap a badge to change its setting.
  • Tap Back.
    •
  • Double-click the Note's card.
  • Change the text.
  • Click a badge to change its setting.
  • Press Esc.
    •

    A long press on a Note's card, or Edit in its ⋮, opens the Note editor. The text, the Lines of a list, the photos and recordings and the Sections fill the page. A Checklist, a Shopping List, a Tally or a Session starts with its name and its description, then its Lines, its Tally or its Session (see "A Note's kind" in Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Frogs). The text is saved on leaving. For a Note with a Routine or Reminder, the top of the editor shows the Occurrence that is due, such as "Due 15:00", or else the latest Missed one, with Done; a one-time Reminder that is the Note's only one also has Done and move to trash (see Routines, Reminders and the Agenda).

    The Note's settings sit under the text, or under the Lines or the Tally, in one row of badges. Each starts with the setting's icon, the same whatever its value, then shows the value: as an icon where the setting has a few fixed values, such as the kind, the Importance, Show on the Agenda and Show ticks, and as short text otherwise, such as "Household" or "Every day · 08:00":

    • Importance: Unimportant, Normal or Important. It is changed here, not in the card's ⋮.
    • Area and Topic: where the Note is filed, or Unsorted, or "Not filed" for a Note with a Routine or the like and no Area. A tap opens File into….
    • Captured: when the Note was captured; its editor also shows the place, the calendar event, the Bluetooth device and the app it was shared from, as far as they were recorded. Each of these but the time has Remove beside it, which asks once and then takes the detail off the Note on every Device; the time it was captured stays. Under its text, each Section shows its own in one small grey line, such as "12:40 · Home · Dentist", its time alone when it was captured the same day as the part before it; a tap on the line opens the same details, with the same Remove. In a Shared Area, the Note's author and the Area's Managers may remove them.
    • Each Routine and Reminder of the Note with its schedule, such as "Every day · 08:00". A tap opens its editor.
    • The kind: Plain Note, Checklist, Shopping List, Tally or Session. A tap offers the kinds the Note can become, with radio buttons; Tally and Session only in Advanced. A Tally's badge only shows it, and so does the badge of a Note whose text is Code.
    • Text format, for a Plain Note: Plain text, Formatted text or Code, with radio buttons; a formatted text does not become plain again (see Formatted text).
    • Show on the Agenda of a Checklist without a Routine or Reminder, and of a Shopping List: Off, One row with the number of open Lines, or Each open Line on its own row, with radio buttons in its editor.
    • Show ticks, while the Note has a box: On or Off, with a switch in its editor.

    A tap on a badge opens its small editor, which names the setting beside the same icon, shows its values in words and changes it at once; the badge then shows the new value. A setting that does not apply has no badge, and where you may not change a setting, as on another person's Note in a Shared Area, its badge only shows it. The row ends with + where you may set things up; it offers Routine, Reminder, Frog and Session, as the alarm clock on the card does, in Simple only Routine and Reminder, and no Session on a Session or a Code text. A screen reader reads each badge as its setting and value, such as "Importance: Important".

    The text of each Section, an Amend or a Note merged in, is edited in place like the Note's own text, in the same field with the same format bar, and saved on leaving; in Simple the editor has no format bar. It keeps its place and the time it was captured. Its author may edit it, and in a Shared Area also the Area's Managers; for anyone else it is only read. Once edited, a Section says under its capture line when it was last edited and by whom, such as "Edited 11/1/2026 14:28 by Ann". The text before every edit stays in the log.

    Copy text in the ⋮ of a Note's card puts the text of the Note and of each of its Sections on the clipboard, as they show, oldest first, with a blank line between them. In the editor, a text that is only read, such as another person's Section, is selected with a long press; the menu beside it offers Copy and Select all.

    On the desktop, a double-click on a card, its pencil or Edit in its ⋮ opens the Note dialog; a single click only selects the card. The dialog shows the same badges and the same Done at the top. Esc, its cross or a click beside it close it and keep the text. Copy as text, in the dialog, the card's ⋮ and the Note menu, copies the Note's text; text that is only read can be selected and copied.

    See what changed in a Note

    See every change of a Note and bring an older text back.

  • Long-press the Note's card.
  • Tap History, the clock with an arrow.
  • Tap Use this version under an older text.
    •
  • Double-click the Note's card.
  • Click Note ▸ History.
  • Click Use this version under an older text.
    •

    History, the clock with an arrow in the editor's bar, lists every change of the Note, the newest first: its text before and after, its Amends added and changed, the Lines of a list added, renamed, checked, unchecked, deleted and restored, its Importance, Area and Topic, kind and text format, the trash, and photos and recordings added and removed. Each change says who made it and when, in a Shared Area by the person's Profile name. An Area or Topic in a locked Area that is not open shows as "A locked Area". Use this version under an older text of the Note, or of an Amend you may edit, brings that text back as a new change; nothing is deleted, and the History keeps every version.

    On the desktop, Note ▸ History opens the same list as a dialog for the Note open in the Note dialog.
