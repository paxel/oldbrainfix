Old Brain Fix has no connection of its own to calendar apps such as FamilyWall: it signs in to no account of theirs and talks to no server of theirs. It reaches them through the phone's calendar and through calendar files. FamilyWall serves as the example; another calendar app that offers an iCal link or imports calendar files works the same way. Each box below shows the steps on the phone and on the desktop.

The desktop app has no Calendar page and shows no calendar appointments; it only saves calendar files.

See your family calendar on the Agenda

Show the appointments of FamilyWall's calendar among your Routines on the Agenda.

  • Generate the iCal link in FamilyWall's Calendar settings and copy it.
  • Add it in Google Calendar on a computer: From URL.
  • Tap Calendar on the Agenda.
  • Switch on Show my calendar's appointments on the Agenda.
  • Tick the FamilyWall calendar.
    •
  • Use the phone: the desktop shows no calendar appointments.
    •

    In the FamilyWall app, tap Calendar, then the settings icon at the top right, then the calendar with your Circle's name. Where it has no link yet, Generate iCal URL makes one; a tap copies it. The FamilyWall website has the same in the Calendars panel. The link carries one calendar in one direction: FamilyWall's events show elsewhere, and changes made there do not go back to FamilyWall.

    Google Calendar subscribes to a link only in a computer's web browser, not in its Android app: next to "Other calendars", click Add other calendars, then From URL, paste the link and click Add calendar. The calendar then shows under "Other calendars" and reaches the phone with the Google account. Where it does not show on the phone:

    • In the Google Calendar app, open Menu ▸ Settings, pick the calendar and make sure "Sync" is on.
    • In the phone's Settings, open Passwords, passkeys & accounts ▸ your Google Account ▸ Account sync and make sure "Calendar" is on.

    Old Brain Fix reads the calendars the phone keeps, the same ones its calendar apps show, and offers each of them on "Calendars on the Agenda". The appointments show among the Occurrences, read only, and nothing of them is kept or synced (see Use your calendar).

    Put your Routines into the family calendar

    Copy your Routines and Reminders into FamilyWall's calendar as a calendar file.

  • Tap All Routines and Reminders on the Agenda.
  • Tap Export and save the calendar file.
  • Open familywall.com in the browser and log in.
  • Tap Calendar, then Import event from ICS.
  • Pick the calendar file.
    •
  • Click All Routines and Reminders on the Agenda.
  • Click Export and choose where to save the file.
  • Open familywall.com in the browser and log in.
  • Click Calendar, then Import event from ICS.
  • Pick the calendar file.
    •

    FamilyWall imports calendar files on its website, not in its app: in Calendar in the left menu, open the calendars on the right and scroll to "Add other Calendars", where Import event from ICS takes the file. Without FamilyWall Premium, only the first 30 events of the file are imported.

    An import copies the events once. A Routine changed in Old Brain Fix afterwards stays as it was in FamilyWall until it is imported again; FamilyWall's help does not say whether a second import replaces the first one.

    Export puts all Routines and Reminders into one file, those of a closed locked Area left out. For a single one, Share in its row's ⋮ on the Agenda gives its own file; on the desktop it is in the row's right-click menu or ⋮.

    On the phone, Write Routines and Reminders into a calendar on the Calendar page writes them into a calendar of the phone instead, and follows every change there. FamilyWall lists importing and syncing Google and Outlook calendars, and subscribing to a calendar by its link, as Premium features.
