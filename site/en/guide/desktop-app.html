The desktop app runs on Linux, Windows and macOS and holds the same Notes, Areas and Routines as the phone once the two are paired. It is a window with a menu bar, the places on the left and the list beside them, a Note opening as a dialog over them, worked with the mouse and the keyboard, with a capture popup and a command line for capturing from anywhere. Settings belong to this computer and are never synced.

Install and first start

The app is installed with Homebrew, Scoop, a .deb, a Flatpak file, an AppImage, a disk image or a zip file; see Install. On Linux, outside the Flatpak, every start writes or refreshes the app's entry in the application menu and its icon.

The first start offers the same three choices as the phone: New to Old Brain Fix with the Starter Set, Add a Device of mine and Restore from a Backup (see Getting started). The Profile takes the name and picture of your login on the computer, or else the computer's name. The desktop asks neither for the speech model nor for alarm access.

The window

The window has the menu bar on top and two columns below it; the border between them can be dragged.

  • Left, the sidebar: Agenda, Unsorted, All Notes and Frogs, then "Areas" with a "+" for a new Area, each Area with its Topics below it, and the Trash. The place the list shows is marked. A right-click on an Area opens New Topic, Rename, Colour and icon, Share…, People when it is shared, and Delete, as far as you manage the Area; a right-click on a Topic opens Rename and Delete. These menus close once an entry is picked, with the mouse, the keyboard or a screen reader.
  • Middle, the list: on top the back and forward arrows and the path to the place shown, such as "Areas › Household › Repairs", where a click on a level shows it; "Areas" leads to All Notes. Below them what is selected on the left. All Notes, an Area and a Topic have a search field; every list of Notes except the Trash has a switch between the compact and the detailed view of its cards and a filter by person. The heading ends with a "+" (see below).

The "+" of All Notes, an Area and a Topic opens a menu: Note opens the quick note, Checklist, Shopping List and Frog ask for a name, Tally and Session open their page, and in an Area Topic adds a Topic. What it makes is filed into the Area or Topic shown, from All Notes into none; Session, Frog and Topic are there for who may set them up. A new Checklist or Shopping List opens in the Note dialog, the cursor in its first Line or its field at the top (see Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Frogs). The "+" of the Agenda and of Unsorted opens the quick note at once. Nothing is made while the list shows another person's Notes only. Every list ends with room after its last card, so that card scrolls fully into view.

In a list of Notes, a click selects a card; its view button, at its top right beside ⋮ and the pencil, switches it between its compact and its detailed view (see "A Note's kind" in Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Frogs), kept on this computer until the list's switch is used again; a double-click or the pencil opens the Note in the Note dialog. A right-click on a card or its ⋮ opens Edit, File into…, the Checklist entries with Show on the Agenda, the Shopping List entries with Show on the Agenda and its three choices (see Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Frogs), Send to a person, Copy as text and Save as Markdown…. A card dragged onto an Area or Topic in the sidebar is filed there, an Unsorted one leaving Unsorted; Notes in the Trash are not dragged.

Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on macOS) adds a card to the selection or takes it away again, and Shift-click selects every card from the one clicked last to this one. Selected cards carry a check mark and a thicker outline. Dragging one of them onto an Area or Topic, File into… in its right-click menu, Note ▸ File into… and Ctrl+M file them all together; the dialog says how many go. A card outside the selection is dragged alone. Esc and showing another place drop the selection. In a Shared Area a Member's selection may hold Notes other people wrote, which only a Manager moves: they stay where they are, and a line at the top says that not every Note was filed; its click shows how many moved and how many stayed.

A click on a photo in the Note dialog shows it full screen. A right-click on a photo in the Note dialog or full screen opens Save image as…, which saves its file where you say, Copy image, which puts the picture on the clipboard, and Open in another program, which opens it in the computer's program for pictures. Saved and copied say nothing; when one of them fails, a line at the top of the window says so, and a click on it shows why and what to do.

The Agenda's bar holds All Routines and Reminders and New Routine or Reminder.

The Note dialog

A Note opens as a dialog over the list and the sidebar, one at a time; the menu bar stays at hand above it. It holds the text with the format bar, under it the Note's settings as badges, then its Lines, photos and recordings, its Tally's setup and its Sections, Add to Note and the rest of what can be done to it. Its cross, Esc, Alt+Left, the mouse's back button and a click beside it close it, and what was typed is kept, a Section typed into Add to Note too.

Each badge shows a setting's icon and its value, as an icon or as short text, as on the phone (see Capture and sort): the Importance, Area and Topic, Captured, each Routine and Reminder, the kind of a Checklist, Shopping List or Tally, Show on the Agenda for a Checklist without a Routine and for a Shopping List, Show ticks while there is a box, and "+" with Routine, Reminder, Frog and Session for who may set them up. A click opens its small editor as a dialog over the Note; Area and Topic opens File into…, a Routine or Reminder its editor. The Routine editor, a Tally's pages, the New Tally page and the New Session page open as dialogs over what they were opened from. Captured's dialog shows Remove beside each captured detail but the time, and a click on the small grey line under a Section's text, such as "12:40 · Home · Dentist", opens the same dialog for that Section; Remove asks once, and the dialog stays open with what is left. In the detailed view of a card the Note and each Section show that line under their text, a Section's with its time alone when it was captured the same day as the part before it.

The text of a Section, an Amend or a Note merged in, is edited in place in a field of its own with the same format bar as the Note's text, by its author and in a Shared Area also by the Area's Managers, and saved as the dialog closes or the field is left; it keeps its place and its capture time, and once edited says under its capture line when and by whom, such as "Edited 3/2/2026 11:00 by Ann". A text that is only read, such as another person's Section or a Note's in the Trash, is selected with the mouse and copied with Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on macOS). Copy as text copies the Note and all its Sections, as they show.

The menu bar

  • File: New Note (Ctrl+N) opens the quick note as a dialog over the window, the cursor in its field, filed into the Area or Topic the list shows (see Capture and sort). Quick note opens the capture popup, as the hotkey does. Import… opens the Import dialog with A folder… and Google Keep… (see Import notes from another app). Save as Markdown… saves the Note in the Note dialog. Quit ends the app, also where closing the window leaves it in the tray.
  • Edit: Cut, Copy and Paste for the text field the cursor was in. Find (Ctrl+F) puts the cursor into the search field, All Notes' where the list has none. Settings… (Ctrl+,) opens this computer's Settings.
  • View: Agenda, Unsorted, All Notes, Frogs and All Routines and Reminders; Detailed views and Compact views for the cards of the list shown; Theme with System, which shows the window light or dark as the computer is set and changes with it, Light and Dark; the choice is kept on this computer.
  • Note, for the Note in the Note dialog: File into… (Ctrl+M), which asks for the Area or Topic and, while no Note is open, files the cards selected in the list, Routine…, Reminder…, Frog, Session, Make it a Checklist, Make it a Shopping List, Make it a Tally, Send to a person, Copy as text and Delete. They are greyed while no Note is open, but File into… while cards are selected, and each one where the Note dialog does not offer it either.
  • Sync: Sync now, Pair a Device, People, Profile and Backups…, each a dialog (see below).
  • Help: User guide opens this page in the browser; About Old Brain Fix shows the version, the documentation and privacy policy, the source code and every third-party component with its licence.

A menu closes once an entry is picked, with the mouse, the keyboard or a screen reader. On macOS the menu bar is part of the window, as on Linux and Windows, and Settings… stays in its Edit menu.

Dialogs

The Settings, About and everything under Sync open as dialogs over the window; the list stays where it was beneath them. The cross at the top right, Esc or a click beside the dialog closes it. A dialog opened from another, such as About from the Settings and the licences from About, has an arrow at the top left that leads back to that one. While a dialog is open, a line that announces something shows at its top.

  • Sync now opens Sync: this computer's code while it is open, the Conflicts, the shared folder, which it syncs at once when one is chosen, the bundles and the paired Devices (see Sync with the phone).
  • Pair a Device: the question about a Device asking to sync, this computer's code, and the field for the code another Device shows.
  • People: every Profile kept here, the blocked ones in a fold of their own, and Add a person from a link. A click on a person opens their Profile over it.
  • Profile: your picture, name and short code, with Send my Profile and Show my QR.
  • Backups…: the Backups and the Recovery Sheet (see Backups, locks and privacy).

Keys instead of swipes

Where the phone goes back with a swipe, the desktop uses keys and the mouse's side buttons. Alt+Left and Alt+Right rest while the cursor is in a text field; the mouse buttons always work.

  • Alt+Left, the mouse's back button or the back arrow above the list: the place the list showed before, as a browser goes back. In a dialog opened from another it leads back to that one, and otherwise closes the dialog, the Note dialog and the quick note keeping what was typed; a photo or table shown full screen closes.
  • Alt+Right, the mouse's forward button or the forward arrow: the place back came from. Showing another place forgets what lay ahead.
  • Ctrl+N, Ctrl+F, Ctrl+M and Ctrl+, (Cmd on macOS): New Note, Find, File into… and Settings…, as in the menu bar; also while the cursor is in a text field and over the Note dialog, not while another dialog is open.
  • Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on macOS): saves the quick note's Note and saves the text being edited in the Note dialog. Enter alone starts a new line.
  • In a Line of a Checklist, Enter splits the Line at the cursor, Backspace at its start joins it with the Line above, Tab and Shift+Tab put it under the Line above and back at the top, and Up and Down move to the Line above and below. In a Shopping List's field Enter adds the Line, and in one of its Lines it ends the rename (see Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Frogs).
  • Ctrl+B and Ctrl+I (Cmd on macOS): bold and italic while the Note's text is edited.
  • Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on macOS) in the quick note's empty field: takes the clipboard's text into the field and its picture into the Note as its photo.
  • Enter in a one-line field of a dialog, such as a name or a link's address, takes it.
  • Esc closes a dialog, as does a click beside it, and closes a photo or table shown full screen. In a table cell, Tab and Shift+Tab move to the next and the previous cell (see Formatted text). The Note dialog and the quick note keep what was typed when they close.

The tray and starting at login

The app shows an icon in the tray: in the notification area on Windows, in the menu bar on macOS, and on Linux where the desktop has a tray: KDE, Xfce, Cinnamon, and GNOME with the AppIndicator extension. A click on the icon opens the window, on macOS its menu; the menu, on Windows and Linux under the right mouse button, holds Open Old Brain Fix, Capture a Note and Quit. Closing the window leaves the app running in the tray, with its notifications, its hotkey, the sync with paired Devices and the weekly Backup; Quit ends it.

Linux without a tray has no icon. There, closing the window ends the app, and with it the notifications and the hotkey, until it is started again.

Settings, "This computer", Start at login is on unless switched off. It writes an autostart entry on Linux (inside the Flatpak through the desktop's Background portal), a LaunchAgent on macOS and a Run entry for your user on Windows. The login start goes into the tray without a window; on Linux without a tray it opens the window.

The app runs once per user. A second start, from the menu or a command, hands its wish to the running app: its window opens, or its capture popup.

The capture popup and its hotkey

The hotkey, Ctrl+Alt+N unless changed, opens the popup "Capture a Note" over all other windows, also while the app runs in the tray without its window. The tray's Capture a Note and the command oldbrainfix capture open it too. It takes text, a voice recording and the clipboard, offers To full editor and what Settings under "Quick note" switched on, as the quick note in the window does, and never asks anything: Enter saves the Note with a Nudge in 1 h, and Esc keeps what is typed the same way (see Capture and sort).

  • The hotkey is set in Settings under "Quick capture", in the field "Hotkey": Ctrl, Alt or Super, maybe Shift, and a letter, a digit, F1 to F12 or Space, such as Super+Shift+F5.
  • On X11, Windows and macOS the app takes the key itself; on Wayland the desktop manages it, asks once and lets you change it in its own keyboard settings. The line under the field says when the key is taken or no hotkey is possible.
  • The key works while the app runs, also from the tray.
  • On Wayland the desktop decides whether the popup stays above other windows and where the keyboard goes once it closes.

The command line

  • oldbrainfix opens the window.
  • oldbrainfix capture opens the capture popup.
  • oldbrainfix capture buy milk keeps "buy milk" as an Unsorted Note with a Nudge in 1 h, without a window. After -- every word is text, also one that starts with "-".
  • oldbrainfix capture - keeps what comes in on standard input.
  • oldbrainfix import FILE imports a sync bundle and prints what came in.
  • oldbrainfix open FILE shows a Sent Item in the window, to accept or decline; a Profile card file adds the person and opens their Profile.
  • oldbrainfix --help prints the usage.

A capture or an import goes through the running app, which plans the Nudge at once; when none runs, the command writes the Note itself, and the Nudge comes once the app runs. A capture that worked prints nothing. The exit code is 0 when it worked, 1 when the Note could not be kept or the bundle imported, and 2 for wrong arguments or nothing to capture. Launchers, key-binding tools and macro pads can run these commands, also where the desktop offers no hotkey.

Drag and drop

  • Pictures and sound files dropped onto the window open the quick note and wait in it, a picture as a "Photo" line and a sound file as a "Voice recording" line, and are saved with what is typed as one Note. Sound files are Ogg, Opus, M4A, MP3, WAV and AAC. A recording that cannot be transcribed is kept all the same, and a line at the top of the window says so once the Note is saved. Other files are left alone.
  • A sync bundle (.obfsync) dropped onto the window is imported; a Sent Item (.oldbrain) is shown to accept or decline; a Profile card file (.obfcard) adds the person and opens their Profile.
  • A Note's card dragged onto an Area or Topic in the sidebar is filed there, with the other cards selected when it is one of them.

Notifications

The alarms of Routines and Reminders, the Nudge, the Changes Nudge and "Missed while Old Brain Fix was not running" come as the system's notifications, for as long as the app runs. The switch "Notifications on this computer" in Settings turns them off. A click on a notification opens the window: on Unsorted for the Nudge; for the Changes Nudge, a save dialog for each person waiting.

  • Linux: an alarm offers Done, 10 min, 30 min, 1 h and "Open Old Brain Fix"; the Nudge offers 10 min, 30 min, 1 h and "Open Old Brain Fix"; the Changes Nudge offers Send. A notification shown again replaces the one before, and an alarm's notification closes once its Occurrence is Done, also on another Device.
  • Windows: the same buttons. Each notification is shown anew, and the app does not close it once its Occurrence is Done.
  • macOS: no buttons; a click on the notification opens the window.

The app plays the alarm sound itself, and an escalated alarm turns the notification urgent; there is no full-screen alarm (see Routines, Reminders and the Agenda).

The speech model and Photo Texts

Transcripts need the speech model, chosen and downloaded under Settings, Speech model, or taken from a file of your own, as on the phone. Recordings without a Transcript, also those from the phone that were not transcribed there, follow once it is in place. Photo Texts need no download: the text recognition is part of the app. Both run on this computer, one recording or photo at a time, while the app runs (see Formatted text, Transcripts and Photo Texts).

Sync with the phone

Sync now in the Sync menu opens Sync. It shows this computer's code, as a QR code and as text with its PIN, for the phone to scan, for as long as it is open; a phone that is not paired yet is asked about first, Trust or Refuse. When a shared folder is chosen, Sync now also syncs through it at once. Pair a Device shows the same code and the field Pair code, where a code the phone shows is typed or pasted and joined with Sync with this code. Paired Devices on the same network then sync on their own for as long as the app runs, also in the tray; the shared folder syncs every half minute. See Sharing and sync for pairing, the shared folder, bundles and Conflicts.

Import… in the File menu opens the Import dialog: A folder… makes Notes of a folder of Markdown or text files, and Google Keep… of a Google Takeout export of Keep (see Import notes from another app).

The AI Task

Only the desktop has the AI port. Settings, Open the AI port lets an AI model of your choice work on your Notes for one task, reachable only from this computer with its token. Sync waits while the port is open and until the review. Done, review closes the port and lists every change with its before and after, to accept or reject as a whole (see The AI Task).

What the desktop does not have

  • The App Lock and Area Locks.
  • The Calendar page and appointments from the calendar on the Agenda.
  • The full-screen alarm.
  • The Google backup.
  • Photos from a camera: photos come from a file, the clipboard or a drop.
  • A full Capture Context: a Note captured on the desktop keeps only its time, without the place, the source app, the calendar event or the Bluetooth device, and there are no Named Places to manage. The Capture Context of a Note from the phone shows in full.
