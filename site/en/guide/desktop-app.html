The desktop app runs on Linux, Windows and macOS and holds the same Notes, Areas and Routines as the phone once the two are paired. It is a window with a menu bar, the places on the left and the list beside them, a Note opening as a dialog over them, worked with the mouse and the keyboard, with a capture popup and a command line for capturing from anywhere. Each box below shows the steps on the desktop and, where the phone has the same, on the phone.

Most settings belong to this computer and are not synced. Simple or Advanced and "Move a Note to the Trash when its one-time Reminder is done" are settings of you as a person: they sync to your own Devices.

Install the desktop app

Put Old Brain Fix on a computer and start it for the first time.

  • Install Old Brain Fix as the Install page says.
  • Open it.
  • Pick one of the three choices on the first start.
    •
  • Install the app as the Install page says.
  • Start Old Brain Fix.
  • Pick one of the choices on the first start.
    •

    The app is installed with Homebrew, Scoop, a .deb, a Flatpak file, an AppImage, a disk image or a zip file; see Install. On Linux, outside the Flatpak, every start writes or refreshes the app's entry in the application menu and its icon.

    An update replaces the running app on the next start: when an older version still runs in the tray, the new one ends it and runs in its place, with the window, the popup, the capture or the import it was started for. If the older one does not end within a few seconds, the new one says so and starts nothing; choose Quit in the tray's menu and start the app again.

    The first start offers the same three choices as the phone: New to Old Brain Fix with the Starter Set, where Simple or Advanced is picked, Add a Device of mine and Restore from a Backup (see Getting started), and a fourth, Rebuild from a vault (see Take your Notes to another app). The Profile takes the name and picture of your login on the computer, or else the computer's name. The desktop asks neither for the speech model nor for alarm access.

    Show a place's Notes and add something there.

  • Tap the folder on the Agenda.
  • Tap an Area, then a Topic.
  • Swipe right to go back one level.
  • Tap + to add something here.

  • Click a place in the sidebar on the left.
  • Click a level of the path above the list.
  • Click the back arrow for the place shown before.
  • Right-click an Area or a Topic for its menu.
  • Click + at the end of the list's heading.

    The window has the menu bar on top and two columns below it; the border between them can be dragged. The window opens as large and where it was closed, maximized if it was, with the sidebar as wide as it was left. This is kept on this computer only. A window whose monitor is gone opens centred.

    • Left, the sidebar: Agenda, Unsorted, All Notes and Frogs, then "Areas" with a "+" for a new Area, each Area with its Topics below it, Tags, which lists every #tag by its levels (see Browse your #tags), and the Trash. A shield at the end of an Area's row shows that it is hidden from AI. The place the list shows is marked. Unsorted says how many Notes it holds, such as "Unsorted 7"; no other place shows a count. The chevron at the end of an Area's row folds its Topics and unfolds them again; this computer keeps each fold, and a folded Area still shows the Topic the list shows. The Areas stand in your own order, the same on all your Devices: drag an Area by its row up or down, and a line shows where it goes when you let go; dropped where it stands or away from the Areas, it stays. A card dropped on an Area's row is filed there instead. A right-click on an Area opens Move up and Move down, but not past the first or last place, Export as Pack…, People when it is shared, Hidden from AI when it is not shared or is marked, and, as far as you manage the Area, New Topic, in Advanced, Rename, Colour and icon, Share… and Delete; a right-click on a Topic opens Export as Pack… and, as far as you manage its Area, Rename and Delete. These menus close once an entry is picked, with the mouse, the keyboard or a screen reader.
    • Middle, the list: on top the back and forward arrows and the path to the place shown, such as "Areas › Household › Repairs", where a click on a level shows it; "Areas" leads to All Notes. Below them what is selected on the left. All Notes, Unsorted, an Area, a Topic and the Trash have a search field; every list of Notes except the Trash has a switch between the compact and the detailed view of its cards and a filter by person. The heading ends with a "+".

    The "+" of All Notes, an Area and a Topic opens a menu: Note opens the quick note, Checklist, Shopping List and Frog ask for a name, Tally and Session open their page, and in an Area Topic adds a Topic. Tally, Session, Frog and Topic are there in Advanced only; Session and Frog for who may set them up there, Topic for who manages the Area. What it makes is filed into the Area or Topic shown, from All Notes into none. A new Checklist or Shopping List opens in the Note dialog, the cursor in its first Line or its field at the top (see Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Frogs). The "+" of the Agenda, beside its heading, and of Unsorted opens the quick note at once. Nothing is made while the list shows another person's Notes only. Every list ends with room after its last card, so that card scrolls fully into view.

    The Agenda's bar holds All Routines and Reminders, New Routine or Reminder and, in Advanced, New Session; the desktop's Agenda has no Calendar and no Profile picture, the menu bar leads to the rest.

    Buttons are rounded rectangles with a fill and an outline, darker in light and lighter in dark under the pointer, and with a ring while they hold the keyboard focus. The main action of a dialog, such as Save, Create or Done, is on the teal accent; Delete, Remove and Move to Trash are on the error colour. Links stay links, entries of a menu stay flat, and icon-only buttons such as ⋮, the pencil and the view button get a rounded background under the pointer and name themselves in a tooltip. Nothing opens or changes on hover alone.

    While a sync of this computer runs, a thin line in the accent moves back and forth under the menu bar, taking no room; the app stays usable meanwhile. A screen reader finds it as a progress indicator named "Syncing…".

    Select, file and open Notes with the mouse

    Pick one or several cards in a list, file them, or open one.

  • Tap a card to switch its view.
  • Tap ⋮ ▸ File into… on a card.
  • Long-press a card to open it.
    •
  • Click a card to select it.
  • Ctrl-click more cards, or Shift-click the last of a run.
  • Drag the selected cards onto an Area or Topic.
  • Click a card's chevron to switch its view.
  • Double-click a card to open it.
    •

    In a list of Notes, a click selects a card; its view button, the chevron at its top right beside ⋮ and the pencil, switches it between its compact and its detailed view, in every list but the Trash (see Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Frogs), kept on this computer until the list's switch is used again; a double-click or the pencil opens the Note in the Note dialog. A web address in a card's text, starting with "http://" or "https://", is a link: a click on it opens it in the browser, a click beside it selects the card.

    A right-click on a card or its ⋮ opens Edit, outside the Trash Open in its own window, File into…, where you may set them up Routine… and Reminder…, in Advanced Frog and Session…, on a Note you may change Merge into… and Importance, then the Checklist and Shopping List entries with Show on the Agenda and its three choices (see Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Frogs), Send to a person, Copy as text, Save as Markdown…, Export as Pack…, Remove “From …” on a Note a Pack brought unclaimed, where the Note has boxes, Show ticks, outside a Shared Area Hidden from AI (see Hide a Note from the AI) and, on a Note you may change, Move to trash last. A file on a card opens with a click (see Attach a file to a Note). Routine…, Reminder…, Frog and Session… are for that card alone, also when more cards are selected. In the Trash a card has no ⋮; its right-click offers Restore alone. A card dragged onto an Area or Topic in the sidebar is filed there, an Unsorted one leaving Unsorted; Notes in the Trash are not dragged.

    Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on macOS) adds a card to the selection or takes it away again, and Shift-click selects every card from the one clicked last to this one. Selected cards carry a check mark and a thicker outline. Dragging one of them onto an Area or Topic, File into… in its right-click menu, Note ▸ File into… and Ctrl+M file them all together; the dialog says how many go. A card outside the selection is dragged alone. Esc and showing another place drop the selection. In a Shared Area a Member's selection may hold Notes other people wrote, which only a Manager moves: they stay where they are, and a line at the top says that not every Note was filed; its click shows how many moved and how many stayed.

    Merge into…, Importance, Move to trash and Restore in a selected card's right-click menu act on all the cards selected too, as do Note ▸ Merge into…, Importance, Delete and, in the Trash, Restore while no Note is open. Merge into… offers every Note, the selected ones included: a selected Note picked takes the others in, and the list stays. Move to trash asks nothing; Ctrl+Z brings all of them back at once, and takes back a Merge of all of them the same way. A Note you may not change stays as it is. Each of them ends the selection.

    Work in the Note dialog

    Read and change one Note in a dialog over the list.

  • Long-press a card to open it.
  • Type into the text.
  • Tap a badge to change that setting.
  • Tap the back arrow to close it.
    •
  • Double-click a card, or click its pencil.
  • Type into the text.
  • Click a badge to change that setting.
  • Click the cross, or press Esc.
    •

    A Note opens as a dialog over the list and the sidebar, one at a time; the menu bar stays at hand above it. It holds the text with the format bar, in Advanced, under it the Note's settings as badges, then its Lines, photos, recordings and files with Attach a file…, its Tally's setup and its Sections, Add to Note and the rest of what can be done to it. Its cross, Esc, Alt+Left, the mouse's back button and a click beside it close it, and what was typed is kept, a Section typed into Add to Note too. Its edges and corners can be dragged to make it larger or smaller; it stays centred, and opens at that size from then on, on this computer.

    Each badge shows a setting's icon and its value, as an icon or as short text, as on the phone (see Capture and sort): the Importance, Area and Topic, Captured, each Routine and Reminder, the kind, on every Note, which also changes it (see Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Frogs), the Text format of a Plain Note (Plain text, Formatted text or Code; see Formatted text), Show on the Agenda for a Checklist without a Routine and for a Shopping List, Show ticks while there is a box, Hidden from AI outside a Shared Area, and "+" with Routine, Reminder, Frog and Session for who may set them up, Frog and Session in Advanced only. A click opens its small editor as a dialog over the Note; Area and Topic opens File into…, a Routine or Reminder its editor. The Routine editor, a Tally's pages, the New Tally page and the New Session page open as dialogs over what they were opened from. Captured's dialog shows Remove beside each captured detail but the time, and a click on the small grey line under a Section's text, such as "12:40 · Home · Dentist", opens the same dialog for that Section; Remove asks once, and the dialog stays open with what is left. In the detailed view of a card the Note and each Section show that line under their text, a Section's with its time alone when it was captured the same day as the part before it.

    The text of a Section, an Amend or a Note merged in, is edited in place in a field of its own with the same format bar as the Note's text, by its author and in a Shared Area also by the Area's Managers, and saved as the dialog closes or the field is left; it keeps its place and its capture time, and once edited says under its capture line when and by whom, such as "Edited 3/2/2026 11:00 by Ann". A right-click in any text field opens Cut, Copy, Paste and Select all at the pointer, Cut and Copy greyed while nothing is selected, and with words selected in a formatted text also the format bar's Bold, Italic, Code, Link and Code block. A text that is only read, such as another person's Section, a Note's in the Trash, a Transcript, a Photo Text or the History, is selected by dragging the mouse over it and copied with Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on macOS); a right-click on it opens Copy and Select all. A link in it opens in the browser with a click. While a selection is dragged, in a text field or in such a text, the mouse wheel scrolls and the selection follows, and dragging past the top or bottom edge scrolls by itself, faster the further out the mouse is. Copy as text copies the Note and all its Sections, as they show. Note ▸ History lists every change of the Note, the newest first (see Capture and sort).

    A click on a photo in the Note dialog shows it full screen. A right-click on a photo in the Note dialog or full screen opens Save image as…, which saves its file where you say, Copy image, which puts the picture on the clipboard, and Open in another program, which opens it in the computer's program for pictures. Saved and copied say nothing; when one of them fails, a line at the top of the window says so, and a click on it shows why and what to do.

A new Note window opens as large as the last one closed, on this computer.

    Use keys instead of swipes

    Go back and forward, and reach what you need, from the keyboard.

  • Swipe right to go back one level.
  • Swipe left on the Agenda for the Areas list.
    •
  • Press Alt+Left or the mouse's back button to go back.
  • Press Ctrl+N for a new Note.
  • Press Ctrl+F to search.
  • Press Ctrl+Enter to save what you typed.
  • Press Esc to close what lies on top.
    •

    Where the phone goes back with a swipe, the desktop uses keys and the mouse's side buttons. Alt+Left and Alt+Right rest while the cursor is in a text field; the mouse buttons always work.

    • Alt+Left, the mouse's back button or the back arrow above the list: the place the list showed before, as a browser goes back, with the search, the person filter and the scroll position it was left with. In a dialog opened from another it leads back to that one, and otherwise closes the dialog, the Note dialog and the quick note keeping what was typed, and the Routine editor, a Tally entry and the New Tally and Session pages saving a change as Esc does; a photo or table shown full screen closes.
    • Alt+Right, the mouse's forward button or the forward arrow: the place back came from, as it was left. Showing another place forgets what lay ahead; a place shown anew from the sidebar or the path starts with an empty search, at the top.
    • Ctrl+N, Ctrl+F, Ctrl+M, Ctrl+Shift+M, Ctrl+K and Ctrl+, (Cmd on macOS): New Note, Find, File into…, Merge into…, Ask… and Settings…, as in the menu bar; also while the cursor is in a text field and over the Note dialog, not while another dialog is open, but Ctrl+F over the Settings, which puts the cursor into their search field.
    • F2: renames the Area or Topic the list shows, as Area ▸ Rename; not while the cursor is in a text field or a dialog is open.
    • Alt+Up and Alt+Down: move the Area the list shows one place up or down in your own order of the Areas, as Area ▸ Move up and Move down, also a Shared Area, whose others keep their own order; not while the cursor is in a text field or a dialog is open. In a Line of a Checklist they move the Line instead.
    • Over the cards of a Note list, while no dialog is open and the cursor is in no field: Up and Down select the next card, Shift+Up and Shift+Down extend the selection, Ctrl+A (Cmd+A on macOS) selects all cards, Enter opens the Note of the card reached last, Delete moves the cards selected to the Trash without asking, for Ctrl+Z to bring back, and 1, 2 and 3 make them Important, Normal or Unimportant. Down in the search field goes to the first card found. The card selected scrolls into view, and after an action Up and Down go on from it while it still shows, so Unsorted is sorted with Down, Ctrl+M, a few letters of the place and Enter for each Note. In the Trash Delete and the digits do nothing. The Note menu shows Delete and the digits beside its entries while they act on the cards selected.
    • Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on macOS): as Edit ▸ Undo, the last delete, check-off, filing or Merge comes back. Deleting what can be restored asks nothing, so Ctrl+Z is the way back at once; it brings a Note back from the Trash with its Routines and Reminders, a Routine, a Reminder, a Frog, a Tally entry, a Line, the "From …" label, a Transcript or a Photo Text, unchecks an Occurrence or a Frog, files Notes back where they were, those from Unsorted back to Unsorted, and undoes a Merge. In a text field Ctrl+Z undoes the typing instead.
    • Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on macOS): saves the quick note's Note and saves the text being edited in the Note dialog. Enter alone starts a new line. In every other dialog it runs the button on the accent colour, such as Save, Create or Send, in the Routine editor, a Tally entry and the New Tally and Session pages too; in Merge into… it merges into the first Note found once something is typed, and in File into… it takes the first row, as Enter does. It does not answer a question that deletes or discards. The first field of a dialog has the cursor as the dialog opens, also in the Routine editor, a Tally's pages, Send feedback and a person's Profile; the pages Settings, Profile, People, Backups and recovery, Sync and Pair a Device open without it. While the cursor is in a field Alt+Left rests, as in every field; Esc and the mouse's back button still leave.
    • Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2 and Ctrl+3 (Cmd on macOS) in the quick note and the capture popup: save the Note with a Nudge in 10 min, 30 min or 1 h, as the Nudge buttons do; the line under them names the keys. The list's 1, 2 and 3 have no Ctrl and do nothing while a dialog is open.
    • Enter in the capture popup saves the Note; Shift+Enter starts a new line.
    • In a Line of a Checklist, Enter splits the Line at the cursor, Backspace at its start joins it with the Line above, Tab and Shift+Tab put it under the Line above and back at the top, Alt+Up and Alt+Down move it one place up or down within its group or the top level, as the Line's Move up and Move down do, and Up and Down move to the Line above and below. In a Line of a Shopping List Alt+Up and Alt+Down move an open Line one place among the open ones. In a Shopping List's field Enter adds the Line, and in one of its Lines it ends the rename (see Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Frogs).
    • Ctrl+B and Ctrl+I (Cmd on macOS): bold and italic while the Note's text is edited.
    • Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on macOS) in the quick note's empty field: takes the clipboard's text into the field and its picture into the Note as its photo.
    • Enter in a one-line field of a dialog, such as a name or a link's address, takes it.
    • Esc closes a menu, a dialog, as does a click beside it, and a photo or table shown full screen; over the list without a dialog it drops the cards selected. In a table cell, Tab and Shift+Tab move to the next and the previous cell (see Formatted text). The Note dialog and the quick note keep what was typed when they close. The Routine editor, a Tally entry and the New Tally and Session pages save a change as their Save does, and ask "Discard changes?" only about one that can't be saved.

    Capture with the hotkey

    Keep a thought from anywhere on the computer, without the window.

  • Long-press the Old Brain Fix icon.
  • Tap Text.
  • Type what's on your mind.
  • Tap 10 min, 30 min or 1 h.
    •
  • Press Ctrl+Alt+N anywhere.
  • Type what's on your mind.
  • Press Enter.
  • Change the key with Record keys in Settings.
    •

    The hotkey, Ctrl+Alt+N unless changed, opens the popup "Capture a Note" over all other windows, also while the app runs in the tray without its window. The tray's Capture a Note, File ▸ Quick note and the command oldbrainfix capture open it too. It takes text, a voice recording and the clipboard: it holds a field, Record voice, Paste, To full editor, the alarm clock, whose menu offers Routine and Reminder, what Settings under "Quick note" switched on, as the quick note in the window does, and the Nudge buttons. It never asks anything: Enter saves the Note with a Nudge in 1 h, or without one once the Note is sorted, Shift+Enter starts a new line, Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2 and Ctrl+3 save with a Nudge in 10 min, 30 min or 1 h, and Esc keeps what is typed the same way (see Capture and sort).

    • The hotkey is set in Settings under "Quick capture": Record keys reads "Press the keys…" and takes the next keys pressed together, Ctrl, Alt or both (Cmd on macOS), maybe Shift, with a letter, a digit, F1 to F12 or Space. While it waits, the app's own keys do nothing; Esc or a click elsewhere stops it and keeps the hotkey. The field "Hotkey" beside it takes the keys typed out, such as Super+Shift+F5; a key with Super is typed there on Linux and Windows, where the window does not hear it.
    • On X11, Windows and macOS the app takes the key itself; on Wayland the desktop manages it, asks once and lets you change it in its own keyboard settings. The line under the field says when the key is taken or no hotkey is possible.
    • The key works while the app runs, also from the tray.
    • On Wayland the desktop decides whether the popup stays above other windows and where the keyboard goes once it closes.

    Capture from the command line

    Keep a Note from a terminal, a launcher or a macro pad.

  • Use the desktop app for this; the phone has no command line.
    •
  • Open a terminal.
  • Type oldbrainfix capture buy milk and press Enter.
  • Find "buy milk" in Unsorted.
    •
    • oldbrainfix opens the window.
    • oldbrainfix capture opens the capture popup.
    • oldbrainfix capture buy milk keeps "buy milk" as an Unsorted Note with a Nudge in 1 h, without a window. After -- every word is text, also one that starts with "-".
    • oldbrainfix capture - keeps what comes in on standard input.
    • oldbrainfix import FILE imports a sync bundle and prints what came in.
    • oldbrainfix open FILE shows a Sent Item in the window, to accept or decline; a Profile card file adds the person and opens their Profile.
    • oldbrainfix --version or -V prints the version, such as "Old Brain Fix 0.6.0", without starting the app.
    • oldbrainfix --help prints the usage.

    A capture or an import goes through the running app, which plans the Nudge at once; when none runs, the command writes the Note itself, and the Nudge comes once the app runs. A capture that worked prints nothing. The exit code is 0 when it worked, 1 when the Note could not be kept or the bundle imported, and 2 for wrong arguments or nothing to capture. Launchers, key-binding tools and macro pads can run these commands, also where the desktop offers no hotkey.

    •

  • Click Done, review.

    Ask about your Notes

    Ask an AI model of your choice about your Notes, answered with links to them.

  • Mark private Notes and Areas Hidden from AI.
  • Tap Ask in the Agenda's bar.
  • Read what is sent, then tap Allow.
  • Type your question and tap Ask.
  • Tap a Note under From these Notes to open it.
    •
  • Enter provider, model and API key under Ask panel in the Settings.
  • Press Ctrl+K, or click AI ▸ Ask….
  • Type your question and press Enter.
  • Click a Note under From these Notes to open it.
  • Click Keep as Note to keep the answer.
    •

    Only the desktop talks to the provider. Old Brain Fix provides no model and no account: under Ask panel in the Settings you choose Anthropic (Claude) or an OpenAI-compatible provider such as DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Ollama or LM Studio, with its base URL (empty asks Ollama on this computer), the model and your own API key, which a model on this computer does not need. The key stays on this computer: it is not synced and is in no Backup. An http:// address is allowed only on this computer or the home network.

    The line under the heading names the provider and the model. The model searches your Notes several times with its own words, reads the Notes it uses and links them in its answer; a click on a link, or on a title under From these Notes, opens the Note over the panel. The model answers only from the Notes it read for this question, never from general knowledge, and says "That's not in your Notes" when they hold nothing about it. A link to a Note it did not read is removed, its words stay, and the answer then says A source could not be checked. Only what the model finds and reads is sent to the provider: the names of your Areas, Topics and tags, your Routines and Reminders with their check-offs when it lists them, the titles and short excerpts of the Notes its searches find, and the Notes it reads in full, with their Sections, Transcripts, Photo Texts, Attachment Texts and Capture Context (when and where a Note was captured, the calendar event and the Bluetooth device). Notes and Areas Hidden from AI are never sent, not even as a count. Asking does not pause sync.

    A question after an answer is asked after it, so you can ask on. Start over drops the questions asked, and Stop puts a question back into the field, as does a question that gets no answer, and the top then says why. The answers are gone once the list shows another place; Keep as Note saves the question and its answer, with its links, as a new Note in Unsorted. When you ask the model to change something, such as "sort what's new" into Areas and Topics, its changes wait as an AI Task: the panel says so, and Review shows them to accept or reject as a whole (see Open the AI port). Sync waits until you decide.

    On the phone, Ask shows in the Agenda's bar once a computer of yours is paired. The phone runs no model and talks to no provider: it sends the question over the paired connection to your computer, which asks its provider with its own key, as the panel does, and sends back the answer with its links, which open the Notes on the phone. The computer must be running, at home or through your VPN (see Ask from away from home). Before the first question, and again whenever the computer asks another provider or model, the phone names the computer, the provider, the model and its address and what is sent, the same as above, and asks your permission; nothing is sent before you tap Allow, and Not now sends nothing. A follow-up is asked after the earlier answers, Start over drops them, as leaving the page does, and Keep as Note saves the question and its answer as a new Note in Unsorted. When the model changes something, the phone says that the changes wait for your review on the computer. When no computer of yours answers, or its provider is not set up, the page says why and what to do; a question that gets no answer goes back into the field, and the top says why.

    Ask from away from home

    Reach your computer from the phone through a VPN, to ask and to sync.

  • Install Tailscale on the phone and sign in.
  • Tap the Profile picture, then Sync with another Device.
  • Type the computer's Tailscale name under Address by hand.
  • Tap Ask in the Agenda's bar.
    •
  • Install Tailscale on the computer and sign in.
  • Leave Start at login ticked in Settings.
  • Leave the computer on.
    •

    Old Brain Fix builds no remote access of its own; a VPN you run outside the app carries the paired connection. A VPN that routes your home network to the phone, such as WireGuard on the router, needs nothing more: the phone reaches the computer at the address it found it at home. Tailscale gives the computer another address and does not carry the network's discovery, so give the phone the computer's Tailscale name, such as desk.tail1234.ts.net, or its Tailscale address, under Address by hand below the computer in Paired Devices. A port after a colon is needed only when the computer hosts on another port than the one it was last found at.

    The phone tries the computer where this network finds it first, then at its address by hand, then where it was last. It trusts only the certificate it paired with, at every address, so a device that answers there in its place gets nothing. Automatic sync takes the same way, so the phone syncs from away too. The address stays on this phone: it is not synced and goes when the computer is forgotten. An address that is none, or a port outside 1 to 65535, is said under the field and not kept.

    Take your Notes to another app

    Write every Note as Markdown files for another app, and bring them back later.

  • Sync the phone with the computer.
  • Export the vault in the desktop app.
    •
  • Click File ▸ Export as vault….
  • Choose the folder the vault goes into.
  • Wait until the top says Vault written.
    •

    Only the desktop writes a vault. It goes into a new folder inside the one you choose, named oldbrainfix-vault- and the date, so nothing in that folder is changed. Each Area is a folder, each Topic a folder inside it, and each Note a Markdown file named by its first line and the day it was captured, with its Sections as dated parts, its Lines as a task list, a Tally's entries as a table, a Session's Tallies as links, and its photos, recordings and files in an attachments folder beside it. Its text is written as it is, and a link to another Note becomes a link to that Note's file, which Obsidian and git hosts follow. What the Markdown does not show, such as the capture time, the Area, the Importance and the Routines and Reminders with their check-offs, is kept as properties at the top of the file.

    Everything this computer holds goes in, Notes of other people in Shared Areas with their author and Notes hidden from AI too, but not where and with what a Note was captured. The folder is not encrypted: keep it where only you can read it. Writing it can take a while; the dialog can be closed, and the top says when it is written, its tap the folder. Nothing you change in the vault comes back by itself, and the vault is not kept current: export again for a new one. A Backup remains the way to keep everything (see Backups, locks and privacy).

    To come back, click Rebuild from a vault on the first start of an empty Old Brain Fix, beside Restore from a Backup, and choose the vault's folder. Each Note comes back with its properties, every Note, Area and Topic new and yours, Shared Areas as your own private ones. A Markdown or text file you made in the other app becomes a new Note with its text, its tags and its file's date, in the Area of its first folder and the Topic of its second. Your other Devices do not sync with the rebuilt Notes: empty them and pair them again. A folder without a Markdown or text file, an Old Brain Fix that holds Notes already, or a vault of a newer version rebuilds nothing, and the top says why.
