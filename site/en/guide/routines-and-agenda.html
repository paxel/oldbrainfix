A Routine is something to be done on a repeating schedule; a Reminder is a Routine that happens only once. Both come from a Note, ring at their time and ring again until they are Done. The Agenda shows what is due today and on the six days after it.

Making a Routine or Reminder

  • From a Note: the alarm clock on the Note's card, "Routine, Reminder, Frog or Session", offers Routine and Reminder; the "+" at the end of the Note editor's badges offers the same, and the capture screen before the Note is saved (see Capture and sort). The editor shows the Note on top under "From the Note"; a tap on it opens the Note. A Note can have several Routines and Reminders; on a Session each of them runs the Session, which its Note sets up (see Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Frogs).
  • Without a Note: "New Routine or Reminder" in the Agenda's bar opens the editor with the field "What is to be done?". The text typed there becomes the Routine's Note.

A new Routine or Session starts every day, all day, a new Reminder once at the next full hour. Save in the top bar writes it; leaving the editor without Save drops the change. Once saved, the bar also holds the calendar button where the phone has a calendar app, History for a Routine, and Delete, which asks once; a deleted Routine stays in the Trash for 30 days, and its Note stays. In a Shared Area only a Manager changes Routines and Reminders; anyone else sees the editor with "Only a Manager of this Area can change this."

The schedule

The schedule is set in one of two modes, Simple and Advanced. The simple mode offers:

  • Once: a Reminder.
  • Every …: a number, changed with − and +, of days, weeks, months or years.
  • Weekdays: one button per weekday; any number of them can be picked.
  • Monthly on a day: every so many months, on a day of the month from 1 to 31. Months without that day are skipped.
  • Monthly on a weekday: every so many months, on the first, second, third, fourth or last of a weekday, such as the last Friday.

Starts (On for Once) picks the first day. Everything but Once has Ends: Never, On a day with its last day, or After a number of times, where each time of day counts separately.

The advanced mode takes the repeat rule as an RRULE, the format calendars use, such as FREQ=MONTHLY;BYDAY=1MO for the first Monday of every month; left empty, it happens once. A schedule the simple mode cannot show opens in the advanced mode.

Under Times of day, Add a time adds a time, on the phone with a picker that opens at the current time, on the desktop from the field beside it, which holds the current time when the editor opens and after each time added and is typed over for another; each time has a button that removes it. Without a time it is all day. A Reminder has at most one time. Under Next the next three Occurrences show while the schedule is edited, or "This never comes up." Checking an Occurrence off early or late never moves the schedule.

Re-nag and Escalation

The folded section Re-nag says how often an ignored alarm rings again, such as "Rings again every 10 min until Done". It rings again at that interval until it is Done or snoozed, or until the next Occurrence is due; then it counts as Missed. A Reminder stops at midnight. The choices are Default and 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes. The default is set in Settings under "Alarms", "Default on this phone", 10 min unless changed.

The folded section Escalation is off at first ("Off: it only Re-nags"). With the switch "Escalate to a full-screen alarm" on, an Occurrence ignored for a number of Re-nags (3 when first turned on; 1, 2, 3, 5 or 10) becomes a full-screen alarm with sound, also over the lock screen. It shows the Note's first line and the time, a big Done, and "Remind me again in …" with 10 min, 30 min and 1 h; Back does not close it. It rings for up to 5 minutes, then Re-nags again until it is Done or snoozed.

When it rings

An Occurrence shows as a notification with Done and Snooze; Snooze turns the same notification into 10 min, 30 min and 1 h. Both work without opening the app, also on the lock screen.

Tapping the notification opens its Note. At the top of the Note editor, above the name, the Occurrence that is due shows as "Due 15:00", or else the latest Missed one with its day, with Done. A one-time Reminder that is the Note's only one also has Done and move to trash: it checks the Reminder off, moves the Note with it to the trash and closes the editor. Snooze stays in the notification. A Note's card, compact or detailed, has a box with the time for today's Occurrence of each of its Routines and Reminders; it checks it off and unchecks it as the box on the Agenda does, and a long press on the card still opens the editor.

An all-day Occurrence only shows on the Agenda until the time set in Settings under "Alarms", All-day items remind from, 15:00 unless changed, on each Device on its own. From then on it rings and Re-nags until its day ends. The Quiet Hours hold back only Nudges and that first ring: when they hold the time, an all-day Occurrence rings once they end, and not at all when they last until midnight. Routines and Reminders with a time ring on time. A calendar file from Export or Share gives an all-day event its alarm at the time of the Device that saved it.

The sound is chosen in Settings under "Alarms", Alarm sound: Old Brain Fix's own chime at first; a tap opens the phone's sound picker to choose another sound of the phone or the chime again. "Set up alarms" there leads to the permissions alarms need to ring on time. While one is missing, a red line on top of the Agenda says so, and a tap on it leads to the setup.

After the phone was off, alarms that fell into that time do not ring late: one Catch-up Notification, "Missed while the phone was off", says how many were Missed.

Missed and the Missed Summary

An Occurrence is Missed when it was never Done: a Routine's when its next Occurrence starts, a Reminder's at midnight. The Missed Summary sits on top of the Agenda, headed "Missed": each Routine with how often it was Missed, such as "Water the plants · 3 times", up to three of its Missed Occurrences, each with a box to check it off late, and "and 2 more" for the rest. The card stays until Seen is tapped; opening the app never clears it.

History

A Routine's History opens from its editor's bar or from the menu of its row on the Agenda. It shows the Streak, now and longest, with the last 7 days as dots; the Adherence this week, this month and over the last 30 days, such as "18 of 20 Done"; and the log of every Occurrence, the latest first: Done with its time, early or late, Missed, due and not Done yet, or still to come, with the snoozes this phone recorded over about the last month. Settings can turn the Streaks off with "Show Streaks on this phone"; the Adherence shows either way. A Reminder has no History.

The Agenda

The Agenda is the start screen. From the top it shows:

  1. the Missed Summary, while there is something Missed;
  2. "x Unsorted", which opens the Unsorted Notes;
  3. "x Sent Items waiting" and "x shared Areas waiting", while there are any;
  4. under "Today": the all-day Occurrences, then the calendar's all-day appointments, then the Shopping Lists as you chose for each, then the Checklists without a Routine (see Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Frogs), then the timed Occurrences and appointments together by time, an Occurrence before an appointment at the same time;
  5. the section "Eat a frog", while there is an open Frog or one was eaten today;
  6. after a gap, tomorrow and the five days after it, dimmed, each under its weekday and date. A day with nothing due says "Nothing planned"; only today, when nothing is due, says "Nothing due today. Tap + to capture a thought." instead.

Each row of an Occurrence holds the box to check it off, its time, its Note's text, its Area's colour and icon, and ⋮. A tap on the row opens the editor. A Shopping List, a Checklist, a Checklist Routine and a Session show as a group: a heading, and under it, indented, its entries, each with its box, such as the open Lines of a Shopping List under the list's name or a Session's picks under its title. A Shopping List's and a Checklist's heading has no box; a Session's box only shows when every pick is done. The Occurrences of the coming days can be checked off today already. An Occurrence checked off today stays on the Agenda for the day, struck through as Done; its box unchecks it. Checking off opens nothing. At midnight the Agenda moves on to the new day.

"Hide what's checked on the Agenda" in Settings, under "Agenda", off unless switched on, hides instead of striking through, on this phone only: a Done Occurrence, a Checklist Routine's checked Line and a Session's pick with its entry leave the Agenda at once, and a group with nothing left goes with them, a Session's heading once every pick is done and the section "Eat a frog" once today's Frog is eaten. There is no undo on the Agenda; switched off again, the setting shows what was checked, struck through, to uncheck it. A Shopping List's and a Checklist's checked Lines leave the Agenda either way.

"Move a Note to the Trash when its one-time Reminder is done" in Settings, under "Agenda", off unless switched on, is yours rather than the phone's: it is the same on all your own Devices. Switched on, Done on a one-time Reminder that is a plain Note's only Routine or Reminder moves the Note with it to the trash, wherever you check it off: on the Agenda, in the widget, in the notification or the full-screen alarm, in the Note editor, which closes, and on its card. A Checklist, a Shopping List, a Tally, a Session and a Note with a Routine or a second Reminder stay. Unchecking does not bring the Note back; restore it from the trash within 30 days, still Done.

⋮ on a row, or a long press, opens a menu with History for a Routine and, where the phone has a calendar app, Share. An Occurrence from a locked Area set to Masked shows as "Private Routine" with its time and can still be checked off; opening it asks for its Area to be opened first.

The Agenda's bar holds, in this order: the folder Search and browse, which opens the Areas list, All Routines and Reminders, Calendar (where the phone has a calendar), New Routine or Reminder, and the Profile picture, which leads to the Profile page and Settings.

The Agenda widget

The home-screen widget "Agenda" shows what the Agenda shows for today, in the same order: the Missed Summary with its Missed Occurrences to check off late, "x Unsorted", which opens the Unsorted Notes, then under "Today" the all-day Occurrences, the calendar's all-day appointments, the Shopping Lists as you chose for each, the Checklists without a Routine, the timed Occurrences and appointments by time, and the section "Eat a frog", then, as on the Agenda, "Tomorrow" and the five days after it, each under its weekday and date and dimmed, with "Nothing planned" on a day without anything. Its groups look as on the Agenda: a Shopping List's or a Checklist's name as the heading, with its open Lines indented under it; a Checklist Routine's Occurrence with its Lines, and a Session's Occurrence with its picks, under it. Every entry has its box. When the rows are taller than the widget, it scrolls. Where the Agenda hides what is checked, the widget hides it too.

A box on the widget checks off what the same box on the Agenda checks off: an Occurrence, a Line for its Occurrence (the last Line checks the Occurrence off), a Checklist's Line, a Frog, a Missed Occurrence, and a Shopping List's Line where the list shows its open Lines. Something checked is unchecked in the app. Only today's rows and the Missed Occurrences are checked off on the widget: a box on a later day checks nothing and opens the app. A Session's box only shows whether it is Done, and a tap on it does nothing. A pick's box shows whether it has an entry; a tap on the pick opens the app at its entry form. Appointments, a Shopping List's or a Checklist's heading and the other headings have no box; a tap on them, or on any other part of a row, opens the app. A row from a locked Area set to Masked shows as on the Agenda, such as "Private Routine" with its time, and names nothing under it. The widget moves on to the new day at midnight, also while the app is closed; what it cannot know before the app is opened again, such as the next day's Frogs, shows once it is.

Sharing one Routine or Reminder

Share in a row's menu hands the Routine as its whole series, with its end, or the Reminder, to another app as a calendar file. In the editor, the calendar button of a saved one offers "Add to calendar" for each of its events, one per time of day, which opens the calendar app filled in, and Share as calendar file.

All Routines and Reminders

This page lists every Routine and Reminder alphabetically by name, also those beyond the Agenda's week. Each card shows whether it is a Routine or a Reminder, its name, its schedule and its Area's colour and icon; a tap opens its editor. New Session in the bar opens the editor of a new Session with the field "What is to be done?"; the text typed there becomes its Note, which holds the Session, with the new Routine as its first (see Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Frogs). Export in the bar hands one calendar file of all of them to the app you choose. Those of a closed locked Area stay out of the list and of the file. One whose schedule a newer version of the app saved follows at the end, saying so; a tap opens its Note.

The Calendar page

The Calendar page opens from the Agenda's bar and from Settings, under "Calendar". It shows only where the phone has a calendar, and has two sections.

Read from your calendar

  • Note the running appointment: a Note captured during an appointment carries it in its Capture Context, such as "During: Dentist".
  • Show my calendar's appointments on the Agenda: turned on, it explains what it does, asks for access to the calendar and opens "Calendars on the Agenda" with a checkbox per calendar, none ticked at first. Afterwards it names the calendars picked; a tap picks others. Turning it off keeps the calendars picked for the next time.

Write to your calendar

Write Routines and Reminders into a calendar: turned on, it explains, asks for access and lets you pick the calendar. The Routines and Reminders then appear in that calendar and follow every change. Old Brain Fix only changes or removes the events it wrote itself; turning the switch off removes them. Wherever that calendar syncs to, they show up too.

Appointments on the Agenda

The appointments of the calendars picked show on every day of the Agenda: the all-day ones after the all-day Occurrences, the timed ones among the timed Occurrences. An appointment's row is read only: it has no box, the calendar's colour runs down its side, and it shows the calendar icon and "From the calendar" with the calendar's name. A tap unfolds its place and description and folds them again. ⋮ or a long press offers Copy into a Note, which makes a new Unsorted Note of its title, time, place and description and shows its card on top of the Agenda. The events Old Brain Fix wrote into a calendar never show as appointments. The appointments are read whenever the Agenda shows them; nothing of them is kept or synced.

On the desktop

  • The Agenda shows in the same order. Its bar holds "+", which opens the quick note, All Routines and Reminders and New Routine or Reminder; the desktop has no Calendar page and shows no calendar appointments.
  • A click on an Occurrence's text opens its editor as a dialog over the window; a right-click or ⋮ opens a menu with History for a Routine and Share, which saves the calendar file where you choose. Export on All Routines and Reminders saves one file the same way; New Session beside it opens the editor of a new Session as a dialog.
  • A Routine's History shows in the list in the middle, the same as on the phone; it also opens from the History button beside the heading of the Routine's editor, and Alt+Left goes back to where it was opened. "Show Streaks on this computer" in Settings, under "History", turns the Streaks off on the desktop.
  • "Hide what's checked on the Agenda" in Settings, under "Agenda", hides what is checked on the desktop's Agenda as on the phone's, for this computer only.
  • "Move a Note to the Trash when its one-time Reminder is done" in Settings, under "Agenda", is the same setting as on the phone, switched on or off for all your own Devices; Done on the Agenda, in a notification, in the Note dialog, which closes, and on the card moves the Note to the trash as there.
  • The simple mode of the schedule offers the same as on the phone, Ends included; the last day is set as the start day is, with the buttons beside it or typed in.
  • Alarms come as notifications while the app runs, also from the tray; only on Linux without a tray does closing the window end the app and its alarms. On Linux and Windows they offer Done, 10 min, 30 min, 1 h and "Open Old Brain Fix"; on macOS they show without buttons. The Re-nag default is "Default on this computer"; "All-day items remind from" is a time typed in beside it.
  • Escalation turns the notification urgent, with the system's alarm sound; there is no full-screen alarm.
  • The Alarm sound is Old Brain Fix's own chime or a sound file ("Choose a sound file…", "Use the own chime"). The app plays it itself, so the notifications stay silent.
  • What became Missed while the app was not running is told in one notification, "Missed while Old Brain Fix was not running".
