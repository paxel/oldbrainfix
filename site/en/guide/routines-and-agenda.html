A Routine is something to be done on a repeating schedule; a Reminder is a Routine that happens only once. Both come from a Note, ring at their time and ring again until they are Done. The Agenda shows what is due today and on the six days after it. Each box below shows the steps on the phone and on the desktop.

The desktop app has no Calendar page and shows no calendar appointments. Its alarms come as notifications while it runs; an escalated alarm turns the notification urgent, with the system's alarm sound, and there is no full-screen alarm.

  • Type the time, then click Add a time.

    • From a Note: the alarm clock on the Note's card, "Routine, Reminder, Frog or Session", offers Routine and Reminder; it sits in the row under a detailed card. The "+" at the end of the Note editor's badges offers the same, and so does the alarm clock of the quick note before the Note is saved, which saves the Routine or Reminder together with the Note and, left with nothing typed, neither (see Sort while capturing). In Simple they offer only Routine and Reminder; Frog and Session are there in Advanced. The editor shows the Note on top under "From the Note"; a tap on it opens the Note. For a Note still being captured it shows the text typed so far, or "The Note being captured", and a tap does nothing. A Note can have several Routines and Reminders; on a Session each of them runs the Session, which its Note sets up (see Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Frogs).
    • Without a Note: "New Routine or Reminder" in the Agenda's bar opens the editor with the field "What is to be done?". The text typed there becomes the Routine's Note.

    A new Routine or Session starts every day, all day, a new Reminder once at the next full hour; the Agenda's "New Routine or Reminder" starts a Routine. Save in the top bar writes it; leaving the editor without Save drops the change. Once saved, the bar also holds the calendar button where the phone has a calendar app, History for a Routine, and Delete, which asks once; a deleted Routine stays in the Trash for 30 days, and its Note stays. In a Shared Area only a Manager changes Routines and Reminders; anyone else sees the editor with "Only a Manager of this Area can change this.", without Delete and Save.

    On the desktop the editor opens as a dialog over the window. A Note is given a Routine or Reminder in the Note dialog, with the "+" at the end of its badges, or with Note ▸ Routine… and Note ▸ Reminder…, which follow with Frog and Session in Advanced; the cards have no alarm clock. The alarm clock of the quick note, in the window and the popup, offers Routine and Reminder too. The dialog has the History button beside its heading and Cancel, Delete and Save at the bottom; it has no calendar button. Esc or a click beside it drops the change. Where only a Manager may change a Routine, a click on it opens its Note instead of the editor.

    Choose when it comes up

    Repeat on chosen weekdays, every few days or weeks, or monthly.

  • Tap the Routine on the Agenda.
  • Tap Weekdays.
  • Tap the days it comes up on.
  • Under Ends, pick when it stops.
  • Tap Save.
    •
  • Click the Routine on the Agenda.
  • Click Weekdays.
  • Click the days it comes up on.
  • Under Ends, pick when it stops.
  • Click Save.
    •

    The schedule is set in one of two modes, Simple and Advanced. They belong to the schedule alone: both show whether the app offers Simple or Advanced. The simple mode offers:

    • Once: a Reminder.
    • Every …: a number, changed with − and +, of days, weeks, months or years.
    • Weekdays: one button per weekday; any number of them can be picked.
    • Monthly on a day: every so many months, on a day of the month from 1 to 31. Months without that day are skipped.
    • Monthly on a weekday: every so many months, on the first, second, third, fourth or last of a weekday, such as the last Friday.

    Starts (On for Once) picks the first day. Everything but Once has Ends: Never, On a day with its last day, or After a number of times, where each time of day counts separately.

    The line When at the top shows the schedule as a sentence, such as "Weekdays Mon, Thu at 08:00, 15:00, until 12/31". The schedule can also be typed there: the buttons below follow as you type, and the next word, weekday names and times are offered under the line. A word it does not understand is marked and changes nothing. It reads what the simple mode offers; on a schedule in the advanced mode it only shows it, with "Switch to Simple to type." Only the schedule is saved, not the sentence.

    The advanced mode takes the repeat rule as an RRULE, the format calendars use, such as FREQ=MONTHLY;BYDAY=1MO for the first Monday of every month; left empty, it happens once. A schedule the simple mode cannot show opens in the advanced mode.

    Under Times of day, Add a time adds a time, on the phone with a picker that opens at the current time, on the desktop from the field beside it, which holds the current time when the editor opens and after each time added and is typed over for another; each time has a button that removes it. Without a time it is all day. A Reminder has at most one time. Under Next the next three Occurrences show while the schedule is edited, or "This never comes up." Checking an Occurrence off early or late never moves the schedule.

    On the desktop the simple mode offers the same, Ends included; the start day and the last day are set with the buttons beside them or typed in.

    Get reminded again until it is Done

    Let an ignored alarm ring again, and turn louder after a while.

  • Tap the Routine on the Agenda.
  • Tap Re-nag and pick how often.
  • Tap Escalation.
  • Switch on Escalate to a full-screen alarm.
  • Tap Save.
    •
  • Click the Routine on the Agenda.
  • Under Re-nag, pick how often.
  • Tick Escalate to an urgent alarm with sound.
  • Click Save.
    •

    The folded section Re-nag says how often an ignored alarm rings again, such as "Rings again every 10 min until Done". It rings again at that interval until it is Done or snoozed, or until the next Occurrence is due; then it counts as Missed. A Reminder stops at midnight. Under "This one" the choices are Default, which names the default, such as "Default (10 min)", and 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes. The default is set in Settings under "Alarms", "Default on this phone", 10 min unless changed.

    The folded section Escalation is off at first ("Off: it only Re-nags"). With the switch "Escalate to a full-screen alarm" on, an Occurrence ignored for a number of Re-nags (3 when first turned on; 1, 2, 3, 5 or 10) becomes a full-screen alarm with sound, also over the lock screen. It shows the Note's first line and the time, a big Done, and "Remind me again in …" with 10 min, 30 min and 1 h; Back does not close it. It rings for up to 5 minutes, then Re-nags again until it is Done or snoozed. Re-nag and Escalation are there in Simple and in Advanced.

    On the desktop, Re-nag and Escalation are open in the editor, not folded. The Re-nag default is "Default on this computer", in Settings under "Alarms". The switch is "Escalate to an urgent alarm with sound": the escalated alarm turns the notification urgent, with the system's alarm sound; there is no full-screen alarm.

    On the iPhone there is no full-screen alarm: an escalated Occurrence rings as an iPhone alarm, through silent mode and Focus, on iOS's own alarm screen; the switch still reads "Escalate to a full-screen alarm". Its Stop counts as Done, and its one Snooze waits 10 min, as the alarm has room for one only. Without the Alarms access it comes as a time-sensitive notification.

    Check it off when it rings

    Mark a Routine or Reminder as Done.

  • Tap the notification.
  • Tap Done at the top.
    •
  • Double-click the Note's card.
  • Click Done at the top.

    An Occurrence shows as a notification with Done and Snooze; Snooze turns the same notification into 10 min, 30 min and 1 h. Both work without opening the app, also on the lock screen.

    Tapping the notification opens its Note. At the top of the Note editor, above the name, the Occurrence that is due shows as "Due 15:00", once its time has come, or else the latest Missed one with its day, with Done. A one-time Reminder that is the Note's only Routine or Reminder also has Done and move to trash, where this Device may change the Note: it checks the Reminder off, moves the Note with it to the trash and closes the editor. Snooze stays in the notification. A Note's card, compact or detailed, has a box with the time for today's Occurrence of each of its Routines and Reminders; it checks it off and unchecks it as the box on the Agenda does, and a long press on the card still opens the editor.

    An all-day Occurrence only shows on the Agenda until the time set in Settings under "Alarms", All-day items remind from, 15:00 unless changed, on each Device on its own. From then on it rings and Re-nags until it counts as Missed: at the start of the day of its next Occurrence, or at midnight when there is none. The Quiet Hours hold back only Nudges and that first ring: when they hold the time, an all-day Occurrence rings once they end, and not at all when they last until it counts as Missed. Routines and Reminders with a time ring on time. A calendar file from Export or Share gives an all-day event its alarm at the time of the Device that saved it.

    The sound is chosen in Settings under "Alarms", Alarm sound: Old Brain Fix's own chime at first; a tap opens the phone's sound picker to choose another sound of the phone or the chime again. "Set up alarms" there leads to the permissions alarms need to ring on time. While one is missing, a red line on top of the Agenda says so, and a tap on it leads to the setup.

    After the phone was off, alarms that fell into that time do not ring late: one Catch-up Notification, "Missed while the phone was off", says how many were Missed.

    On the desktop, alarms come as notifications while the app runs, also from the tray; only on Linux without a tray does closing the window end the app and its alarms. On Linux and Windows they offer Done, 10 min, 30 min, 1 h and "Open Old Brain Fix"; on macOS they show without buttons. A click on the notification or "Open Old Brain Fix" brings the window up; it opens no Note. A double-click on the Note's card opens the Note dialog, which shows the same Due line, Done and Done and move to trash at the top; a click on the box on a card checks it off and opens nothing. "All-day items remind from" is a time typed in, in Settings under "Alarms". The Alarm sound is Old Brain Fix's own chime or a sound file ("Choose a sound file…", "Use the own chime"). The app plays it itself, so the notifications stay silent. What became Missed while the app was not running is told in one notification, "Missed while Old Brain Fix was not running".

    On the iPhone the notification is time-sensitive and offers Done and the three snoozes, 10 min, 30 min and 1 h, at once; each Re-nag is a notification of its own. There is no choice of sound: notifications ring with the iPhone's notification sound and alarms with its alarm sound, and Alarm sound changes nothing there. After the iPhone was off there is no Catch-up Notification; the Missed Summary shows what was Missed when the app opens.

    The Agenda is the start screen. From the top it shows:

    1. the Missed Summary, while there is something Missed;
    2. "x Unsorted", which opens the Unsorted Notes;

A tap on the row opens the editor. A Shopping List, a Checklist, a Checklist Routine and a Session show as a group: a heading, and under it, indented, its entries, each with its box, such as the open Lines of a Shopping List under the list's name or a Session's picks under its title. Each Shopping List and each Checklist without a Routine or Reminder shows as you chose for it in its card's ⋮ or its badge "Show on the Agenda": Off, One row with the number of open Lines, a heading with the count and no Lines, or Each open Line on its own row under its heading. The choice is yours, the same on all your own Devices; a list without open Lines never shows. The Occurrences of the coming days can be checked off today already. An Occurrence checked off today stays on the Agenda for the day, struck through as Done; its box unchecks it. Checking off opens nothing. At midnight the Agenda moves on to the new day.

    "Hide what's checked on the Agenda" in Settings, under "Agenda", off unless switched on, hides instead of striking through, on this phone only: a Done Occurrence, a Checklist Routine's checked Line and a Session's pick with its entry leave the Agenda at once, and a group with nothing left goes with them, a Session's heading once every pick is done and the section "Eat a frog" once today's Frog is eaten. There is no undo on the Agenda; switched off again, the setting shows what was checked, struck through, to uncheck it. A Shopping List's and a Checklist's checked Lines leave the Agenda either way.

    "Move a Note to the Trash when its one-time Reminder is done" in Settings, under "Agenda", off unless switched on, is yours rather than the phone's: it is the same on all your own Devices. Switched on, Done on a one-time Reminder that is a plain Note's only Routine or Reminder moves the Note with it to the trash, where this Device may change the Note, wherever you check it off: on the Agenda, in the widget, in the notification or the full-screen alarm, in the Note editor, which closes, and on its card. A Checklist, a Shopping List, a Tally, a Session and a Note with a Routine or a second Reminder stay. Unchecking does not bring the Note back; restore it from the trash within 30 days, still Done.

    ⋮ on a row, or a long press, opens a menu with History for a Routine and, where the phone has a calendar app, Share. An Occurrence from a locked Area set to Masked shows as "Private Routine" with its time and can still be checked off; opening it asks for its Area to be opened first.

    The Agenda's bar holds, in this order: the folder Search and browse, which opens the Areas list, All Routines and Reminders, Calendar (where the phone has a calendar), New Routine or Reminder, New Session (in Advanced), and the Profile picture, which leads to the Profile page and Settings. When they do not all fit, on a narrow phone or with Fat fingers, the last of them go into ⋮ after the Profile picture. A swipe to the left anywhere on the Agenda opens the Areas list too, and a swipe to the right on the Areas list, or Back, returns to the Agenda: the two pages follow the finger side by side, and letting go past the middle, or a quick short flick, switches; otherwise they spring back.

    The home-screen widget "Agenda" shows what the Agenda shows, in the same order: the Missed Summary with its Missed Occurrences to check off late, "x Unsorted", which opens the Unsorted Notes, then under "Today" the all-day Occurrences, the calendar's all-day appointments, the Shopping Lists and the Checklists as you chose for each, the timed Occurrences and appointments by time, and the section "Eat a frog", then, as on the Agenda, "Tomorrow" and the five days after it, each under its weekday and date and dimmed, with "Nothing planned" on a day without anything and "Nothing due today." on an empty today. Every entry has its box. When the rows are taller than the widget, it scrolls. Where the Agenda hides what is checked, the widget hides it too.

    A box on the widget checks off what the same box on the Agenda checks off: an Occurrence, a Line for its Occurrence (the last Line checks the Occurrence off), a Checklist's Line, a Frog, a Missed Occurrence, and a Shopping List's Line where the list shows its open Lines. Something checked is unchecked in the app. Only today's rows and the Missed Occurrences are checked off on the widget: a box on a later day checks nothing and opens the app. A Session's box only shows whether it is Done, and a tap on it does nothing. A pick's box shows whether it has an entry; a tap on the pick opens the app at its entry form. Appointments, a Shopping List's or a Checklist's heading and the other headings have no box; a tap on them, or on any other part of a row, opens the app. A row from a locked Area set to Masked shows as on the Agenda, such as "Private Routine" with its time, and names nothing under it. The widget moves on to the new day at midnight, also while the app is closed; what it cannot know before the app is opened again, such as the next day's Frogs, shows once it is.

    On the desktop the Agenda shows in the same order, without calendar appointments and without the line about alarms, and without Private Routine rows; an empty today says "Nothing due today. Click + to capture a thought." Its bar holds "+", which opens the quick note, All Routines and Reminders, New Routine or Reminder and, in Advanced, New Session.     On the iPhone the Agenda widget comes in medium and large.     Use your calendar

    See your appointments on the Agenda, and your Routines in a calendar.

  • Tap Calendar on the Agenda.
  • Switch on Show my calendar's appointments on the Agenda.
  • Tick the calendars to show.
  • Switch on Write Routines and Reminders into a calendar.
  • Pick the calendar to write into.
    •
  • Click ⋮ on a Routine on the Agenda.
  • Click Share.
  • Choose where to save the calendar file.
  • Import the file into your calendar app.
    •

    The Calendar page opens from the Agenda's bar and from Settings, under "Calendar". It shows only where the phone has a calendar, with the sections the phone offers.

    Read from your calendar:

    • Note the running appointment: a Note captured during an appointment carries it in its Capture Context, such as "During: Dentist".
    • Show my calendar's appointments on the Agenda: turned on, it explains what it does, asks for access to the calendar and opens "Calendars on the Agenda" with a checkbox per calendar, none ticked at first. Afterwards it names the calendars picked; a tap picks others. Turning it off keeps the calendars picked for the next time.

    Write to your calendar: Write Routines and Reminders into a calendar, turned on, explains, asks for access and lets you pick the calendar. The Routines and Reminders then appear in that calendar and follow every change. Old Brain Fix only changes or removes the events it wrote itself; turning the switch off removes them. Wherever that calendar syncs to, they show up too.

    The appointments of the calendars picked show on every day of the Agenda: the all-day ones after the all-day Occurrences, the timed ones among the timed Occurrences. An appointment's row is read only: it has no box, the calendar's colour runs down its side, and it shows the calendar icon and the calendar's name; a screen reader reads it as "From the calendar" with the name. A tap unfolds its place and description and folds them again. ⋮ or a long press offers Copy into a Note, which makes a new Unsorted Note of its title, time, place and description and shows its card on top of the Agenda. The events Old Brain Fix wrote into a calendar never show as appointments. The appointments are read whenever the Agenda shows them; nothing of them is kept or synced.

    Share in a row's menu hands the Routine as its whole series, with its end, or the Reminder, to another app as a calendar file. In the editor, the calendar button of a saved one offers "Add to calendar" for each of its events, one per time of day, which opens the calendar app filled in, and Share as calendar file.

    The desktop has no Calendar page and shows no calendar appointments. Share in a row's right-click menu or ⋮ saves the calendar file where you choose, and Export on All Routines and Reminders saves one file of all of them the same way; a calendar app can import it from there.
