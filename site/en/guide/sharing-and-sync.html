Your own Devices sync everything with each other. Other people get only what you give them: an Area you share, or a single Note you send. There is no server; changes travel over your home network, through a shared folder or as files through any messenger, encrypted. Each box below shows the steps on the phone and on the desktop.

Some changes wait for your decision before anything of them is kept: an Area offered to you from afar, a Sent Item from someone not among your People, and, on the desktop, an AI Task. They wait, also across a restart, until you accept or decline them; declining leaves nothing behind.

Let someone add you

Give another person your Profile card, so they have you among their People.

  • Tap your Profile picture on the Agenda.
  • Tap Send my Profile and pick the messenger.
  • Tap Show my QR instead, for someone next to you.
    •
  • Click Sync ▸ Profile.
  • Click Send my Profile and choose where to save the card.
  • Send the card file through any messenger or mail.
  • Click Show my QR instead, for someone next to you.
    •

    The Profile picture in the Agenda's top corner opens the Profile page: your picture, your name and your code, then Backups and recovery, Your Devices, People and Settings of this Device. Your Profile is the same on all of your Devices. A tap on the picture changes it: Choose a picture, Take a photo, or Show initials instead while it has a picture.

    A Profile card is your name and the id of your Profile. It adds you to another person's People; it never shares an Area. Send my Profile sends it as a small file with your picture, such as Ann.obfcard, through the app you pick, such as a messenger.   • Tap the person under People on your Profile page.
  • Tap Compare codes.
  • Ask them to read out the code on their Profile page.
  • Tap Codes match if it is the same.
    •
  • Click Sync ▸ People, then the person.
  • Click Compare codes.
  • Ask them to read out the code on their Profile page.
  • Click Codes match if it is the same.
    •

    Every Profile has a short code of eight characters, made from its id and its first key. A person is verified once you made sure in person that the code is theirs. Their QR scanned with the camera marks them verified at once. Compare codes asks you to have them read out the code on their own Profile page. Codes match marks them verified; Codes differ says that this Profile may not be the person it names, and leaves it unverified.

    A card that came as a file or a link, or as a QR in a picture, adds the person as "Not verified: compare codes in person to be sure." Sent Items and shared Areas from them say whether they are verified.

    A tap on a person in People, or on their picture anywhere it shows, opens their Profile: their picture, name and code, whether they are verified, with Compare codes while they are not, and Their Notes, every Note they wrote that you can see. Under Name and picture for me you give them a name and a picture only you see, on your own Devices, such as "Mum"; their own name and picture stay as they are. Block is on the bar (see Block a person).

    Share an Area with someone next to you

    Let another person sync one Area with you, both Devices on the same Wi-Fi.

  • Tap Search and browse on the Agenda.
  • Tap ⋮ next to the Area, then Share….
  • Ask them to open Sync and show their Device's code.
  • Tap Scan the code and scan it.
  • Compare the code with their Profile page, then tap Share.
    •
  • Right-click the Area in the left pane, then click Share….
  • Click Show the code.
  • Ask them to scan the code in Sync on their phone.
  • Compare the code with their Profile page, then click Share.
    •

    A Shared Area is an Area other people sync with. Only that Area goes to them, with its Topics, Notes, Routines, Reminders and their history; everything outside it stays private. In the Areas list, ⋮ next to an Area offers Share… to its owner and its Managers, and People once it is shared. A Shared Area shows the names of its people under it.

    Share… opens the share page. In Advanced, pick Member or Manager under "They may" (see Change what a person may do); in Simple the person joins as a Member. Then meet the other person, both Devices on the same Wi-Fi:

    • On their Device, they open Sync and show its code. You tap Scan the code, or type it into Pair code and tap Invite.
    • Or you tap Show this Device's code, and they scan it in Sync on their Device.

    Nobody types a name. Once the Devices met, their Profile shows with its code: "Compare the code with the one on their Profile page. Only then is anything shared." Share gives their Device this Area and nothing else, and the Area shows as shared from then on. A phone shares with a phone as well as with a computer.

    On the desktop, Share… is in the right-click menu of an Area in the left pane; in Advanced it asks for the role. Show the code then opens Pair a Device with this computer's code for the other person's phone to scan in Sync, and their Profile shows there to confirm.

    Share an Area with someone far away

    Offer an Area as a file to a person in your People.

  • Tap ⋮ next to the Area, then Share….
  • Tap Share with… and pick the person.
  • Pick how changes travel with them.
  • Pick the messenger to send the offer through.
    •
  • Right-click the Area in the left pane, then click Share….
  • Click Share with… and click the person.
  • Pick how changes travel with them.
  • Choose where to save the offer, then send the file.
    •

    Under "Someone not nearby", Share with… lists the people in People the Area can go to, those not verified marked "Not verified". Pick one, with the role chosen above. The first time, the app asks "How do changes travel with {name}?":

    • A shared folder you both reach: Nextcloud, Syncthing or a USB folder; changes travel on their own. Without a shared folder yet, you choose one.
    • Files through a messenger: you send changes as a file from their Profile page (see Send your changes to a person).

    The offer goes as a file through the app you pick. The question is asked only when this Device can use a shared folder; otherwise changes travel as files. On the desktop, Share with… saves the offer as a file to send.

    The person who gets the offer opens the file with Old Brain Fix. Their Agenda shows "1 shared Area waiting" until they decide, also across a restart; its preview says who shares which Area as what, and how changes travel. "Nothing of the Area is kept until you accept." Compare codes marks the sharer verified on Accept. Accept takes the Area in, and asks for the shared folder when changes travel through one and none is chosen yet; Decline keeps nothing; Decline and block keeps nothing and blocks the sharer. Until the person accepts, and also after a decline, they are listed among the Area's people, and you remove them as anyone else.

    Change what a person may do

    Make someone in a Shared Area a Manager or a Member, or remove them.

  • Tap Search and browse on the Agenda.
  • Tap ⋮ next to the Shared Area, then People.
  • Tap ⋮ next to the person.
  • Tap Make Manager, Make Member or Remove.
    •
  • Right-click the Shared Area in the left pane.
  • Click People.
  • Click Make Manager, Make Member or Remove.
    •

    Roles are chosen and changed in Advanced. In Simple a person joins as a Member, and the roles of the people show without Make Manager or Make Member.

    • Manager: everything in the Area.
    • Member: adds Notes and changes their own, adds to any Note, checks off Occurrences and Frogs, and adds and checks Lines of any Checklist or Shopping List. A Member does not change Routines, Reminders, Frogs, other people's Notes or the Lines of their Checklists and Shopping Lists.

    The first to share an Area is its first Manager. People lists everyone in it with their role. A Manager has Make Manager or Make Member, and Remove, for everyone else: nothing more of the Area goes to a person removed, and what they already have stays with them.

    A Note's History shows who changed what in it, in a Shared Area too (see See what changed in a Note).

    Hand a Note to another app

    Pass a Note's text, photos and recordings to a messenger, mail or another program.

  • Tap ⋮ on the Note's card.
  • Tap Share to an app.
  • Pick the app.
    •
  • Double-click the Note's card.
  • Click Copy as text, or Save as Markdown….
  • Paste the text, or open the file, in the other program.
    •

    A Note's ⋮ on its card, and the bar of the Note editor, hold two ways to hand a Note on. Share to an app hands the Note to another app, such as a messenger or mail: its text and its Sections, each under its date, and the photos and recordings this Device holds, as files. Nothing of it is encrypted, and the other side needs no Old Brain Fix. Send to a person sends it to another person with Old Brain Fix (see Send a Note to a person).

    A single photo goes out on its own: a tap on it, in a card's detailed view or in the Note editor, shows it full screen, and its ⋮ holds Save, which puts it into the phone's Pictures in the album "Old Brain Fix", Copy, which puts the picture on the clipboard, and Share, which hands it to the app you pick. Save needs Android 10 or later. Saved and copied say nothing; when one of them fails, an announcement at the top says so, and its tap tells why and what to do. A photo whose file is not on this phone yet has no ⋮.

    On the desktop, Copy as text and Save as Markdown… in the Note editor hand a Note to other programs. The Markdown file keeps plain text as written, escaped where Markdown would read it otherwise, and its photos and recordings go into a folder of their own beside it, named as the file, so the files of two Notes never mix.

    Send a Note to a person

    Send a Note with its Routines and Reminders to someone with Old Brain Fix.

  • Tap ⋮ on the Note's card.
  • Tap Send to a person.
  • Pick who gets it.
  • Tap Send and pick the messenger.
    •
  • Double-click the Note's card.
  • Click Send….
  • Pick who gets it, then click Send.
  • Choose where to save the file, then send it.
    •

    Send to a person, on a Note's ⋮ and in the Note editor's bar, sends the Note as a Sent Item to another person with Old Brain Fix, outside any Shared Area. "Who gets it?" offers each person whose Device's keys are known here, "{name}, encrypted", and Anyone, not encrypted: whoever gets that file can read it. "It carries the text, the Sections and the Routines and Reminders; no photos, recordings or where it was captured." Send hands the file to the app you pick. Sending the same Note again sends it as an update; Send a cancellation instead lets them move it to their trash, with its alarms off.

    Take in a Note someone sent you

    Look at a Sent Item before anything of it is kept.

  • Tap the file in the messenger to open it.
  • Check who sent it and what it holds.
  • Tap Accept, or Decline.
    •
  • Drop the file onto the window.
  • Check who sent it and what it holds.
  • Click Accept, or Decline.
    •

    A Sent Item opened with Old Brain Fix shows its preview: who sent it, whether they are verified ("Verified: code compared in person", "Not verified: known here, code not compared yet" or "Not verified: this Profile is new here", each with the code), whether it came encrypted, and its text, Sections, Routines and Reminders. Accept adds it to Unsorted with its alarms on, and the sender to People; Decline keeps nothing; Decline and block keeps nothing and blocks the sender. On the desktop, a Sent Item dropped onto the window, or picked under Import bundle…, is opened too.

    A Checklist comes with its Lines as the sender sees them. A Shopping List comes with its Lines, each with its reappear time ("Reappear time: 2 weeks", or "None") and all open, as none has been checked yet on your side; a Line the sender set to Once and checked stays with them. Updates add, rename and remove the sender's Lines; the Lines you added, your checks and the reappear times you set stay. A Device with an earlier version, such as 0.3.1, reads a reappear time it does not know, such as 3 days, 2 months or None, as one week.

    An update shows "What changes", such as a Routine moved from one time to another, and Apply update. Your own Sections, Routines and Reminders, Done marks, Importance and where you filed the Note stay as they are; a text you changed yourself is replaced. A cancellation offers Move to the trash, from where the Note can be restored. Only the sender can update or cancel what they sent.

    A Sent Item from someone not among your People is held: the Agenda shows "1 Sent Item waiting", also across a restart, and its preview says only who it is from, "An unknown person: not among your People." "What they sent stays hidden until you tap Show." Accept, Decline and Decline and block work before Show too; Show is not remembered, so the item is hidden again the next time it opens.

    Give someone copies of your Notes

    Send copies of an Area, a Topic or a Note, to keep as their own.

  • Tap ⋮ beside an Area, a Topic or on a Note's card.
  • Tap Export as Pack….
  • Untick what should stay with you.
  • Tap Share… and pick the messenger.
    •
  • Use the phone: the desktop makes no Packs yet.
    •

    A Pack is a file of copies, for another person with Old Brain Fix. They keep the copies as their own: nothing links them back to yours, and you can't update or cancel them. To do things together, share an Area instead; to send one Note you may change later, send it to a person.

    The tree shows only what you started from: a Note, a Topic's Notes, or an Area's Notes and its Topics. Each Note brings what it is: its text, Amends, kind, the Lines of a list unticked, a Tally's fields, a Session's Tallies, its Routines and an open Frog. It never brings Tally entries, ticks, check-offs, where and when it was captured, or its History. Notes other people wrote in a Shared Area are offered too. Only the names of the Areas and Topics go along.

    Everything starts ticked but Reminders, photos and recordings; Photo Texts and Transcripts go without their files. Ticking a Session ticks the Tallies it picks from, also those of other Areas, which show under it; a Session whose Tallies are all unticked is unticked too. Tick all and Untick all are on the bar, and the size above the tree follows your ticks. A locked Area asks to be unlocked first.

    The Pack is not encrypted: whoever gets the file can read it. Add my Profile, off at first, puts your name and picture into it and signs it, so the receiver can tell it is from you and that nobody changed it.

  • Untick what you don't want.
  • Tap Add.

Add puts the copies into new Areas and Topics, never into one you have: where an Area of that name exists, the new one is called "Health (Pack)". Routines start on the day you add them, and a Reminder already past is left out. Opening the same Pack twice adds the copies twice.

    Sync with another Device of yours

    Pair your own Devices once; from then on they hold the same Notes.

  • Tap your Profile picture on the Agenda.
  • Tap Sync with another Device.
  • Tap Scan the code and scan the other Device's code.
  • Tap Let it join on the other Device.
    •
  • Click Sync ▸ Pair a Device.
  • Scan the code shown with the phone's Sync page.
  • Click Trust when the computer asks about the phone.
    •

    On the Profile page, under Your Devices, Sync with another Device opens Sync. Devices of your own pair once by code; both then hold the same Notes, Areas and Routines. The settings of this Device stay on it and are never synced; the settings of your person, Simple or Advanced and "Move a Note to the Trash when its one-time Reminder is done", are the same on all of your own Devices and never reach anyone else.

    Both Devices are on the same Wi-Fi. Either one shows its code and the other scans or types it, in both directions, also from phone to phone. Show this Device's code shows a QR code with the code to type under it, for as long as the page is open. On the other Device, Scan the code, or type it into Pair code and tap Sync now.

    The Device showing the code asks "Let {name} join?": "It gets everything on this Device and syncs with it from then on. Say yes only to a Device of your own." Let it join pairs them. A code that points outside your home network is refused. When both Devices had a Profile of their own, the app asks "Which one is you?"; the one you pick is kept on all of your Devices.

    "Paired Devices on the same Wi-Fi sync on their own, while the app is open and every quarter of an hour in the background." Under Paired Devices, Sync lists each one with when it last synced, Forget; a Device that can be found on the network also has the switch Sync automatically, on by default. Another person's Device, met by sharing an Area in person, shows as "Another person's Device" and gets only the Areas shared with them.

    While this Device syncs, through the network or the shared folder, a thin line moves back and forth under the top bar; the app stays usable meanwhile. With "remove animations" on the phone the line stands still, and a screen reader says "Syncing…" once each time the app comes into view.

    On the desktop, Sync now and Pair a Device in the Sync menu show this computer's code, as a QR code and as text with its PIN, while their dialog is open; a phone that is not paired yet is asked about first, Trust or Refuse. In Pair a Device a phone's code is typed into Pair code and joined with Sync with this code. "Paired Devices on the same network sync on their own for as long as this app runs, also in the tray." While one syncs, the thin line moves under the menu bar. Only on Linux without a tray does closing the window end the app (see The desktop app).

    Let an AI work on your Notes

    Have an AI model of your choice do one task on your Notes, such as fixing typos.

  • Pair the phone with the computer.
  • Run the AI Task in the desktop app.
  • Sync the phone once the review is done.
    •
  • Click Open the AI port in Settings.
  • Click Copy client config and paste it into the AI client.
  • Give the AI its task.
  • Click Done, review.
  • Click Accept all, or Reject all.
    •

    The AI Task runs only in the desktop app; the phone gets its accepted changes by sync. On the desktop, Settings, Open the AI port opens a port reachable only with its token, and shows its address with ways to copy what a client needs: Copy client config for MCP clients that connect over HTTP, Copy the command for Claude Code and Copy the config for Claude Desktop. While the port is open and until the review, sync is paused, and a banner says so.

    The AI reads your Notes with what kind each is, its parts and its Boxes, the Areas and Topics, the Routines and Reminders and this Device's settings. It can change the text of Notes and Sections, where Notes are filed, Merges, the trash, Importance, captured details, Areas, Topics, Routines and Reminders. It can also set up Tallies (their fields with unit, target and rule), Sessions (pool, count and how it picks), Checklists and Shopping Lists (their Lines) and Frogs, and turn them back into plain Notes. What you recorded it only reads, each with its date and for a range of days if asked: a Tally's entries, the ticks of Lines and Boxes, check-offs, eaten Frogs and a Session's picks, so it can answer how often you ran in October. It never adds an entry, ticks, checks off, picks or eats a Frog, and it never sends, shares or changes a setting.

    Above the button, Who may connect sets who reaches the port the next time it opens, kept on this computer; the port itself stays closed at every start. This computer only, the default, lets in programs on this computer through 127.0.0.1. Docker containers on this computer lets in an AI client running in a container, which connects as host.docker.internal; the address and everything copied then use http://host.docker.internal:<port>/mcp. Every container on the Docker bridge can reach the port while it is open, and only its token keeps them out. On Linux the port listens on the Docker bridge (docker0) alone, and the container must be started with --add-host=host.docker.internal:host-gateway; without a Docker bridge the card says so and the port does not open. With Docker Desktop on macOS and Windows the port stays on 127.0.0.1, where Docker Desktop forwards the name.

    Done, review closes the port and opens "Review the AI Task": every change, grouped by kind, such as Notes filed, Texts changed, Tallies changed, Checklists and Shopping Lists changed, Frogs changed and Sessions changed, each with its before and after. "Nothing of this is kept until you accept it." Accept all keeps every change; Reject all asks once and keeps none. Where you changed something yourself after the AI did, your edit stays, marked "Your edit stays". One AI Task runs at a time. An AI Task waiting for its review waits, also across a restart, until you accept or reject it.
