Step-by-step guides to Old Brain Fix on the phone and on the desktop. The pictures show the app with example data, in the site's light or dark theme.

  • Getting started: The first start: a new start with the Starter Set, a Device of your own added, or a Backup restored.
  • Capture and sort: Capturing a Note as text, voice or photo, and sorting it right away or after the Nudge.
  • Routines, Reminders and the Agenda: Setting up Routines and Reminders, Re-nag and Escalation, what is Missed, and the Agenda's week with the appointments of your calendar.
  • Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Frogs: Checklists and Shopping Lists, Tallies with their entries and charts, Sessions that pick Tallies, and Frogs to eat one a day.
  • Formatted text, Transcripts and Photo Texts: Formatting a Note's text, status marks and #tags, tables, Transcripts with the speech models, Photo Texts, and the search.

The Agenda

The Agenda is the start screen: today's Occurrences, the Frogs to eat one of, and the rest of the week dimmed below, with the count of Unsorted Notes on top.
