A Note can become a Checklist, a Shopping List, a Tally or a Session, and it can spawn a Frog besides Routines and Reminders. A Note is one of these kinds at a time, or a Plain Note. Each box below shows the steps on the phone and on the desktop.

The desktop app does the same with the mouse and the keyboard. The "+" at the top of a list opens a menu of what it makes; a Note opens in the Note dialog over the window, where the Lines are typed as on the phone; Show on the Agenda is three radio buttons in a list card's menu; and Frogs sits on the left. The details of each box say where the desktop differs.

    Change a Note's kind

    Turn a Note into a Checklist, a Shopping List, a Tally or a Session, or back.

  • Long-press the Note's card.
  • Tap the Kind badge.
  • Tap the kind it should be.
    •
  • Double-click the Note's card.
  • Click the Kind badge.
  • Click the kind it should be.
    •

    Every Note has one kind: a Plain Note, a Checklist, a Shopping List, a Tally or a Session. A Plain Note is its text. For the other kinds the Note's first line is its name, shown as a heading at the top of its card and editor; the rest of its text is its description, small under the name and folded to one line until a tap unfolds it. The Lines, the Tally or the Session come right after it, then the badges, photos and recordings, and the Amends at the end. In the editor the name and the description are two fields; saving writes them back as one text, and a Note opened and left unchanged is not written.

    • The kind badge in the Note editor shows the kind. A tap offers the kinds the Note can become: a Plain Note becomes a Checklist, a Shopping List, a Tally or a Session, the last two only in Advanced; a Checklist becomes a Shopping List or a Plain Note; a Shopping List becomes a Checklist or a Plain Note; a Session becomes a Plain Note. A Tally stays a Tally.
    • Made a Session with the kind badge, a Note gets the pool, the count and the rule but no Routine yet: it runs once a Routine is added, and until then its compact card shows how many it picks, such as "Picks 3 Tallies".
    • A Note whose text is Code becomes no other kind; its badge only shows it until the text format is changed back (see Formatted text, Transcripts and Photo Texts).
    • A Checklist that becomes a Shopping List keeps its Lines in their order, the checked ones struck, groups flattened; a Shopping List that becomes a Checklist keeps the Lines that show, the struck ones checked. The text stays as it is. The old Lines stay in the log and no longer show.

    On the desktop the Note dialog shows a Note of a kind as the phone's editor does: its name, its description folded under it, then the Lines or the setup of the Tally or the Session. Its kind badge changes the kind, as on the phone; its Session opens a dialog with the pool, the count, the counts per weekday and the rule, without a schedule. The card's menu, also opened with a right-click on the card, offers Make it a Checklist and Make it a Shopping List on a plain Note, and the Note menu of the menu bar offers them for the Note open in the dialog, with Make it a Tally in Advanced.

    Keep a Checklist

    Check off Lines, such as a packing list, and use it again.

  • Tap ⋮ ▸ Make it a Checklist on a Note's card.
  • Long-press the card to type or change Lines.
  • Tap a Line's box to check it.
  • Tap Uncheck all to use it again.
    •
  • Click ⋮ ▸ Make it a Checklist on a Note's card.
  • Double-click the card to type or change Lines.
  • Click a Line's box to check it.
  • Click Uncheck all to use it again.
    •

    A Checklist is a Note whose Lines are checked off. Make it a Checklist is in the ⋮ of the Note's card, and the kind badge in the Note's editor offers it. The Note's first line stays its text, as its name; every other line that is not blank becomes a Line. A line that starts with a space or a tab goes under the Line above, and one that starts with "✓ " is checked. A Note made a Checklist leaves Unsorted.

    • In the detailed view every Line has its checkbox; a checked Line is struck through in place. The compact view shows "3 of 5 open".
    • A Line can have a group of Lines under it, two levels at most. Checking every Line of a group checks the Line above it, checking that Line checks its whole group, and unchecking one Line of the group unchecks it again. A group shows in a frame with the Line it is under.
    • In the editor the Lines are edited as one block of text, every Line starting with its box; a new list starts with one empty Line. Return adds a new Line with an empty box under the cursor's Line; in the middle of a Line it moves the rest of the Line into the new one. Backspace at the start of a Line joins it with the Line above, which keeps its box and whether it is checked; the joined Line's checks stay in the log. An empty Line simply goes. Put under the Line above and Back to the top level on the bar above the keyboard move the Line with the cursor into a group and out.
    • Everything typed is kept on leaving the editor; Lines left empty are dropped. ⋮ on a Line offers Move up, Move down, Put under the Line above or Back to the top level, and Delete. On the phone Delete asks nothing: the announcement at the top, "Line deleted", brings the Line back with a tap, and the folded "Removed Lines" under the Lines restores each deleted Line with Restore. The desktop asks nothing either: Ctrl+Z or Edit ▸ Undo brings the Line back, and the Note dialog has the same "Removed Lines".
    • Uncheck all shows while a Line is checked, to use the list again. Back to a plain Note, in the ⋮ of the card, and Plain Note behind the editor's badge "Checklist" turn it back: every Line becomes a line of the Note's text, a checked one marked "✓ ".
    • In a Shared Area anyone with a role adds and checks Lines; renaming, moving and deleting a Line stay with the author of the Note or of the Line and the Managers.

    A Checklist with a Routine: a Routine or Reminder spawned from a Checklist starts each Occurrence with every Line unchecked, on all of your own Devices and for everyone in a Shared Area alike. Its row on the Agenda lists the Lines indented under its title, each with its box, checked as for that Occurrence, and they can be checked there. The Occurrence is Done once every Line is checked for it, by anyone, or when its box is checked off. The Note's card and editor show the Lines as checked for the Occurrence it is on now. Where "Hide what's checked on the Agenda" is switched on, its checked Lines leave the Agenda row at once (see Routines, Reminders and the Agenda).

    On the desktop the Lines are typed in place as on the phone, as one block of text: Enter adds a Line below or moves the rest of a Line into it, Backspace at its start joins it with the Line above, Tab and Shift+Tab move it into a group and out, in place of the buttons above the keyboard, and Up and Down move to the Line above and below. A Line's menu opens with its ⋮.

    Show a list on the Agenda

    Choose how a Checklist or a Shopping List shows on today's Agenda.

  • Long-press the list's card.
  • Tap the Show on the Agenda badge.
  • Tap one of the three choices.
    •
  • Double-click the list's card.
  • Click the Show on the Agenda badge.
  • Click one of the three choices.
    •

    Show on the Agenda is in the ⋮ of the list's card too. Its three choices:

    • Off: not at all.
    • One row with the number of open Lines, such as "Weekly shopping: 4 open" or "Before the trip: 3 of 7 open". This is how every Shopping List starts.
    • Each open Line on its own row: the list's name as a heading and its open Lines indented under it, each with its box, which checks it there; a Line checked today stays struck through for the day, and its box unchecks it. This is how every Checklist starts, but for one imported from Google Keep, which starts Off.

    A list without open Lines does not show, whatever the choice, until a Line comes back, is unchecked or is added; only on the day its last Lines were checked it shows them, struck through, as each open Line on its own row. The rows come after today's all-day Occurrences, the Shopping Lists before the Checklists. A tap on the heading opens the list with the cursor in a new empty Line after the open ones, the keyboard up; left unused, that Line is dropped. A tap on a Line's words opens the list as it is. A Checklist with a Routine shows as its Occurrence and has no choice. A Routine or Reminder spawned from a Shopping List is Done only when checked off. A Line checked there stays on the Agenda for the day, struck through, a Shopping List's under the open Lines, also one checked Once; where "Hide what's checked on the Agenda" is on, it leaves at once.

    The choice is yours alone: it goes to your own Devices, and in a Shared Area each person decides for their own Agenda. A Shopping List nobody chose for starts as the Device's earlier setting for all Shopping Lists showed them: Off where it said "Not at all", each open Line where it said "Each Line that came back today". A list of a locked Area set to Masked shows only its heading, "Private Shopping List" or "Private Checklist" with its counts.

    On the desktop a list shows on today's Agenda as on the phone, its Lines checked today struck through, and a click on a Line's words opens it in the Note dialog; a click on its heading opens it there with the keyboard in a new empty Line after the open ones. Show on the Agenda is three radio buttons in the card's menu, before Uncheck all on a Checklist and before Back to a plain Note on a Shopping List, and a badge in the Note dialog whose dialog offers the same three; a click on one closes it.

    Keep a Shopping List

    Check off what you bought; it comes back when it is due again.

  • Tap ⋮ ▸ Make it a Shopping List on a Note's card.
  • Tap a Line's box once it is bought.
  • Long-press the card, then tap a Line's Reappear time badge.
  • Tap After and pick how long.
    •
  • Click ⋮ ▸ Make it a Shopping List on a Note's card.
  • Click a Line's box once it is bought.
  • Double-click the card, then click a Line's Reappear time badge.
  • Click After and pick how long.
    •

    A Shopping List is a Note whose Lines come back a while after they are checked, such as the weekly shopping. Make it a Shopping List is in the ⋮ of the Note's card, and the kind badge in the Note's editor offers it. As for a Checklist, the first line stays the name and every other line becomes a Line; Shopping Lists have no groups, and a line marked "✓ " is struck.

    • Checking a Line strikes it through and moves it to the end, showing the day it comes back, such as "back Sat, Oct 31". At the start of that day it is open again, in its place.
    • Each Line has its Reappear time on a badge at its end: "—" for none, "Once", or a number with a unit, such as "4w" or "3m". The badge is on the Lines in the Note editor, not on the card. A tap on it opens it: None, Once or After, with a number from 1 to 99 and day, week, month or year, 1 week at first; each choice is kept at once. A new Line has none: once checked it stays struck until a time is set, which then counts from its check. A Line comes back as many days or weeks on, or on the same day of the month as many months or years on (on the last day of a shorter month), by the Device's clock. A Line Once is gone when checked.
    • In the editor the Lines are edited as on a Checklist: one block of text, every Line starting with its box, Return for a new Line, Backspace at the start to join a Line with the one above. The Lines stay in the order they were typed in, also the next time. While the editor is open a Line checked there stays in its row; the next time it shows below the open ones.
    • A name the list already has adds nothing, whatever its case and spaces around it: a struck or gone Line of that name comes back at once, and an open one stays.
    • A Line left empty is dropped on leaving. ⋮ on a Line offers Delete; on the phone it asks nothing and is undone as on a Checklist, with "Removed Lines" under the Lines; on the desktop Ctrl+Z or Edit ▸ Undo brings it back.
    • In the detailed view the open Lines show with their checkboxes; the struck ones are folded under them as "2 struck through", which a tap shows and hides again; the phone keeps them shown until they are folded again. The compact view shows "3 open". Back to a plain Note, in the ⋮ of the card, and Plain Note behind the editor's badge "Shopping List" write the Lines into the text, the struck ones marked "✓ ".

    Capturing into a Shopping List: Add to a Shopping List in the ⋮ of the capture screen makes each line of the capture a Line of the list picked, and keeps no Note. With one list to add to, it reads Add to Weekly and picks that list at once; with several, the list used last is at the top. A recording gives the lines of its Transcript. When it is offered is described in Capture and sort. On the desktop it is in the More menu of the quick note's action row, with the lists to pick, the one used last at the top, or with one list the entry naming it, once Settings under "Quick note" switch on "Add to a Shopping List" and while a Shopping List exists.

    On the desktop the Reappear time's dialog stays open after a choice; Close, Esc or a click beside it closes it. Its number is set with − and +.

    Count what you do with a Tally

    Record how much of something you did, such as push-ups or pages read.

  • Tap + in an Area, then Tally.
  • Type its Name and the name of a field.
  • Tap the field's badges to set its unit and target.
  • Tap Add field for another one.
  • Tap Save.
    •
  • Click + beside an Area's name, then Tally.
  • Type its Name and the name of a field.
  • Click the field's badges to set its unit and target.
  • Click Add field for another one.
  • Click Save.
    •

    A Tally is a Note that records how much of something was done, such as push-ups, pages read or minutes of video. Tallies are made in Advanced. The New Tally page holds its Name, then a row per field, one at first, and Add field for the next. A field's row is its name and its badges: its kind, a number or a Check field; how several sets or entries in a bar count, Sum or Largest, shown as an icon; its unit; and its target. A Check field has no unit and no rule. A tap on a badge opens its small editor. Save makes the Tally with every field that has a name; rows left without one are dropped; Save asks for a Name, at least one field with a name and targets that are numbers. Make it a Tally in the ⋮ of a Note's card, or of the capture screen, opens the page of its first field instead; Save makes the Note a Tally, and it leaves Unsorted.

    A field has a Name, a Unit, an optional Target per bar of the chart, prefilled in every new entry, and how "Several sets or entries in a bar count": Their sum or The largest, which follows the kind of unit until it is picked. The unit is typed into one field. Under it, the known units of time, distance, mass, speed, temperature and angle that match what is typed show as suggestions, by their symbol or their name in English or German: "k" offers kg, km and km/h, "min" offers min. A tap on a suggestion picks that known unit; anything else typed, such as "pages", stays a unit of your own, and a field left empty has no unit. Once entries hold values for a field, its kind of unit stays, and only units of that kind are suggested. Settings under "Units" choose whether this Device shows the known units in metric or imperial, or as the phone's region does; the values are stored in metric either way. Hide field takes a field out of view; its values stay stored and come back with Show field again.

    A new field's Kind of field is Number or Check field. A Check field is a box in each entry; a tick counts as 1, so its chart shows how often it was done, such as one Tally per room, Bathroom and Kitchen, for a cleaning Session to pick. Its kind stays once it is added.

    The Note's editor holds the setup. Under "Fields" first two badges of the Tally itself: One bar per Day, Week, Month or Year, the length of a bar of its charts, a day at first; its targets count per bar, so a target of 2 on a Tally of months is 2 a month. And Counts in sets, off at first: switched on, each entry holds several sets, such as 60 kg × 10 and then 65 kg × 8, and each field totals the sets of an entry by its rule, reps by their sum, a weight by the largest. Both are kept with the Tally, on all your Devices and for everyone in a Shared Area. Then each field's name with its rule, unit and target as badges, the rule as its icon, the unit and target as text, such as "pages" and "50 pages", a Check field its kind and target, and "Hidden" while it is hidden. A tap on the name or a badge opens the field's page; Add field opens the page of a new one. The editor shows no chart and no entries.

    A Tally whose field multiplied another one in an earlier version, such as 3 Rounds of 20 Push-ups, counts in sets: that entry shows as three sets of 20 push-ups. The field that multiplied says "Counts the sets of older entries" and shows no more in the entry form.

    On the desktop the New Tally page is a dialog over the window, with Save and Cancel at its top, and each field's rule, unit and target are badges whose click opens their small editor. Make it a Tally is not in a card's ⋮ there: it is a button at the bottom of the Note dialog, in the Note menu, behind the kind badge, and in the quick note's More menu. The suggestions under a field's unit are small buttons, picked with a click; the pointer resting on one shows its kind of unit, such as Mass.

    Record what you did in a Tally

    Add an entry and see it in the Tally's chart.

  • Tap Add entry on the Tally's card.
  • Type the values and tick the boxes.
  • Tap Add set for each further set.
  • Tap Save.
    •
  • Click Add entry on the Tally's card.
  • Type the values and tick the boxes.
  • Click Add set for each further set.
  • Click Save.
    •

    The Tally shows on its card in the detailed view: under its name Add entry, then one chart per field shown, a bar per the Tally's "One bar per", and the entries, newest first. The latest five show; the older ones are folded under how many they are, such as "12 older entries", which a tap shows and hides again, kept per Tally on this Device. On the phone Add entry is also in the ⋮ of the Tally's card.

    • An entry holds a box per Check field, ticked once per entry above the sets; a value per number field, prefilled with the targets, so saving the targets is one tap, and a field without a target with its value in your latest entry that has one, selected when the field takes the cursor, so typing replaces it; the Date it counts for, today for a new one; and one Line of text. A value left empty is none. Numbers are typed like 12, 2.5 or 1:30. Leaving the entry, by Back on the phone, by Esc or a click beside it on the desktop, saves it as Save does once something changed; with a value that is no number, or a new entry left empty, it asks "Discard changes?" first. An entry left as it opened, targets and all, is not saved; on the desktop Cancel saves nothing either.
    • In a Tally that counts in sets the values come as "Set 1", "Set 2" and so on, a row across the number fields each. Add set adds a set that starts with the values of the set before; a set after the first can be removed. The entry shows each set on a line of its own, such as "1. Weight 60 kg · Reps 10".
    • Each chart shows slim bars of a fixed width with gaps, the newest on the right and as many as fit the width, the target as a thin line, the days the bars cover and the highest bar's value. A single bar stays slim. Older values are in the entries; the chart does not scroll sideways.
    • The compact card shows each field's total of the current bar against its target, today's for a Tally of days.
    • A tap on an entry opens it to change; a long press offers Edit and Delete. On the phone Delete asks nothing: the announcement at the top, "Entry deleted", brings it back with a tap; on the desktop Ctrl+Z or Edit ▸ Undo does. Either way the entry can be restored under the folded "Removed entries".
    • In a Shared Area, "Show entries" chooses Mine, Everyone's or one person's; your own show at first.

    On the desktop a Tally's card shows the same in its detailed view as on the phone; its buttons, radio buttons and entries take their own click, a click beside them selects the card. A click on an entry opens it; a right-click offers Edit and Delete. The card's ⋮ has no Add entry. The entry's Date has buttons for the day before and the day after.

    Let a Session pick Tallies

    Let a Session pick a few Tallies each time, such as exercises.

  • Tap + in an Area, then Session.
  • Type its Name.
  • Tick the Tallies of its Pool.
  • Tap the badges to set how many, how and when.
  • Tap Save.
    •
  • Click + beside an Area's name, then Session.
  • Type its Name.
  • Tick the Tallies of its Pool.
  • Set how many it picks, how, and when.
  • Click Save.
    •

    A Session is a Note that picks a few Tallies from its pool for each Occurrence of its Routines. Sessions are made in Advanced. The Note holds what it picks from and how; each of its Routines only says when it runs, such as Monday and Wednesday at 7:00 and Saturday at 10:00.

    The New Session page holds its Name; its Pool, a checkbox per Tally, ending in New Tally, which opens the Tally page and comes back with the new Tally ticked; then three badges: how many it picks each time, set with − and +, how it picks, and its schedule, every day, all day, at first, which opens the Routine editor's schedule. Save makes the Session's Note with its first Routine; it asks for a Name and at least one Tally. Its weekdays' counts and the order for Rolling are set later on its Note's page. A Tally made with New Tally goes to the top of the pool, ticked.

    The Note editor's kind badge makes a Plain Note a Session, starting with the pool, and a Session a Plain Note again; its Routines then run as plain ones, and what was picked and recorded stays. On a plain Note, Session under the alarm clock on its card or the capture screen, or behind the "+" at the end of the Note editor's badges, makes the Note a Session with its first Routine; on a list or a Tally the "+" behind Session makes a plain Routine; New Session on the Agenda's bar and on the bar of All Routines and Reminders makes one whose Note is the text typed in its editor. These open the Routine editor with the Session on top of the schedule: the pool, with handles to drag for Rolling's order, the count with − and +, the folded "Count per weekday" with a switch per weekday, and the rule as radio buttons. With no Tally yet, that pool says how to make one.

    A Session's page in the Note editor holds, after its name and description:

    • Pool: a checkbox per Tally it may pick, ending in New Tally. While the Note lies in a Shared Area, only that Area's Tallies are offered. It keeps at least one.
    • When its Tallies carry #tags in their text, such as #sport or #cleaning, the tags show above the list, those most Tallies carry first; picking one or more shows only the Tallies with any of them, and picking a tag again takes it back. Ticked Tallies the filter hides stay ticked, and a line under the list says how many. New Tally then starts the new Tally's name with the picked tags. The filter is not saved; it lasts while the page is open. The New Session page and the Routine editor that makes a new Session filter their pool the same way; the editor of a Session's Routine has no pool, as the Note holds it. While the list is filtered, Rolling's order cannot be changed.
    • How many it picks each time: 1 to 99, set with − and +.
    • Among the badges, How it picks: Random, a new random pick each time; Rolling, through the pool in its order, continuing where it stopped; or Least selected, the Tallies with the fewest entries first. Rolling goes on from one Routine's run to the next.
    • Among the badges, Count per weekday: each weekday picks as many as every day until it gets a count of its own.
    • Each of its Routines and Reminders as a badge. A tap opens its editor, which holds its schedule, Re-nag and Escalation; the "+" at the end of the badges adds another Routine or Reminder, which runs the same Session, or in Advanced a Frog.

    The detailed view of a Session's card shows its pool, today's picks of each of its Routines, to be recorded there as on the Agenda, how often it was done and skipped in each of the last four weeks, and its latest five past runs, newest first: the date and time of each and each picked Tally, done or not done, such as "Push-ups: done". Its page in the Note editor lists all of its past runs under its badges. Each Routine has its own Streak and Adherence.

    On the Agenda: today's row of each Routine of a Session lists its picks indented under its title, in the order picked, each with its box and each Tally's name with its targets, such as "Push-ups 20 times"; the box is checked and the name struck through once it has an entry. A tap on a pick or its box opens its entry form, prefilled as any new entry; saving records it for this Occurrence. A tap on a pick that has its entry opens that entry to change. Each pick without an entry offers Another one while the pool holds a Tally not picked. The Occurrence is Done once every pick has an entry, by anyone; removing an entry undoes that. The box of the Session's title only shows this: it checks itself, and a tap on it does nothing. Tomorrow and the days after show only how many it picks, such as "Picks 3 Tallies": the picks are made on the day. They are the same on all of your own Devices and for everyone in a Shared Area. Where "Hide what's checked on the Agenda" is on, a pick with its entry leaves the Agenda at once, and the Session's heading once every pick is done. A Session made with an earlier version reads as a Session Note with its pool and its picks.

    On the desktop the New Session page is the Routine editor's page for a Session, as a dialog over the window: its name, its pool with New Tally at its end, which opens the New Tally page over it and comes back with the new Tally ticked, How many it picks each time with − and +, the folded Count per weekday, so the weekdays' counts are set right there, How it picks as radio buttons, and its schedule, every day, all day, at first, with Cancel and Save at the bottom. The "+" badge of the Note dialog holds Routine, Reminder, and in Advanced Frog and Session; on a Session it adds a Routine or Reminder that runs it. The Note menu of the menu bar offers the same. A Session's pool is ordered for Rolling with arrow buttons instead of handles, and a weekday gets its own count with a checkbox instead of a switch.

    Eat a frog

    Get one put-off job done each day, without a time.

  • Tap the alarm clock on a Note's card, then Frog.
  • Check one Frog under Eat a frog on the Agenda.
  • Tap Another one to swap a Frog.
  • Tap Search and browse, then Frogs, for all of them.
    •
  • Double-click the Note's card.
  • Click + at the end of its badges, then Frog.
  • Check one Frog under Eat a frog on the Agenda.
  • Click Frogs on the left for all of them.
    •

    A Frog is a Note's job with no time, done once. Frogs are made in Advanced. Frog under the alarm clock on a Note's card or the capture screen, or behind the "+" at the end of the Note editor's badges, makes one at once; a Note has one open Frog at a time. A Frog never rings and is never Missed; something that has to ring is a Reminder.

    Under today's Occurrences, the Agenda's section "Eat a frog" offers two open Frogs a day, and eating one is enough. Another one swaps a Frog for the next. Once one is checked off, only that one shows for the day, struck through; unchecked, the others show again. Where "Hide what's checked on the Agenda" is on, the section goes once today's Frog is eaten. The pick is the same on all of your own Devices. A tap on a Frog opens its Note; the heading opens the Frog view, and so does Frogs on the bar of the Areas list, between All Notes and the Trash, which stays in Simple too.

    The Frog view lists the open Frogs, oldest first, each with its box, and below them the folded "Done" section with those checked off, the latest first, each with its date ("Done on …"); its box unchecks it. A long press offers Delete; the Frog goes to the Trash and its Note stays. On the phone it asks nothing, and the announcement at the top, "Frog moved to the Trash", brings it back with a tap; on the desktop Ctrl+Z or Edit ▸ Undo does. In a Shared Area a Manager makes and deletes Frogs, and anyone with a role checks them off; a Frog one person eats is eaten for everyone.

    The Note editor shows a Note's Frog as a Frog badge with the frog: Open while it is not eaten, then Eaten today or Eaten yesterday, after which the badge goes; it shows in Simple too. A tap offers Eat now, which checks it off, while it is open, and Remove the Frog, which moves it to the Trash as Delete does; its Note stays. In a Shared Area anyone with a role may eat it and a Manager may remove it. On the desktop the badge sits in the Note dialog and a click opens the same menu.

    On the desktop Frogs on the left, below All Notes, and View ▸ Frogs open the Frog view as well; a right-click on a Frog there offers Delete. The cards have no alarm clock: a Frog is made in the Note dialog with the "+" at the end of its badges, which offers Frog while the Note has no open Frog, or with Note ▸ Frog.
