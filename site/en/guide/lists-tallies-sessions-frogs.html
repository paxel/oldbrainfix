A Note can become a Checklist, a Shopping List, a Tally or a Session, and it can spawn a Frog besides Routines and Reminders. A Note is one of these kinds at a time, or a Plain Note.

Making one from the notes browser

The "+" of an Area, a Topic and All Notes fans out upward from the button, each an icon with its word: Note, Checklist, Shopping List, Tally, Session and Frog, and in an Area Topic. Session, Frog and Topic show only for who may set them up, in a Shared Area the Managers. What is made is filed into the Area or Topic shown; made from All Notes it is filed into none. While the fan is open, "+" reads as close; a second tap on it, a tap outside or Back closes it.

  • Note opens the capture screen, as the Agenda's "+" does.
  • Checklist and Shopping List ask for the list's name; Create opens the Note's editor with the cursor in its first Line, to type the Lines in place.
  • Frog asks for its name only and makes the Frog with its Note.
  • Tally opens one page: its Name, then a row per field, one at first, and Add field for the next. A field's row is its name and three badges: how a day with several entries counts, Sum or Largest, shown as an icon, its unit and its target. A tap on a badge opens its small editor; the unit is typed with the known units it may mean as suggestions, and the rule follows the kind of unit until it is picked. Save makes the Tally with every field that has a name; rows left without one are dropped.
  • Session opens one page: its Name; its Pool, a checkbox per Tally, ending in New Tally, which opens the Tally page and comes back with the new Tally ticked; then three badges: how many it picks each time, set with − and +, how it picks, and its schedule, every day, all day, at first, which opens the Routine editor's schedule. Save makes the Session's Note with its first Routine; its weekdays' counts and the order for Rolling are set later on its Note's page.

Leaving a name dialog with Cancel, or the Tally or Session page without Save, makes nothing; a Tally made with New Tally stays.

A Note's kind

Every Note has one kind: a Plain Note, a Checklist, a Shopping List or a Tally. A Plain Note is its text. For the other kinds the Note's first line is its name, shown as a heading at the top of its card and editor; the rest of its text is its description, small under the name and folded to one line until a tap unfolds it. The Lines or the Tally come right after it, then the badges, photos and recordings, and the Amends at the end. In the editor the name and the description are two fields; saving writes them back as one text, and a Note opened and left unchanged is not written.

In a list every card shows its Note in its compact view or its detailed view. The detailed view, the default, shows the Note whole; the compact view shows a glance by its kind: a Plain Note its first line and the two lines after it, a Checklist its name and "3 of 7 open", a Shopping List its name and "4 open", a Tally its name and each field's total today against its target, such as "Pages 30 / 50 pages", in a Shared Area of your own entries, and a Session its name, the next run of any of its Routines and "done 4 / skipped 1 this week" over all of them. The compact view has no Capture Context and none of the buttons at the bottom of the card; its ⋮ sits top right and offers File into… and Merge into… after Edit. The button on the list's bar shows every card compact or detailed. A tap on a card switches only that card; the phone keeps it so for that list until the button on the bar is used again. A long press still opens the editor.

  • The kind badge in the Note editor shows the kind. A tap offers the kinds the Note can become: a Plain Note becomes a Checklist, a Shopping List or a Tally; a Checklist becomes a Shopping List or a Plain Note; a Shopping List becomes a Checklist or a Plain Note. A Tally stays a Tally.
  • A Checklist that becomes a Shopping List keeps its Lines in their order, the checked ones struck, groups flattened; a Shopping List that becomes a Checklist keeps the Lines that show, the struck ones checked. The text stays as it is. The old Lines stay in the log and no longer show.

Checklists

A Checklist is a Note whose Lines are checked off, such as a packing list. Make it a Checklist is in the ⋮ of the Note's card, and the kind badge in the Note's editor offers it. The Note's first line stays its text, as its name; every other line that is not blank becomes a Line. A line that starts with a space or a tab goes under the Line above, and one that starts with "✓ " is checked. A Note made a Checklist leaves Unsorted.

  • In the detailed view every Line has its checkbox; a checked Line is struck through in place. The compact view shows "3 of 5 open".
  • A Line can have a group of Lines under it, two levels at most. Checking every Line of a group checks the Line above it, checking that Line checks its whole group, and unchecking one Line of the group unchecks it again.
  • A group shows in a frame with the Line it is under.
  • In the editor the Lines are edited as one block of text, every Line starting with its box; a new list starts with one empty Line. Return adds a new Line with an empty box under the cursor's Line; in the middle of a Line it moves the rest of the Line into the new one. Backspace at the start of a Line joins it with the Line above, which keeps its box and whether it is checked; the joined Line's checks stay in the log. An empty Line simply goes. Put under the Line above and Back to the top level on the bar above the keyboard, Tab and Shift+Tab on the desktop, move the Line with the cursor into a group and out.
  • Everything typed is kept on leaving the editor; Lines left empty are dropped. ⋮ on a Line offers Move up, Move down, Put under the Line above or Back to the top level, and Delete.
  • Uncheck all shows while a Line is checked, to use the list again. Back to a plain Note, in the ⋮ of the card, and Plain Note behind the editor's badge "Checklist" turn it back: every Line becomes a line of the Note's text, a checked one marked "✓ ".

In a Shared Area anyone with a role adds and checks Lines; renaming, moving and deleting a Line stay with the author of the Note or of the Line and the Managers.

Checklists on the Agenda

Today's Agenda shows each Checklist without a Routine or Reminder while it has open Lines, after the Shopping Lists: its name as a heading and its open Lines indented under it, each with its box, which checks it there. A tap on the heading or on a Line's words opens the list. Once every Line is checked the list is gone, until a Line is unchecked or added. Show on the Agenda in the ⋮ of the Checklist's card, and as a badge in its editor, takes it off the Agenda and puts it back. Every Checklist starts with it on. The choice is yours alone: it goes to your own Devices, and in a Shared Area each person decides for their own Agenda. A Checklist of a locked Area set to Masked shows only its heading, "Private Checklist" with its counts.

A Checklist with a Routine

A Routine or Reminder spawned from a Checklist starts each Occurrence with every Line unchecked, on all of your own Devices and for everyone in a Shared Area alike. Its row on the Agenda lists the Lines indented under its title, each with its box, checked as for that Occurrence, and they can be checked there. The Occurrence is Done once every Line is checked for it, by anyone, or when its box is checked off. The Note's card and editor show the Lines as checked for the Occurrence it is on now.

Shopping Lists

A Shopping List is a Note whose Lines come back a while after they are checked, such as the weekly shopping. Make it a Shopping List is in the ⋮ of the Note's card, and the kind badge in the Note's editor offers it. As for a Checklist, the first line stays the name and every other line becomes a Line; Shopping Lists have no groups, and a line marked "✓ " is struck.

  • Checking a Line strikes it through and moves it to the end, showing the day it comes back, such as "back Sat, Oct 31". At the start of that day it is open again, in its place.
  • Each Line has its Reappear time: Once, or 1, 2, 3 or 4 weeks; a new Line has 1 week. It comes back on the same weekday as many weeks on, by the Device's clock. A Line Once is gone when checked.
  • In the editor the Lines are edited as on a Checklist: one block of text, every Line starting with its box, Return for a new Line, Backspace at the start to join a Line with the one above. The Lines stay in the order they were typed in, also the next time. While the editor is open a Line checked there stays in its row; the next time it shows below the open ones.
  • A name the list already has adds nothing, whatever its case and spaces around it: a struck or gone Line of that name comes back at once, and an open one stays.
  • A Line left empty is dropped on leaving. ⋮ on a Line offers the reappear time as a choice and Delete.
  • In the detailed view the open Lines show with their checkboxes; the struck ones are folded under them as "2 struck through", which a tap shows and hides again; the phone keeps them shown until they are folded again. The compact view shows "3 open". Back to a plain Note, in the ⋮ of the card, and Plain Note behind the editor's badge "Shopping List" write the Lines into the text, the struck ones marked "✓ ".

Shopping Lists on the Agenda

Show on the Agenda in the ⋮ of the Shopping List's card, and as a badge in its editor, chooses how today's Agenda shows the list:

  • Off: not at all.
  • One row with the number of open Lines, such as "Weekly shopping: 4 open". This is the default.
  • Each open Line on its own row: the list's name as a heading and its open Lines indented under it, each with its box, checkable there.

A list without open Lines does not show, whatever the choice, until a Line comes back or is added. The rows come after today's all-day Occurrences; a tap opens the list. The choice is yours alone: it goes to your own Devices, and in a Shared Area each person decides for their own Agenda. A list nobody chose for starts as the Device's earlier setting for all Shopping Lists showed them: Off where it said "Not at all", each open Line where it said "Each Line that came back today". A list of a locked Area set to Masked shows only a heading, "Private Shopping List" with its count. A Routine or Reminder spawned from a Shopping List is Done only when checked off.

Capturing into a Shopping List

Add to a Shopping List in the ⋮ of the capture screen makes each line of the capture a Line of the list picked, and keeps no Note. A recording gives the lines of its Transcript. When it is offered is described in Capture and sort.

Tallies

A Tally is a Note that records how much of something was done, such as push-ups, pages read or minutes of video. Make it a Tally in the ⋮ of the Note's card, or of the capture screen, opens the page of its first field; Save makes the Note a Tally, and it leaves Unsorted.

Fields

A field has a Name, a Unit, an optional Target, prefilled in every new entry, and how "A day with several entries counts": Their sum or The largest, which follows the kind of unit until it is picked. The unit is typed into one field. Under it, the known units of time, distance, mass, speed, temperature and angle that match what is typed show as suggestions, by their symbol or their name in English or German: "k" offers kg, km and km/h, "min" offers min. A tap on a suggestion picks that known unit; anything else typed, such as "pages", stays a unit of your own, and a field left empty has no unit. Once entries hold values for a field, its kind of unit stays, and only units of that kind are suggested. Settings under "Units" choose whether this Device shows the known units in metric or imperial, or as the phone's region does; the values are stored in metric either way. Hide field takes a field out of view; its values stay stored and come back with Show field again.

Multiplies makes a field the factor of another field of the same Tally, such as Rounds of Push-ups. In each entry the other field then counts as the two values multiplied: 3 rounds of 10 push-ups count as 30 in the totals, the targets and the charts, and the entry shows "Push-ups 3 × 10 times". An entry without a value for the multiplier counts the other field as it is. The multiplier counts no value of its own: it has no total, no chart and no target, and its page shows neither. This applies to older entries too. A field multiplies one other field shown, never itself, one that multiplies, or while another field multiplies it; hiding the field it multiplies makes it a field of its own again. Nothing does so too.

The Note's editor holds the setup: under "Fields" each field's name with its rule, unit and target as badges, the rule as its icon, the unit and target as text, such as "pages" and "50 pages", what it multiplies while it does or may, such as "Push-ups" or "Nothing", a multiplier without its rule and target, and "Hidden" while it is hidden. A tap on the name or a badge opens the field's page; Add field opens the page of a new one. The editor shows no chart and no entries.

Entries and charts

The Tally shows on its card in the detailed view: under its name Add entry, then "One bar per" Day, Week, Month or Year, one chart per field shown, and the entries, newest first. The latest five show; the older ones are folded under how many they are, such as "12 older entries", which a tap shows and hides again. The length of a bar and the older entries shown are kept per Tally on this Device. Add entry is also in the ⋮ of the Tally's card.

  • An entry holds a value per field, prefilled with the targets, so saving the targets is one tap; the Date it counts for, today for a new one; and one Line of text. A value left empty is none. Numbers are typed like 12, 2.5 or 1:30.
  • Each chart shows a bar per day, week, month or year, as many as fit the width, the target as a line, the days the bars cover and the highest bar's value.
  • A tap on an entry opens it to change; a long press offers Edit and Delete. Delete asks once; the entry can be restored under the folded "Removed entries".
  • In a Shared Area, "Show entries" chooses Mine, Everyone's or one person's; your own show at first.

Sessions

A Session is a Note that picks a few Tallies from its pool for each Occurrence of its Routines. The Note holds what it picks from and how; each of its Routines only says when it runs, such as Monday and Wednesday at 7:00 and Saturday at 10:00. The Note editor's kind badge makes a Plain Note a Session, starting with the pool, and a Session a Plain Note again; its Routines then run as plain ones, and what was picked and recorded stays. Session under the alarm clock on a Note's card or the capture screen, or behind the "+" at the end of the Note editor's badges, makes the Note a Session with its first Routine; New Session on the bar of All Routines and Reminders makes one whose Note is the text typed in its editor.

A Session's page in the Note editor holds, after its name and description:

  • Pool: a checkbox per Tally it may pick, ending in New Tally. While the Note lies in a Shared Area, only that Area's Tallies are offered. With no Tally yet, the pool says how to make one; it keeps at least one.
  • How many it picks each time: 1 to 99, set with − and +.
  • Among the badges, How it picks: Random, a new random pick each time; Rolling, through the pool in its order, continuing where it stopped; or Least selected, the Tallies with the fewest entries first. Rolling goes on from one Routine's run to the next.
  • Among the badges, Count per weekday: each weekday picks as many as every day until it gets a count of its own.
  • Each of its Routines and Reminders as a badge. A tap opens its editor, which holds its schedule, Re-nag and Escalation; the "+" at the end of the badges adds another Routine or Reminder, which runs the same Session.

The detailed view of a Session's card shows its pool, today's picks of each of its Routines, to be recorded there as on the Agenda, and how often it was done and skipped in each of the last four weeks. Each Routine has its own Streak and Adherence.

Today's row of each Routine of a Session on the Agenda lists its picks indented under its title, in the order picked, each with its box and each Tally's name with its targets, such as "Push-ups 20 times"; the box is checked and the name struck through once it has an entry. A tap on a pick or its box opens its entry form, prefilled with the targets; saving records it for this Occurrence. Each pick without an entry offers Another one while the pool holds a Tally not picked. The Occurrence is Done once every pick has an entry, by anyone; removing an entry undoes that. The box of the Session's title only shows this: it checks itself, and a tap on it does nothing. Tomorrow and the days after show only how many it picks, such as "Picks 3 Tallies": the picks are made on the day. They are the same on all of your own Devices and for everyone in a Shared Area. A Session made with an earlier version reads as a Session Note with its pool and its picks.

Frogs

A Frog is a Note's job with no time, done once. Frog under the alarm clock on a Note's card or the capture screen, or behind the "+" at the end of the Note editor's badges, makes one at once; a Note has one open Frog at a time. A Frog never rings and is never Missed; something that has to ring is a Reminder.

Under today's Occurrences, the Agenda's section "Eat a frog" offers two open Frogs a day, and eating one is enough. Another one swaps a Frog for the next. Once one is checked off, only that one shows for the day, struck through; unchecked, the others show again. The pick is the same on all of your own Devices. A tap on a Frog opens its Note; the heading opens the Frog view, and so does Frogs on the bar of the Areas list, between All Notes and the Trash.

The Frog view lists the open Frogs, oldest first, each with its box, and below them the folded "Done" section with those checked off, the latest first, each with its date ("Done on …"); its box unchecks it. A long press offers Delete, which asks once; the Frog goes to the Trash and its Note stays. In a Shared Area a Manager makes and deletes Frogs, and anyone with a role checks them off; a Frog one person eats is eaten for everyone.

On the desktop

  • A card's view button, at its top right beside ⋮ and the pencil, switches that card between its compact and its detailed view, as a tap does on the phone; a click on the card only selects it. Compact views and Detailed views in the View menu do what the button on the list's bar does.
  • The Note dialog shows a Note of a kind as the phone's editor does: its name, its description folded under it, then the Lines or the setup of the Tally or the Session. Its kind badge changes the kind, as on the phone, and the card's menu offers the lists as well. The "+" badge of the Note dialog holds Routine, Reminder, Frog and Session; on a Session it adds a Routine or Reminder that runs it.
  • A Checklist shows on today's Agenda as on the phone, and a click on its row opens it in the Note dialog. Show on the Agenda is a checkbox in the card's menu, before Uncheck all, and a badge in the Note dialog.
  • A Shopping List shows on today's Agenda as on the phone, and a click on its row opens it in the Note dialog. Its Show on the Agenda is three radio buttons in the card's menu, before Back to a plain Note, and a badge in the Note dialog whose dialog offers the same three; a click on one closes it.
  • The "+" at the top of All Notes, an Area or a Topic opens a menu with Note, Checklist, Shopping List, Tally, Session and Frog, Session and Frog for who may set them up. The New Tally page and the New Session page are dialogs over the window. On the New Tally page each field's rule, unit and target are badges whose click opens their small editor; the unit is typed with the known units as small buttons under it. The New Session page is the Routine editor's page for a Session: its name, its pool with New Tally at its end, which opens the New Tally page over it and comes back with the new Tally ticked, its count and rule, and its schedule, every day, all day, at first.
  • The Lines of a Checklist and a Shopping List are typed in place as on the phone, as one block of text: Enter adds a Line below or moves the rest of a Line into it, Backspace at its start joins it with the Line above, and on a Checklist Tab and Shift+Tab move it into a group and out, in place of the buttons above the keyboard; Up and Down move to the Line above and below.
  • The menus of Lines and entries open with ⋮, and with a right-click where the Line is not typed in.
  • A Tally's card shows the same in its detailed view as on the phone; its buttons, radio buttons and entries take their own click, a click beside them selects the card.
  • The suggestions under a field's unit are small buttons, picked with a click; the pointer resting on one shows its kind of unit, such as Mass.
  • A Session's pool is ordered for Rolling with arrow buttons instead of handles, and a weekday gets its own count with a checkbox instead of a switch.
  • The quick note's action row offers the same as the phone's, Add to a Shopping List among them.
  • Frogs on the left, below Unsorted, opens the Frog view as well.
