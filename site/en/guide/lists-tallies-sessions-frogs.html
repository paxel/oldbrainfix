A Note can become a Checklist, a Shopping List or a Tally, and it can spawn a Session or a Frog besides Routines and Reminders. A Note is at most one of a Tally, a Checklist and a Shopping List.

Making one from the notes browser

The "+" of an Area, a Topic and All Notes fans out upward from the button, each an icon with its word: Note, Checklist, Shopping List, Tally, Session and Frog, and in an Area Topic. Session, Frog and Topic show only for who may set them up, in a Shared Area the Managers. What is made is filed into the Area or Topic shown; made from All Notes it is filed into none. While the fan is open, "+" reads as close; a second tap on it, a tap outside or Back closes it.

  • Note opens the capture screen, as the Agenda's "+" does.
  • Checklist and Shopping List ask for the list's name; Create opens the Note's editor with the cursor in its first Line, to type the Lines in place.
  • Frog asks for its name only and makes the Frog with its Note.
  • Tally opens one page: its Name, then a row per field, one at first, and Add field for the next. A field's row is its name and three badges: how a day with several entries counts ("Σ Sum" or "Largest"), its unit and its target. A tap on a badge opens its small editor; the unit is typed with the known units it may mean as suggestions, and the rule follows the kind of unit until it is picked. Save makes the Tally with every field that has a name; rows left without one are dropped.
  • Session opens one page: its Name; its Pool, a checkbox per Tally, ending in New Tally, which opens the Tally page and comes back with the new Tally ticked; then three badges: how many it picks each time, set with − and +, how it picks, and its schedule, every day, all day, at first, which opens the Routine editor's schedule. Save makes the Session; its weekdays' counts and the order for Rolling are set later in its editor.

Leaving a name dialog with Cancel, or the Tally or Session page without Save, makes nothing; a Tally made with New Tally stays.

Checklists

A Checklist is a Note whose Lines are checked off, such as a packing list. Make it a Checklist is in the ⋮ of the Note's card and in the Note's editor. The Note's first line stays its text, as title; every other line that is not blank becomes a Line. A line that starts with a space or a tab goes under the Line above, and one that starts with "✓ " is checked. A Note made a Checklist leaves Unsorted.

  • On a full card every Line has its checkbox; a checked Line is struck through in place. A compact card shows "2 of 5 checked".
  • A Line can have a group of Lines under it, two levels at most. Checking every Line of a group checks the Line above it, checking that Line checks its whole group, and unchecking one Line of the group unchecks it again.
  • A group shows in a frame with the Line it is under.
  • In the editor the Lines are edited as one block of text, every Line starting with its box; a new list starts with one empty Line. Return adds a new Line with an empty box under the cursor's Line; in the middle of a Line it moves the rest of the Line into the new one. Backspace at the start of a Line joins it with the Line above, which keeps its box and whether it is checked; the joined Line's checks stay in the log. An empty Line simply goes. Put under the Line above and Back to the top level on the bar above the keyboard move the Line with the cursor into a group and out.
  • Everything typed is kept on leaving the editor; Lines left empty are dropped. ⋮ on a Line offers Move up, Move down, Put under the Line above or Back to the top level, and Delete.
  • Uncheck all shows while a Line is checked, to use the list again. Back to a plain Note, in the ⋮ of the card and, in the editor, behind the badge "Checklist", turns it back: every Line becomes a line of the Note's text, a checked one marked "✓ ".

In a Shared Area anyone with a role adds and checks Lines; renaming, moving and deleting a Line stay with the author of the Note or of the Line and the Managers.

Checklists on the Agenda

Today's Agenda shows each Checklist without a Routine or Reminder as one row while it has open Lines, such as "Before the trip: 3 of 7 open", after the Shopping Lists; a tap opens the list. Once every Line is checked the row is gone, until a Line is unchecked or added. Show on the Agenda in the ⋮ of the Checklist's card, and as a badge in its editor, takes it off the Agenda and puts it back. Every Checklist starts with it on. The choice is yours alone: it goes to your own Devices, and in a Shared Area each person decides for their own Agenda. A Checklist of a locked Area set to Masked shows as "Private Checklist" with its counts.

A Checklist with a Routine

A Routine or Reminder spawned from a Checklist starts each Occurrence with every Line unchecked, on all of your own Devices and for everyone in a Shared Area alike. Its row on the Agenda lists the Lines under its title, checked as for that Occurrence, and they can be checked there. The Occurrence is Done once every Line is checked for it, by anyone, or when its box is checked off. The Note's card and editor show the Lines as checked for the Occurrence it is on now.

Shopping Lists

A Shopping List is a Note whose Lines come back a while after they are checked, such as the weekly shopping. Make it a Shopping List is in the ⋮ of the Note's card and in the Note's editor. As for a Checklist, the first line stays the title and every other line becomes a Line; Shopping Lists have no groups, and a line marked "✓ " is struck.

  • Checking a Line strikes it through and moves it to the end, showing the day it comes back, such as "back Sat, Oct 31". At the start of that day it is open again, in its place.
  • Each Line has its Reappear time: Once, or 1, 2, 3 or 4 weeks; a new Line has 1 week. It comes back on the same weekday as many weeks on, by the Device's clock. A Line Once is gone when checked.
  • In the editor the Lines are edited as on a Checklist: one block of text, every Line starting with its box, Return for a new Line, Backspace at the start to join a Line with the one above. The Lines stay in the order they were typed in, also the next time. While the editor is open a Line checked there stays in its row; the next time it shows below the open ones.
  • A name the list already has adds nothing, whatever its case and spaces around it: a struck or gone Line of that name comes back at once, and an open one stays.
  • A Line left empty is dropped on leaving. ⋮ on a Line offers the reappear time as a choice and Delete.
  • On a full card the Lines show with their checkboxes, open ones first; a compact card shows "3 open". Back to a plain Note, in the ⋮ of the card and behind the editor's badge "Shopping List", writes the Lines into the text, the struck ones marked "✓ ".

Shopping Lists on the Agenda

Settings under "Shopping Lists on the Agenda" choose how today's Agenda shows them, on this Device only:

  • Each Line that came back today, on its own row, checkable there.
  • One row per Shopping List on a day when Lines came back, such as "Weekly shopping: 3 back". This is the default.
  • One row per Shopping List on every day it has open Lines, such as "Weekly shopping: 4 open".
  • Not at all.

The rows come after today's all-day Occurrences; a tap opens the list. A Routine or Reminder spawned from a Shopping List is Done only when checked off.

Capturing into a Shopping List

Add to a Shopping List in the ⋮ of the capture screen makes each line of the capture a Line of the list picked, and keeps no Note. A recording gives the lines of its Transcript. When it is offered is described in Capture and sort.

Tallies

A Tally is a Note that records how much of something was done, such as push-ups, pages read or minutes of video. Make it a Tally in the ⋮ of the Note's card, or of the capture screen, opens the page of its first field; Save makes the Note a Tally, and it leaves Unsorted.

Fields

A field has a Name, a Unit, an optional Target, prefilled in every new entry, and how "A day with several entries counts": Their sum or The largest, which follows the kind of unit until it is picked. The unit is typed into one field. Under it, the known units of time, distance, mass, speed, temperature and angle that match what is typed show as suggestions, by their symbol or their name in English or German: "k" offers kg, km and km/h, "min" offers min. A tap on a suggestion picks that known unit; anything else typed, such as "pages", stays a unit of your own, and a field left empty has no unit. Once entries hold values for a field, its kind of unit stays, and only units of that kind are suggested. Settings under "Units" choose whether this Device shows the known units in metric or imperial, or as the phone's region does; the values are stored in metric either way. Hide field takes a field out of view; its values stay stored and come back with Show field again.

Entries and charts

The Tally shows in the Note's editor: its fields, Add entry and Add field, then "One bar per" Day, Week, Month or Year, one chart per field shown, and the entries, newest first. Add entry is also in the ⋮ of the Tally's card.

  • An entry holds a value per field, prefilled with the targets, so saving the targets is one tap; the Date it counts for, today for a new one; and one Line of text. A value left empty is none. Numbers are typed like 12, 2.5 or 1:30.
  • Each chart shows a bar per day, week, month or year, as many as fit the width, the target as a line, the days the bars cover and the highest bar's value.
  • A tap on an entry opens it to change; a long press offers Edit and Delete. Delete asks once; the entry can be restored under the folded "Removed entries".
  • In a Shared Area, "Show entries" chooses Mine, Everyone's or one person's; your own show at first.

Sessions

A Session is a Routine that picks a few Tallies from its pool for each Occurrence. It is spawned as a Routine is, with Session under the alarm clock, "Routine, Reminder, Frog or Session", on a Note's card or the capture screen, or behind the "+" at the end of the Note editor's badges. New Session on the bar of All Routines and Reminders makes one whose Note is the text typed in its editor. A Session has a Routine's schedule, Re-nag, Escalation, Streak and Adherence. On top of the schedule, its editor holds:

  • Pool: a checkbox per Tally it may pick. While the Session's Note lies in a Shared Area, only that Area's Tallies are offered. For Rolling, a handle on each Tally drags it to its place in the order. With no Tally yet, the pool says how to make one; the Session is saved once the pool holds one.
  • How many it picks each time: 1 to 99, set with − and +.
  • Count per weekday, folded: each weekday shows "As every day" until its switch gives it a count of its own, starting from every day's and set with − and +; switched off again, it picks as many as every day.
  • How it picks: Random, a new random pick each time; Rolling, through the pool in its order, continuing where it stopped; or Least selected, the Tallies with the fewest entries first.

Today's row of a Session on the Agenda lists its picks under its title, in the order picked, each Tally's name with its targets, such as "Push-ups 20 times", struck through once it has an entry. A tap on a pick opens its entry form, prefilled with the targets; saving records it for this Occurrence. Each pick without an entry offers Another one while the pool holds a Tally not picked. The Occurrence is Done once every pick has an entry, by anyone, or when its box is checked off; removing an entry undoes that. Tomorrow and the days after show only how many it picks, such as "Picks 3 Tallies": the picks are made on the day. They are the same on all of your own Devices and for everyone in a Shared Area.

Frogs

A Frog is a Note's job with no time, done once. Frog under the alarm clock on a Note's card or the capture screen, or behind the "+" at the end of the Note editor's badges, makes one at once; a Note has one open Frog at a time. A Frog never rings and is never Missed; something that has to ring is a Reminder.

Under today's Occurrences, the Agenda's section "Eat a frog" offers two open Frogs a day, and eating one is enough. Another one swaps a Frog for the next. Once one is checked off, only that one shows for the day, struck through; unchecked, the others show again. The pick is the same on all of your own Devices. A tap on a Frog opens its Note; the heading opens the Frog view, and so does Frogs on the bar of the Areas list, between All Notes and the Trash.

The Frog view lists the open Frogs, oldest first, each with its box, and below them the folded "Done" section with those checked off, the latest first, each with its date ("Done on …"); its box unchecks it. A long press offers Delete, which asks once; the Frog goes to the Trash and its Note stays. In a Shared Area a Manager makes and deletes Frogs, and anyone with a role checks them off; a Frog one person eats is eaten for everyone.

On the desktop

  • Make it a Checklist, Make it a Shopping List and Make it a Tally are in the Note dialog, and the card's menu offers the lists as well. The "+" badge of the Note dialog holds Routine, Reminder, Frog and Session; the kind's badge holds Back to a plain Note.
  • A Checklist shows on today's Agenda as on the phone, and a click on its row opens it in the Note dialog. Show on the Agenda is a checkbox in the card's menu, before Uncheck all, and a badge in the Note dialog.
  • The "+" at the top of All Notes, an Area or a Topic opens a menu with Note, Checklist, Shopping List, Tally, Session and Frog, Session and Frog for who may set them up. The New Tally page and the New Session page are dialogs over the window. On the New Tally page each field's rule, unit and target are badges whose click opens their small editor; the unit is typed with the known units as small buttons under it. The New Session page is the Routine editor's page for a Session: its name, its pool with New Tally at its end, which opens the New Tally page over it and comes back with the new Tally ticked, its count and rule, and its schedule, every day, all day, at first.
  • A Line is typed in place as on the phone: Enter splits it, Backspace at its start joins it with the Line above, and Tab and Shift+Tab move it into a group and out, in place of the buttons above the keyboard; Up and Down move to the Line above and below. In a Shopping List's field Enter adds the Line, and in one of its Lines it ends the rename.
  • The menus of Lines and entries open with ⋮, and with a right-click where the Line is not typed in. Tally entries show 30 at a time.
  • The suggestions under a field's unit are small buttons, picked with a click; the pointer resting on one shows its kind of unit, such as Mass.
  • A Session's pool is ordered for Rolling with arrow buttons instead of handles, and a weekday gets its own count with a checkbox instead of a switch.
  • The quick note's action row offers the same as the phone's, Add to a Shopping List among them.
  • Frogs on the left, below Unsorted, opens the Frog view as well.
