Old Brain Fix is a note and reminder app for Android, with a desktop app for Linux, Windows and macOS. Notes are captured as text, photo or voice and filed into Areas and Topics. Routines and Reminders ring at the exact minute and repeat until they are marked Done. All data is stored on the user's devices; there is no account and no server.
