# Changelog

All notable changes to Old Brain Fix are documented in this file.
Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/); versioning: [SemVer](https://semver.org/).

## [0.6.0]

### Added

- On the desktop, `oldbrainfix --version` and `oldbrainfix -V` print the installed version, such as "Old Brain Fix 0.6.0", without starting the app.
- On the phone and the desktop, Snooze (10 min, 30 min, 1 h) is also in the Agenda row's ⋮ (on the desktop also its right-click) and beside Done at the top of the Note editor or Note dialog, so an Occurrence can still be snoozed once its notification is gone.
- On the phone and the desktop, Not today in the notification, the Agenda row's ⋮ and the Note editor or Note dialog offers Skip, which leaves an Occurrence neither Done nor Missed and stops its alarm, and Tomorrow, which skips it and adds a one-time Occurrence the day after at the same time, without changing the schedule; on the desktop Ctrl+Z takes either back, and its notification shows Snooze's and Not today's choices in place.
- A skipped Occurrence shows struck through as skipped on the Agenda for the day, never shows in the Missed Summary, and leaves the Adherence, which counts it beside the share, such as "92 % this month · 3 skipped"; a Session's "skipped" now counts these skips instead of its Missed runs.
- On the phone and the desktop, the When line of the Routine editor also reads a one-time Reminder in words relative to now, such as "tomorrow 7 pm", "tonight", "in 2 hours" or "in 3 days" (in German "morgen 19 Uhr", "heute Abend", "in 2 Stunden"), and times such as "7 pm" (in German "19 Uhr"). "Tomorrow" without a time is when this Device's Quiet Hours end, or 09:00 while they are off, and "tonight" is 19:00. On the phone and the desktop a new Reminder also offers the buttons In 1 h, Tonight, Tomorrow and In a week under the When line, each with its time, which set the Reminder with one tap or click.
- On the phone, every Note list (Unsorted, an Area, a Topic, All Notes, the search's hits and the Trash) has Select in its bar, which puts a box on each card, and the Notes ticked are filed with File into…, merged into one Note with Merge into…, given an Importance or moved to the Trash at once, with one undo at the top for all of them, or restored together in the Trash. On the desktop the cards selected with Ctrl-click or Shift-click offer the same in a card's right-click menu and in the Note menu, and Ctrl+Z takes back the trash or the Merge of all of them at once.
- On the desktop, the card lists work with keys: Up and Down move the selection, Shift+Up and Shift+Down extend it, Ctrl+A selects all cards, Enter opens the Note, Delete moves the cards selected to the Trash for Ctrl+Z to bring back, 1, 2 and 3 make them Important, Normal or Unimportant, Ctrl+Shift+M merges them, and Down in the search field goes to the first card found; Unsorted and the Trash have a search field too, which Ctrl+F there reaches instead of going to All Notes, and the Trash's also finds its Routines, Reminders and Frogs by their Note.
- On the desktop, Ctrl+Enter runs the main action of every dialog, such as Save, Create or Send, also in the Routine editor, a Tally entry and the New Tally and Session pages, the first field of a dialog such as a name, the Routine editor or a Tally entry has the cursor as it opens, Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2 and Ctrl+3 in the quick note and the capture popup save with a Nudge in 10 min, 30 min or 1 h, and Shift+Enter in the popup starts a new line while Enter still saves.
- On the desktop, the window opens as large and where it was closed, maximized if it was, with the sidebar as wide and the Note dialog as large as they were left, each kept on this computer only; a window whose monitor is gone opens centred, and the Note dialog is resized by dragging its edges and corners.
- On the desktop, the menu bar has an Area menu with Rename (F2), Colour and icon, Share…, People, Export as Pack… and Delete for the Area or Topic shown, and an AI menu that opens the AI port's card in the Settings and the review of an AI Task that waits, and the sidebar shows how many Notes Unsorted holds, such as "Unsorted 7", and folds an Area's Topics with the chevron at the end of its row, kept on this computer.
- On the phone and the desktop, the Areas list and the sidebar keep your own order of the Areas, which a drag on the handle of an Area's row on the phone, a drag of the Area's row in the sidebar, Move up and Move down in the desktop's Area menu and right-click, and Alt+Up and Alt+Down change, the same on all of your Devices and in File into…, Area Locks, Link to… and Notes for the feedback; the others in a Shared Area keep their own order, and once an Area has been moved, a new Area goes to the end.
- On the phone, a long press on the "+" of the Agenda or the lock screen opens the capture screen for text at once, without the fan, and a screen reader announces it with the button.
- On the phone and the desktop, Settings has a search field at the top that shows only the settings whose name or detail line holds the words typed, under their section's heading, and says so when none does; on the desktop Ctrl+F puts the cursor into it while the Settings are open.
- On the desktop, Alt+Up and Alt+Down move the Checklist or Shopping List Line the cursor is in one place within its group or the top level, as Move up and Move down in its ⋮ do, and a Line is dragged up or down by the handle beside its ⋮, a line showing where it goes; a Shopping List's open Lines move among the open ones, on the phone too by Move up and Move down in their ⋮, and the struck ones stay where they are.
- On the desktop, Record keys beside the Settings' hotkey field takes the next keys pressed together as the hotkey, none of the app's own keys working meanwhile and Esc stopping it, while the field still takes the keys typed out.
- On the phone, Add to home screen in a Tally card's ⋮ puts a shortcut on the home screen that opens the Tally's entry page directly, after the unlock behind the App Lock, and goes back to the home screen once the entry is saved; on the iPhone, Add to the app icon puts the Tally among the actions of the app icon's long press instead.
- On the phone and the desktop, Open a Backup… on the Backups page, on the desktop also in the File menu, opens a Backup file with the passphrase or the recovery key and shows its Notes as a Pack's tree under its date, those from its Trash marked and on the phone those of a closed locked Area only once unlocked, and Add brings the ticked ones back as copies in new Areas without restoring the Backup or changing anything else.
- On the desktop, "Open in its own window" in the Note dialog and a card's menu opens the Note in a window of its own beside the main window, one window per Note, for reading and editing, with "Keep on top" to keep it above all other windows (greyed on Wayland, which does not allow it).

### Changed

- On the phone and the desktop, Sync picks a sync bundle's Device or Everything with radio buttons instead of a dropdown.
- On the phone and the desktop, deleting what can be restored no longer asks first: a Note moved to the Trash, a Routine or Reminder, a Frog, a Tally entry, a Line, the "From …" label, a Transcript or a Photo Text goes at once. On the phone the announcement at the top undoes it with a tap for 10 seconds; on the desktop Ctrl+Z and Edit ▸ Undo undo the last delete, check-off, filing or Merge. A Checklist and a Shopping List show their deleted Lines under the folded "Removed Lines" in the Note editor and the desktop's Note dialog, each with Restore.
- On the phone and the desktop, a tap or click on an Occurrence on the Agenda or on its notification opens its Note in the editor or the desktop's Note dialog, with Done, Snooze and Not today at the top once it is due, instead of the Routine editor or the app as it was; Edit schedule in the row's ⋮ (on the desktop also its right-click menu) and the Routine's badge open the schedule.
- On the phone and the desktop, File into… starts with a filter over the Areas and Topics, shows the last three Areas or Topics this Device filed into at the top, and offers New Area… at the end and New Topic… under each Area (in Advanced, for who may set up a Topic there), which make the Area or Topic and file the Note into it at once.
- On the phone and the desktop, leaving the Routine editor, a Tally entry or the New Tally and New Session pages, by Back on the phone, by Esc, a click beside the dialog or back on the desktop, saves a change that can be saved and asks "Discard changes?" only about one that can't, instead of dropping it.
- On the phone and the desktop, a Line of a Shopping List or Checklist checked on the Agenda stays there struck through for the day, on the phone also in the Agenda widget, and its box unchecks it, while "Hide what's checked on the Agenda" still removes it at once.
- On the phone and the desktop, a tap or click on a Shopping List's or Checklist's heading on the Agenda opens the list with the cursor in a new empty Line after the open ones, on the phone with the keyboard up; left unused, that Line is dropped.
- On the phone and the desktop, Add to a Shopping List on the capture screen or in the quick note's More menu names the list and picks it at once while there is only one, such as "Add to Weekly", and otherwise lists the list used last at the top.
- On the phone and the desktop, a new Tally entry starts each field without a target with its value in your latest entry, selected so that typing replaces it.
- On the phone and the desktop, a compact card's ⋮ on the phone and a card's ⋮ and right-click on the desktop offer Routine… and Reminder…, in Advanced also Frog and Session…, beside File into… and Merge into…; on the phone a card's ⋮ puts Send to a person, Share to an app, Copy text and Export as Pack… under one Share…, and a detailed card's pencil opens the Note editor in one tap.
- On the desktop, back and forward show each place with the search, person filter and scroll position it was left with, instead of clearing them; a place shown anew starts with none and at the top.
- Sync over the local network carries a photo or voice recording of up to 256 MiB, as bundles and Backups do, instead of 64 MiB.

### Fixed

- Sending a Note to a person, on the phone and the desktop, no longer picks the first person in the list: nobody is picked until you choose, and an update starts at the person this Device last sent the Note to, so a private Note does not go to the wrong person.
- On the desktop, the app no longer keeps a processor core busy while it waits in the tray or idles: the line under the menu bar moves at a steady pace and stops after two minutes if a sync hangs, nothing is drawn while the window is hidden, and the AI port answers a tool call when it comes instead of checking ten times a second.
- On the desktop, a start of an updated app ends an older version still running in the tray and runs in its place, instead of opening the older version's window until it was quit.
- On a narrow phone, the days and times in the Missed Summary and the share beside each Adherence figure in a Routine's history wrap onto a second line instead of running off the screen.
