# Changelog

All notable changes to Old Brain Fix are documented in this file.
Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/); versioning: [SemVer](https://semver.org/).

## [0.2.0]

### Added
- On the phone and the desktop, 30 minutes after the first change that people synced by files have not been sent yet, a notification "Changes for Bob and Carol" comes, repeats like the Nudge and waits out the Quiet Hours until each of them was sent their file, and tapping or clicking it offers each person their file in turn, while a file counts as sent, there and with "Send changes to …", only once an app was picked on the phone's share sheet or the file was saved on the desktop.
- On the phone and the desktop, the first start asks whether the user is new to Old Brain Fix, adds a Device of their own or restores a Backup, and "Add a Device of mine" shows this Device's pair code or takes the other Device's, then syncs before any setup, bringing the Profile, Areas and everything else, so that the phone skips the Starter Set and only offers the speech model and the alarm setup, while a failed or cancelled Pairing returns to the first screen.
- On the phone, text Capture has a Gallery button next to Photo and Voice that picks one or more pictures from the phone into the Note as its photos, each with its Photo Text.
- On the phone, an audio file shared in from another app, such as a WhatsApp voice message, becomes a voice Note with its Transcript, several shared at once one Note with a recording each, and a file the phone cannot decode is kept in the Note with an announcement that it could not be transcribed, whose tap shows the reason.
- On the desktop, "From file…" in the capture field and files dropped onto the window make pictures and sound files, such as a WhatsApp voice message in Ogg Opus, m4a, mp3 or WAV, into one Note with their photos and voice recordings, which wait in the capture until it is saved with what is typed, each with its Photo Text or Transcript, a sound file the desktop cannot decode kept in the Note with an announcement, once the Note is saved, whose click shows the reason; voice Notes in Ogg Opus or MP3 from the phone also play and are transcribed on the desktop.
- On the phone and the desktop, a person can be blocked on their Profile page or with "Decline and block" in the preview of a Sent Item from them, which deletes everything they wrote but their check-offs from all of the user's Devices, keeps and shows nothing more of theirs, refuses their Sent Items, leaves the Shared Areas they manage and lists them in a folded "Blocked" section of People, from where Unblock fetches their writing again.
- On the phone, Sync shows the phone's own pair code as a QR with the typed code under it for another phone, a tablet or the desktop to join, Share shows an invite to a Shared Area the same way, and the phone hosts automatic sync for its paired Devices while the app is open and during its background sync, so phones and tablets pair and sync with each other without a computer, and the desktop's Sync view takes such a code typed or pasted in to join a phone or tablet.
- On the phone and the desktop, a person can be added from their Profile card: on the phone "Send my Profile" on the Profile page sends a link through any messenger and "Show my QR" shows it as a QR, "Add a person" in People takes a link pasted, a QR scanned with the camera, which marks the person verified at once, or a QR in a picture, and a card link tapped in another app opens in the app; on the desktop "Copy my Profile link" and "Show my QR" on the Profile page do the same and "Add a person from a link" in People takes a pasted link; a person new to the user is offered "Send yours back" on their Profile page.
- On the phone and the desktop, "Share with…" on the Share page of an Area shares it with a person in People who is not nearby, as Manager or Member, asks once whether changes travel with them through a shared folder or as files, and sends the offer as a file through any messenger, which the desktop saves where the user says; the other phone or desktop, given the file opened or dropped onto the window, shows "Ann shares Household with you as Member" with Accept, Compare codes, Decline and "Decline and block", keeps nothing before Accept, lists undecided offers on the Agenda, and the person's Profile page offers "Send changes to Ann" when changes travel as files.
- On the phone and the desktop, "Replace Note text" and "Append to Note text" under each Transcript and Photo Text in the Note editor, also in a merged Note's Section, take the generated text over into the Note's text, "Remove generated text" removes it after asking once while the recording or photo stays, and "Transcribe again" and "Read the text again" make it anew, also after it was removed.
- On the phone and the desktop, Routines and Reminders ring with a chime of Old Brain Fix's own instead of the notification sound, and "Alarm sound" in Settings picks another sound of the phone or, on the desktop, a sound file that the app plays itself while its notifications stay silent, with the own chime ringing in place of a file that is gone or cannot be played, while Escalation still rings with the system's alarm sound.
- On the desktop, "Open the AI port" in the Settings lets an AI model of the user's choice, local or in the cloud, work on their Notes, Areas, Topics, Routines and Reminders for one task through an MCP server reachable only from this computer and only with its token, with the client config copied for Claude Code, Claude Desktop or another client, while sync pauses and every change waits until "Done, review" lists them by kind with before and after for "Accept all" or "Reject all", a review left open waiting for the next start.
- On the phone and the desktop, "Routine, Reminder, Frog or Session" on a Note's card, or "Frog" in the desktop's Note editor, makes a Frog, a job with no time done once, and the Agenda offers two Frogs a day in an "Eat a frog" section under today's Occurrences, the same on all of the user's own Devices, where "Another one" swaps a Frog, checking one off hides the other for the day, and the heading opens the Frog view with the open Frogs and a folded "Done" section, while a long press, or a right-click on the desktop, deletes a Frog made by mistake and the trash restores it.
- On the phone and the desktop, "Make it a Tally", in a Note's ⋮ on the phone and in its editor on the desktop, gives the Note fields, each a number with a unit from a known list, shown in metric or imperial as set in Settings or by the region, or typed freely, an optional target and whether a day counts the sum or the largest, and "Add entry" records the fields prefilled with their targets, a date and a line of text, which the Note shows as a list of entries with their authors, by default only the user's own in a Shared Area, and as one chart per field by day, week, month or year, while a removed entry or a hidden field can be restored.
- On the phone and the desktop, "Session" in "Routine, Reminder, Frog or Session" on a Note's card, or "Session" in the desktop's Note editor, makes a Routine that picks Tallies from a pool for each Occurrence, as many as its count or a weekday's own count says, by Random, Rolling or Least selected, and the Agenda lists today's picks with their targets under it, the same on all of the user's own Devices and for everyone in a Shared Area, where tapping or clicking a pick records an entry prefilled with its targets, "Another one" swaps a pick, and the Occurrence is Done once every pick has an entry.
- On the phone and the desktop, Share in the ⋮ of an Occurrence on the Agenda, on the desktop also after a right-click, shares its Routine as the whole series with its end, or its Reminder, as a calendar file, which the desktop saves where the user chooses, as it does with Export.
- On the desktop, the All Routines and Reminders page lists every Routine and Reminder alphabetically by name, opens one's editor with a click and writes one calendar file of all of them with Export.
- On the phone, "Show my calendar's appointments on the Agenda" under "Read from your calendar" on the Calendar page asks for calendar access and opens the list of the phone's calendars, none picked at first, and the Agenda then shows the appointments of the calendars picked beside the Occurrences of every day, read only, marked with their calendar's icon, colour and name, without the calendar mirror's own entries and without copying or syncing anything, while a tap shows an appointment's place and description and "Copy into a Note" in its ⋮ or after a long press makes a new Note of its title, time, place and description.
- On the phone, the Note editor and "Add to Note" type in two rows: the whole text on top, where a tap puts the cursor at the nearest word boundary, and above the keyboard one line of the text around the cursor whose ◀ and ▶ buttons move it one character, repeating faster while held, and whose long press starts selecting with those buttons; Settings of this Device can switch it off.
- On the phone and the desktop, "Make it a Checklist" on a Note's card and in its editor turns every line of the Note but the first into a Line with a checkbox, in formatted text without the mark of a bulleted or numbered item or a quote, a line starting with a space or a tab going under the line above into a group drawn as one frame under it, where a checked Line stays in place struck through, checking every Line of a group checks its parent and checking the parent checks the group, the editor adds, renames, moves, indents and deletes Lines, full cards are checkable and compact ones show "4 of 9 checked", "Uncheck all" readies the list for the next use and "Back to a plain Note" writes every Line back into the text with "✓ " before the checked ones, while in a Shared Area every Member checks and adds Lines and only the author and the Managers change the rest, two renames of one Line made apart become a Conflict, Sent Items, Bundles and Backups carry Checklists, and a Routine or Reminder spawned from a Checklist starts each Occurrence with every Line unchecked, on all Devices and for everyone in a Shared Area, and counts the Occurrence Done once every Line is checked, by anyone, while checking it off by hand still works.
- On the phone and the desktop, "Make it a Shopping List" on a Note's card and in its editor turns every line of the Note but the first into a Line with a checkbox and no groups, where checking a Line strikes it through and moves it to the end with the day it comes back, such as "back Thu, Nov 12", which is the same weekday one to four weeks on as chosen under "Reappear time" in the Line's ⋮ beside "Delete", one week for a new Line, and from the start of that day the Line is open again at the top, while a Line set to Once is gone once checked, adding a name the list already has brings its struck or gone Line back at the top instead of adding it twice, making it, "Back to a plain Note", the cards, Shared Areas and Conflicts work as for a Checklist, and Settings of this Device choose how Shopping Lists show under today on the Agenda: each Line that came back today on its own row to check off there, one row per list on a day when Lines came back such as "Groceries: 3 back" (the default), one row per list on every day it has open Lines, or not at all, and "Add to a Shopping List" in the ⋮ of every capture screen makes each line typed, and each line of a voice recording's Transcript once it is there, a Line of the list picked and keeps no Note.
- On the phone and the desktop, a ☐, ✅ or ❌ at the start of a line of a Note's text, also after spaces or a list item's "- ", opens a small menu with Check, Fail and Open when tapped or clicked, the current state left out, and the choice changes the mark in the text, while such a character in the middle of a line stays plain text, the editor puts ☐ at the start of the cursor's line, and in a Shared Area only the Note's author and the Managers change the marks.
- On the phone and the desktop, a word with "#" in front, such as "#Garten", is highlighted in the text of a Note and of its Sections, on cards and in the editor, and tapping or clicking it on a card or in a Section opens the search for it, which finds every Note holding it in any case and not the word without "#", while a number such as "#126", a heading "# Title", "C#" and the "#" in a web address are not highlighted, and nothing new is stored.
- On the phone and the desktop, the text of a Note and of its Sections can be formatted with bold, italic, strike-through, two heading levels, bulleted and numbered lists with one level of indent, quotes, links and inline code, from a format bar that formats the selection or the word at the cursor, by typing Markdown such as "# ", "- ", "1. ", "> ", "**bold**" or "`code`", or by pasting Markdown, while a text stays plain until it is first formatted and is kept as Markdown from then on, cards, Sections, Sent Items and Backups show it formatted, search finds its words and not its marks, a Transcript or Photo Text put into it stays plain text, and a link opens only when it is a web or e-mail address.
- Formatted text can hold tables, drawn to fit the screen and opened full screen with the first row, and the first column when it is marked as headings, kept in view, each cell holding one line of formatting and a ☐, ✅ or ❌ at its start that can be changed, rows and columns put in, moved and deleted from their headings, and Markdown tables that are pasted in or imported become tables.
- On the phone and the desktop, a Note's ⋮ and its editor offer "Send to a person" for a Sent Item and, to hand the Note to another app such as a messenger or mail, "Share to an app" on the phone, which gives the share sheet the Note's text and its Sections, each under its date, with HTML beside the Markdown of formatted text, and its photos and recordings as files, and "Copy as text" and "Save as Markdown…" on the desktop, which put the same text with its HTML on the clipboard or write the Markdown file with its photos and recordings beside it, while Transcripts and Photo Texts go along only for a Note or Section without words of its own.
- On the phone, "Paste" on the text capture screen beside Photo, Voice and Gallery, and as a fifth way in the fan of "+", reads the clipboard only when tapped and fills the capture with its text and with its picture as the Note's photo, both into one Note, and says on the screen when the clipboard holds neither, while on the desktop the capture's Paste button and Ctrl+V in the empty capture window take text and pictures alike.
- On the phone and the desktop, a Routine's or Reminder's schedule, a person's role in a Shared Area, a Profile or an Override that a newer version of Old Brain Fix saved in a form this version does not know is kept as it is, changed by nothing on this Device and shown with "Saved by a newer version of Old Brain Fix. Update the app to change it." in the list of all Routines and Reminders and under its Note, among the people of the Area, on the Profile page and under People.
- On the phone and the desktop, a database that a newer version of Old Brain Fix saved, as after going back to an earlier version, is not opened, and in place of the app only "This Device's data was saved by a newer Old Brain Fix; install that version again." shows, with nothing on the Device changed, on the phone without Capture and on the desktop for the tray, the capture popup and the command line too.
- On the phone and the desktop, a pair code, Sent Item or Backup of a newer version of Old Brain Fix is announced as coming from a newer Old Brain Fix, with "Update Old Brain Fix on this Device" as the fix, instead of as no code, unreadable or no Backup, and the shared folder's keys and Profiles files and this Device's settings that a newer version saved are kept as they are rather than read as missing, with an announcement that an update is needed.

### Changed
- On the phone and the desktop, every capture screen, on the desktop the small capture window and the main window's capture too, shows the Note card's buttons for File into…, Routine, Reminder, Frog, Session, Importance and Make it a Tally above the save buttons, whose choices show on the screen and are saved together with the Note when the screen is left or saved, nothing of them for an empty Note, while a Note filed or set up this way gets one Save instead of the three Nudge buttons and no Nudge, and Stop ends a voice recording and returns to the capture screen with the recording and room to type instead of saving at once.
- On the phone, "+" on the Agenda, also while the app is locked, fans out Text, Voice, Photo, Gallery and Paste instead of opening text Capture at once, and closes again on a second tap, a tap outside or Back.
- On the phone and the desktop, the unit of a Routine's "every N", its Re-nag and Escalation, the Device's Re-nag and Nudge repeat in Settings, and on the phone the App Lock's "Lock again after" are columns of radio buttons instead of dropdowns, with a value set elsewhere kept as an extra option.
- On the phone and the desktop, the app starts faster with a speech model: its checksum is computed once when it is downloaded or picked, and again only when the file's size or modification time changes.
- On the phone and the desktop, restoring a Backup brings back the playback speed of voice recordings chosen on the Device it was made on.
- On the phone and the desktop, the preview of a Sent Item from a person not among the user's People shows only the sender's name and that they are unknown until "Show", while Accept, Decline and "Decline and block" work before "Show" too, and such an item left undecided waits, also through a restart, until it is decided, behind an "x Sent Items waiting" line on the Agenda.
- On the phone and the desktop, the Note lists, that is each Area, each Topic, Unsorted, the search results and a person's Notes, show every Note in full, with all its Sections oldest first, each under its date and its Capture Context, its photos at the card's width with a capped height, opening full screen with zoom on a tap or click, its recordings and its Transcripts and Photo Texts dimmed and labelled under them, while a switch on each list's bar, remembered per list on the Device, shows the Notes compact by their first line, a tap or click unfolds a compact Note, and the editor opens with a long press or Edit in the card's ⋮ on the phone and with a double-click or Edit on the desktop instead of a tap.
- On the phone, the Agenda's bar opens All Routines and Reminders in place of the calendar export, which lists every Routine and Reminder alphabetically by name, also those beyond the Agenda's week, opens one's editor with a tap and writes one calendar file of all of them with Export instead of selecting them to share, and a Calendar page with the sections "Read from your calendar", the running appointment noted at Capture, and "Write to your calendar", the calendar mirror, to which a row in Settings leads too.
- On the phone, a swipe between the Agenda and the browser levels turns the page when let go past a third of the screen instead of half, while a quick flick turns it as before.
- On the phone and the desktop, announcements no longer carry names, counts or error texts but say only that a job finished, failed or waits, with the details behind a tap or click, a failure's tap or click opens a dialog with the cause, what to do and the full text with Close and Report, a code that is no pair code or recovery key is said in red under its field, and a Backup written is shown only by its date on the phone's Backups page and the desktop's Backups card.
- On the phone and the desktop, Photo Text reads German letters (ä, ö, ü, ß) and €, and reads most photos more accurately.

### Fixed
- On the phone and the desktop, the Note editor's trash button asks once before deleting the Note, with the same dialog as the Note card's Delete, including the choice to keep or delete its Routines and Reminders.
- On the phone and the desktop, a voice Note without words is titled "Voice recording" instead of "Photo" wherever only its words fit, such as the Agenda, the home-screen widget, the Missed Summary and alarms.
- On the phone and the desktop, a new Transcript in which Whisper only repeats the words it was given, the German lead-in or the list of Area and Topic names, shows "No words heard." instead, and such a repeat before or after real speech is left out of the Transcript.
- On the phone and the desktop, the German texts call the People list "Menschen", a bundle "Bündel" and a verified sender "Bestätigt", as the German glossary does, instead of "Personen", "Sync-Paket" or "Paket" and "Geprüft", and on the desktop the text under "Start at login" no longer says that the app starts into the tray, which it does only on Linux with a tray.
- On the phone, the notices before the location, calendar, Nearby devices and microphone permissions no longer say that what they note stays on the user's devices and is never sent anywhere, but that it goes only where its Note goes: to the user's own Devices and to the people of a Shared Area the Note is filed in.
- On the phone, the Photo, Voice, Gallery and Paste buttons of the text capture screen are filled in the full teal accent, in the light and the dark theme, instead of a pale tone that hardly stood out from the background.
- On the desktop, voice recordings played faster or slower keep the pitch of the voice, as on the phone.
- On the desktop, an Area lists only its Notes without a Topic, as on the phone; a search in the Area still finds the Notes of its Topics.
- On the desktop, the Agenda shows the whole week, as on the phone: after tomorrow come the five following days, each under its weekday and date, an empty day as "Nothing planned".
- On the desktop, the Note editor shows a photo's Photo Text under the photo, as on the phone, not only after the photo is removed.
- On the desktop, "New to Old Brain Fix" on the first start offers the Starter Set, as on the phone, to pick which Areas and Topics to start with, instead of starting without any.
- On the desktop, a click on the Nudge's notification opens the window on Unsorted instead of only bringing the window up, on Windows and macOS a click on a notification opens the window too, and on Windows the notifications offer Done, the snoozes and "Open Old Brain Fix", as on Linux.
- On the desktop, a picture pasted into the window's capture or dropped onto the window waits in the capture as a "Photo" line and is saved with what is typed on Save or on leaving, as in the capture popup, instead of becoming a Note at once.
- On the desktop, "File into…" without Areas, today on the Agenda with nothing due, the "About Old Brain Fix" page and the Conflicts in Sync say to click and name the desktop's own way instead of telling to tap +.
- On the desktop, a Sent Item that cannot be taken is announced at the top only as not taken, without the error text, and a click on the announcement opens why, what to do and the full text with Close and Report.
