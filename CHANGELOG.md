# Changelog

All notable changes to Old Brain Fix are documented in this file.
Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/); versioning: [SemVer](https://semver.org/).

## [0.3.0]

### Added

- On the phone and the desktop, today's Agenda shows each Checklist without a Routine or Reminder that has open Lines as one row after the Shopping Lists, such as "Before the trip: 3 of 7 open", which opens the list, and "Show on the Agenda" in the ⋮ of its card and in its editor, on for every Checklist, takes it off the Agenda for this person alone, kept on their own Devices, so in a Shared Area each person decides for their own Agenda.

- On the phone, the ⋮ of a photo shown full screen offers Save, which puts it into the phone's Pictures in the album "Old Brain Fix" from Android 10 on, Copy, which puts the picture on the clipboard, and Share, which hands it to the app picked on the share sheet, and on the desktop a right-click on a photo in the Note editor or shown full screen offers "Save image as…", which saves its file where the user says, "Copy image", which puts the picture on the clipboard, and "Open in another program", which opens it in the computer's program for pictures.
- On the phone under Settings ▸ Import and on the desktop with File ▸ Import… ▸ "A folder…", a folder of Markdown and text files becomes Notes, each dated when its file was last changed, in a new Area named after the folder, none of them Nudged.
- On the phone with "Import from Google Keep" under Settings ▸ Import and on the desktop with File ▸ Import… ▸ "Google Keep…", a Google Takeout export of Keep, as its zip file or unpacked folder, makes each of its notes a Note in a new Area "From Google Keep", dated when it was made in Keep, with its labels as #tags, a list as a Checklist with its checked Lines that stays off the Agenda until switched on, and its photos as photos, none of them Nudged and none from Keep's trash.
- On the phone, the "+" of an Area, a Topic and All Notes fans out Note, Checklist, Shopping List, Tally, Session and Frog, in an Area also Topic, each filed into the Area or Topic shown, or into none from All Notes: a Checklist or a Shopping List takes its name and opens with the cursor in its Lines, a Frog takes its name only, a Tally takes one page with its name and its fields, each field's rule, unit and target as badges, and a Session takes one page with its name, its pool, where "New Tally" makes a Tally without leaving the page, and its count, rule and schedule, every day all day at first, as badges; on the desktop the "+" at the end of the list's heading offers the same kinds in a menu, its pages as dialogs over the window.
- On the desktop, View ▸ Theme offers System, which shows the window light or dark as the computer is set and follows its changes, on Linux through the desktop portal, Light and Dark, kept on this Device, and the text in both themes has at least the contrast WCAG AA asks for.
- On the desktop, a click selects a card, Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on macOS) adds a card to the selection or takes it away and Shift-click selects every card from the one clicked last, and the selected cards, marked with a check, move together into an Area or Topic when one of them is dragged onto it in the sidebar or with File into… from a card's right-click, the Note menu or Ctrl+M, whose dialog says how many go, while a card this Device may not move stays where it is and the announcement's details say how many moved and how many stayed.
- The place, the calendar event, the Bluetooth device and the source app captured with a Note or a Section can each be removed on its own, on every Device and for the people of a Shared Area, where the Note's author and the Area's Managers may remove them, while the time it was captured stays, on the phone and the desktop with "Remove" beside each in the Captured badge's dialog and under each Section, and on the desktop the AI port can remove them as a change that waits for the review.
- On the desktop, the AI port's card in the Settings offers "Who may connect", kept on this Device: "This computer only", as before, or "Docker containers on this computer", for which the port listens on Linux only on the Docker bridge and on macOS and Windows on 127.0.0.1, accepts only the bridge's address and host.docker.internal as the request's host, copies its configs and command with http://host.docker.internal:<port>/mcp and, without a Docker bridge, says so and does not open, while in both a request naming any other host is refused.

### Changed

- The quick note, the phone's text capture screen and on the desktop a dialog over the window, which File ▸ New Note, Ctrl+N and "+" open in place of the capture field above the lists, and the capture popup, shows only the text, the buttons that add a recording, a picture or a file, the Nudge buttons and "To full editor", which saves the Note Unsorted with a Nudge in 1 h and opens it in the Note editor, and Settings under "Quick note" switch File into…, Routine, Reminder, Frog, Session or Tally, which adds "Make it a Tally", Add to a Shopping List, the Importance and the format bar on for it, and the Nudge buttons off for a single Save.
- On the phone and the desktop, a Session's editor sets how many it picks each time, 1 to 99, with − and + instead of a typed number, and each weekday shows "As every day" until its switch, on the desktop its checkbox, gives it a count of its own, set the same way.
- On the phone and the desktop, a new Routine or Session starts every day, all day, instead of at 08:00, a new Reminder still at the next full hour, and "Add a time" starts from the current time, on the phone in its picker instead of at 08:00 and on the desktop in the time field, which it adds as is or which is typed over.
- "Send my Profile" and "Send yours back" send the Profile card as a small file with the picture, such as `Ann.obfcard`, instead of a link, on the phone through the app picked and on the desktop saved where the user says in place of "Copy my Profile link", and a card file tapped in a messenger, the Files app or a download on the phone, or dropped onto the desktop's window, picked under "Import bundle…" or given to `oldbrainfix open`, opens in the app and adds the person by the same rules; card links no longer open the app, and the QR and pasted links work as before.
- On the phone and the desktop, the Lines of a Checklist are typed in place in the Note editor, where Return splits a Line or starts a new one, Backspace at the start of a Line joins it with the Line above, and two buttons above the phone's keyboard, or Tab and Shift+Tab on the desktop, move it into a group and out, and a Shopping List's field adds a Line on Return while its Lines are renamed in place; everything typed is kept on leaving, and Lines left empty are dropped.
- On the phone, the field of the Note editor, of Add to Note and of the quick note with the format bar shows a table as the same bordered grid as the card instead of its Markdown, opens it full screen with a tap, and treats it as one block: the cursor passes it whole, text typed right before or after it goes on a line of its own, and deleting it removes the whole table, asking first while its cells hold words.
- On the phone, the home-screen Agenda widget shows everything the Agenda shows for today, in its order: the Missed Summary, the Unsorted count, calendar appointments, Routines and Reminders with a Checklist Routine's Lines and a Session's picks, Shopping List and Checklist rows and "Eat a frog", Masked as on the Agenda, scrolls when taller than the widget, and checks off each row the Agenda can check off, Occurrences, Lines, Frogs, Missed Occurrences and Shopping List Lines, with the same result.
- On the phone, every page with a title and buttons in its top bar shows the back button and the other buttons on the top row and the title on its own line under them, wrapped onto a second line instead of cut off, while a wide screen, such as a tablet, keeps the title beside the buttons.
- On the phone and the desktop, a Tally field's unit is typed into one field, under which the known units whose symbol or English or German name starts with what is typed show as suggestions in the Device's unit system, such as kg, km and km/h for "k", on the desktop as small buttons that name their kind on hover, a tap or click on one picks that known unit and anything else typed stays a unit of the user's own, instead of a list of every known unit and "Another unit".
- On the phone and the desktop, the Note editor shows the Note's settings as one wrapping row of badges under its text, each an icon with its value, for the Importance, the Area and Topic, when and where it was captured, each Routine and Reminder with its schedule, the kind of a Checklist, Shopping List or Tally, Show on the Agenda and Show ticks, where a tap or click opens a small editor that changes the setting, and the "+" at the end of the row offers Routine, Reminder, Frog and Session.
- On the desktop, the window has a menu bar with File, Edit, View, Note, Sync and Help, a sidebar on the left with the Agenda, Unsorted, All Notes, Frogs and the Areas and Topics that marks the place shown, and above the list back and forward buttons that, like Alt+Left, Alt+Right and the mouse's side buttons, go through the places shown as a browser does instead of one level up, beside a clickable path such as "Areas › Household › Repairs", a Note opens as a dialog over the list, one at a time, instead of in a pane on the right, keeping what was typed when it closes, while the Settings, Sync, Pair a Device, People, Profile, Backups and About open from the menu bar as dialogs over the window instead of as views in it reached through the Profile picture.
- On the phone and the desktop, the button that opens the Trash shows a bin with an arrow pointing up instead of the plain bin, which deletes.

### Fixed

- In the phone's two-row editor, the row above the keyboard shows the cursor's line from its start with the cursor right after the text before it, and scrolls sideways only when the line is wider than the row, to keep the cursor in view.
- On the phone, the Note editor's table button opens the full-screen view on the table it just put in, its first cell being edited, also in a plain Note holding a box typed since the last save, where the view stayed empty.
- On the phone, the Note editor offers Frog and Session beside Routine and Reminder, opening what the card's menu opens, and on the phone and the desktop All Routines and Reminders has "New Session", which makes a Session whose Note is the text typed in its editor.
- On the phone, every page with a floating button, the Agenda, the Areas list and its search results, an Area, a Topic, Named Places and the screen of the App Lock, scrolls its last row fully above the button, so its ⋮, checkbox and text can be reached.
- On the desktop, an Unsorted card can be moved into an Area or Topic by dragging it onto the sidebar, with File into… in its right-click menu or with Ctrl+M, where it could not be moved before.
