# Changelog

All notable changes to Old Brain Fix are documented in this file.
Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/); versioning: [SemVer](https://semver.org/).

## [0.4.0]

### Added

- On the phone and the desktop, a field of a Tally can multiply another field of the same Tally, such as Rounds of Push-ups: its page has "Multiplies" with Nothing and the other fields, and the setup shows it as a badge. In each entry the other field counts as the two values multiplied, so 3 rounds of 10 push-ups count as 30 in the totals, targets and charts, and the entry shows "Push-ups 3 × 10 times"; an entry without a value for the multiplier counts as it is. The multiplier has no total, chart or target of its own. It is read from the entries as they are, so older entries count the same; hiding the field it multiplies makes it a field of its own again. On the desktop the AI port sets it up and reads entries with the product.
- On the phone and the desktop, Settings has an Agenda section with "Hide what's checked on the Agenda", off by default and kept for each Device. Switched on, a Done Occurrence, a Checklist Routine's checked Line and a Session's pick with its entry leave the Agenda at once instead of staying struck through, and a group with nothing left goes with them, "Eat a frog" too once today's Frog is eaten; the phone's widget follows. On the desktop the AI port reads the setting.
- On the phone and the desktop, a Routine or Reminder is checked off from its Note: the Note editor shows at the top the Occurrence that is due, such as "Due 15:00", or else the latest Missed one with its day, with Done, and for a one-time Reminder, the Note's only one, also "Done and move to trash", which moves the Note with the Reminder to the trash and closes the editor. A Note's card, compact or detailed, has a box for today's Occurrence of each of its Routines and Reminders, which checks it off and unchecks it as the Agenda's box does; a long press on the card still opens the editor. Snooze stays in the notification.
- On the phone and the desktop, the pool of a Session, on its Note's page, the New Session page and the Routine editor, shows the #tags found in its Tallies' texts above the list, such as #sport and #cleaning, those most Tallies carry first. Picking one or more shows only the Tallies carrying any of them; ticked Tallies the filter hides stay ticked, and a line says how many. New Tally from a filtered list starts the new Tally's name with the picked tags. The filter is not saved and lasts while the page is open; Rolling's order is changed only in the whole list.
- On the phone and the desktop, Settings ▸ Agenda has "Move a Note to the Trash when its one-time Reminder is done", off by default. It is a setting of the person, the same on all of their own Devices, and never reaches anyone else. Switched on, Done on a one-time Reminder that is a plain Note's only Routine or Reminder moves the Note with it to the trash, wherever it is checked off: the Agenda, the phone's widget and full-screen alarm, a notification, the Note editor or dialog, which closes, and the card. Checklists, Shopping Lists, Tallies, Sessions and Notes with a Routine or a second Reminder stay. Unchecking does not bring the Note back; it comes back from the trash, still Done. On the desktop the AI port reads the setting.
- On the phone and the desktop, a formatted text holds code blocks: lines between two fences of three backticks or tildes show in a fixed-width font on a grey background, exactly as written, with no format, table, #tag or box read inside. A word after the opening fence, such as "```bash", names the language and is kept without showing. As in CommonMark, a block ends only at a fence of the same character at least as long as the opening one, so a block opened with four backticks keeps a line of three, and a block without a closing fence runs to the end of the text. "Code block" in the format bar puts the lines of the selection into a block, or takes a block's fences off. Before, fenced code showed as written.
- On the phone and the desktop, a plain Note's whole text can be Code: it shows exactly as written in a fixed-width font on a grey background, without line wrapping, and nothing in it is read as a format, a #tag or a box. In the Note editor it scrolls sideways; on a card it is cut off at the edge. The editor's new "Text format" badge switches between Plain text, Formatted text and Code; a formatted text can become Code but still never plain again. When a pasted text looks like a script or code, such as one starting with "#!/" or lines ending in ";" or "{", the editor offers "Keep as code" under the text, and the paste does not format the text. Search finds the words of a Code text, and sharing and copying give it as written. A Note with a Code text can't be made a Checklist, Shopping List, Tally or Session until its text format is changed back. Older versions of the app show a Code text read-only. On the desktop the AI port reads the format of a Note's text and sets it.
- On the phone and the desktop, every Note has a History, on the Note editor's bar on the phone and under Note ▸ History on the desktop. It lists every change, the newest first, with who made it and when: the text before and after, Amends added and changed, Lines added, renamed, checked, unchecked, deleted and restored, the Importance, the Area and Topic, the kind, the text format, the trash, and photos and recordings. "Use this version" brings an older text of the Note or of an Amend back as a new change; nothing is deleted. On the desktop the AI port reads it.
- On the phone and the desktop, a web address written into a text, starting with "http://" or "https://", is a link: a tap or click on it opens it in the browser, in plain and formatted texts, on cards, in Sections and in the read views of the Note editor and dialog. A tap beside it still switches the card, and a long press still opens the editor. Punctuation right after the address, such as a full stop or a closing bracket, is not part of it. An address inside code, a code block, a Code text or a link stays as it is, and a #tag or a box inside an address is none. The text itself is not changed.
- One switch, Simple or Advanced, on the phone and the desktop. The first start asks it on the Starter Set page, Simple picked; Settings changes it any time. It is a setting of the person, the same on all of their own Devices. Simple offers making Notes, Checklists, Shopping Lists, Reminders and Routines; Advanced adds Tallies, Sessions, Frogs, Topics, the format bar and roles in Shared Areas. What was made before stays visible and usable in Simple. Someone who never chose, as everyone updating from 0.3.1, has Advanced. On the desktop the AI port reads the choice.
- On the phone, Settings ▸ Look has "Fat fingers", off by default and kept for each Device. Switched on, text shows 1.3 times its size on top of the system's font size, and icons, buttons and tap targets 1.3 times theirs; the Agenda's rows grow with them, and so does the text of the home-screen widget. Actions that no longer fit on a page's bar, such as some of the Note editor's, go into ⋮ at its end. On the desktop, Settings ▸ This computer has "Bigger text and icons", off by default, which zooms the window to 1.3 times. The AI port reads the setting.
- On the phone and the desktop, the app explains itself with its own Notes, in their own Area "Old Brain Fix" with a brain icon: "Quick start", a Checklist of steps to try that shows on the Agenda as one row with its count, and "What the icons mean", with pictures of the icons and their names and meanings in its text. An update also brings a "What's new in X.Y.Z" Note with that release's notes, one for each version passed. Each Note is made once per person, in the language of the Device that makes it first; the user's other Devices get it by sync and make none, and a deleted one does not come back. The Area icon "brain" can be picked for any Area.
- On the phone, "Export as Pack…" in the menu of an Area, a Topic and a Note, on a card's ⋮ and on the Note editor's bar, makes a Pack: a file of copies of Notes for another Old Brain Fix user, shared through any messenger, mail or files. A tree shows what it holds, every Note ticked with its Routines, Photo Texts and Transcripts, while Reminders, photos and recordings start unticked; "Tick all", "Untick all" and the size update as you tick. A Note brings what it is, its text, Amends, kind, Lines unticked, Tally fields, Session pool, Routines and an open Frog, never Tally entries, ticks, check-offs, its Capture Context or its history; ticking a Session ticks the Tallies it picks from, and a Session without one stays out. "Add my Profile" signs the Pack with your Profile. Opened with the app, a Pack shows the same tree, all ticked, and "Add" puts the copies into new Areas and Topics, "Health (Pack)" where a Health exists, Routines starting that day and Reminders already past left out. With a Profile it says who it is from, offers to add them to People, and "Claim as mine" unticked leaves each Note a "From Ann" badge until you remove it; a changed Pack and one from a person you blocked are refused. The desktop can't make or open Packs yet; its core reads and writes the same files, and its AI port reads who a Note came from.
- On the phone and the desktop, the schedule in the Routine editor starts with a line, "When", that shows it as a sentence in the app's language, such as "Weekdays Mon, Thu at 08:00, 15:00, until 12/31". Typing in it sets the repeat, the times, the start and the end as you type, with the next word, weekday names and times offered under it; words it does not understand are marked and change nothing. It covers everything the Simple schedule can do; on an Advanced schedule it shows a summary and "Switch to Simple to type". Only the schedule is saved, not the sentence.
- The guide has a page on syncing through Syncthing or Autosync.
- The guide has a page on calendar apps such as FamilyWall: their appointments on the phone's Agenda through the phone's calendar, and Routines and Reminders exported into them as a calendar file.

### Changed

- On the phone and the desktop, a place captured within a Named Place shows only the Named Place's name, such as "Home", on the cards, in the Captured badge and its dialog and with each Section, where it also showed the coordinates; a place without a Named Place still shows its coordinates.
- On the phone and the desktop, the Capture Context of a Note and of each Section shows as one line under its text, smaller and in a lighter colour, so the text reads first: its time, place, calendar events, Bluetooth devices and the app it was shared from, joined by " · ", such as "12:40 · Home · Dentist", the Note's on its full card and each Section's on the full card and in the Note editor, where it showed above the text on full cards, with a line of its own for each detail. A Section captured the same day as the part before it shows only its time, and in the Note editor a Section's line opens its Captured details, each with Remove.
- On the phone and the desktop, a Checklist, a Shopping List or a Tally starts with its name, the first line of its text, as a heading, and its description, the rest of the text, small under it and folded to one line until a tap unfolds it; its Lines or its Tally follow right after, then the badges, and the Amends at the end. In the Note editor the name and the description are two fields, saved back as one text; no Note is rewritten for it.
- On the phone and the desktop, the Note editor's kind badge shows on every Note, "Plain Note" too, and changes the kind: a Plain Note becomes a Checklist, a Shopping List or a Tally, a Checklist becomes a Shopping List and a Shopping List a Checklist with their Lines kept, and a list becomes a Plain Note again. The editor's own "Make it a Checklist" and "Make it a Shopping List" buttons are gone.
- On the phone and the desktop, every card in a Note list has a compact view and a detailed view, where a compact card showed the first line and the media and a tap only unfolded it. The compact view shows a Plain Note's first line and the two lines after it, a Checklist's name and "3 of 7 open", a Shopping List's name and "4 open", a Tally's name and each field's total today against its target, and for a Note that runs a Session its next run and "done 4 / skipped 1 this week"; it has no Capture Context and none of the buttons at the bottom, its ⋮ sits top right and offers File into… and Merge into… on the phone. The list's switch shows every card compact or detailed; a tap on a card, on the desktop its view button beside ⋮, switches only that card, and the Device keeps it so until the list's switch changes. A Shopping List's detailed view folds its struck Lines under the open ones until a tap shows them; that fold and a card's unfolded description stay as left on the Device. The Agenda's just-captured card starts detailed.
- On the phone and the desktop, a Tally is read on its card: its detailed view shows "Add entry", "One bar per" Day, Week, Month or Year, one chart per field and its entries, newest first, where they showed only in the Note editor. The latest five entries show and the older ones fold under how many they are until a tap shows them; the length of a bar and that fold are kept per Tally on the Device. In a Shared Area "Show entries" picks whose, your own at first, and the compact view's totals count your own entries too, where they counted everyone's. The Note editor holds only the setup: each field's rule, unit and target as badges, a tap opening the field's page, and Add field.
- On the phone and the desktop, a Session is a kind of Note: the Note holds the pool, how many it picks, the counts of single weekdays and the rule, and any number of its Routines run it, such as Monday and Wednesday at 7:00 and Saturday at 10:00. Before, each Session was one Routine with its own pool. The Note editor's kind badge makes a Plain Note a Session and back. Its page shows the pool with New Tally, the count with − and +, and the rule and the weekdays' counts as badges; "+" adds a Routine that runs it, and a Routine's editor holds only its schedule. Rolling goes on from one Routine's run to the next. The detailed card shows the pool, today's picks to record and how often it was done and skipped in each of the last four weeks; the compact card shows the next run of any of its Routines and this week's runs of all of them. A Session made with an earlier version keeps its pool and its picks and reads as a Session Note; nothing is rewritten for it.
- On the desktop the AI port reads a Session's setup on its Note with the Routines that run it, and sets up a Note as a Session or changes its setup, as a change that waits for the review.
- On the desktop the AI port reads the name and description of a Note of a kind, and can turn a Checklist into a Shopping List or back as a change that waits for the review.
- On the phone and the desktop, an all-day Routine or Reminder rings from 15:00, where it rang from 09:00 and then Re-nagged all day; before that time it only shows on the Agenda. The time is set for each Device in Settings under Alarms, "All-day items remind from". When the Quiet Hours hold that time, its first ring waits until they end. A calendar file from Export or Share gives an all-day event its alarm at the same time, and the AI port's read_settings lists it.
- On the phone and the desktop, each badge of the Note editor, the Tally setup and the New Tally and New Session pages starts with one icon for its setting, the same whatever the value, such as one for the kind. A setting with a few fixed values shows its value as an icon, such as the kind, the Importance, Show on the Agenda, Show ticks and the rule of a Tally field or a Session; any other value shows as short text, such as "Household". A badge no longer shows an icon next to the same word, the dialog of a badge still names everything in words, and a screen reader still reads each badge as its setting and its value.
- On the phone and the desktop, the Agenda shows a Shopping List, a Checklist without a Routine, a Checklist Routine and a Session as a heading with its entries indented under it, each with its box. A Shopping List showing its open Lines is its name with those Lines under it, where each Line was a row of its own; a Checklist is its name with its open Lines, checkable there, where it was one row "Before the trip: 3 of 7 open"; a Session's picks have boxes, where they were bullets. A Session's own box only shows Done: it checks itself once every pick has an entry, and a tap on it does nothing. The Agenda's rows are tighter, with boxes and buttons still 40 dp on the phone. The home-screen Agenda widget shows the same groups: a Shopping List's and a Checklist's name with their open Lines indented under it, each with its box, which checks it there, a Session with its picks under it, each with its box, and a tap on a pick opens its entry form.
- On the desktop, buttons look like buttons: rounded rectangles with a fill and an outline in light and dark, darker or lighter under the pointer and while pressed, with a ring while they have the keyboard focus. The main action of a dialog or page, such as Save, Done or Create, is on the teal accent; Delete, Remove and Move to Trash are on the error colour. Links stay links, and icon-only buttons such as ⋮ and Edit get the same rounded background under the pointer.
- On the phone and the desktop, a Checklist without a Routine has the same three choices as a Shopping List under "Show on the Agenda" in its card's ⋮ and its editor's badge, where it had an on/off switch: Off, one row with the number of its open Lines, such as "Before the trip: 3 of 7 open", or each open Line under its name. A Checklist switched off stays off and one switched on shows each open Line; the home-screen widget shows it the same way. On the desktop the AI port reads the choice with the Checklist and sets it as a change that waits for the review.
- On the phone, a folder first in the Agenda's top bar, "Search and browse", opens the Areas list, where the small floating magnifier above "+" opened it. Only "+" floats on the Agenda; the search by text and by #tag stays in the notes browser.
- On the phone, the home-screen Agenda widget shows the same days as the Agenda: today, then tomorrow and the five days after it, each under its heading with its Occurrences and appointments, where it showed only today. It scrolls through them. Only today's rows and the Missed Occurrences are checked off on the widget; a tap on a later day's row opens the app.
- On the phone and the desktop, each Line of a Shopping List ends in a badge with its reappear time in the Note editor: "—" for none, "Once", or a number with a unit, such as "4w" or "3m". A tap opens None, Once or After with a number from 1 to 99 and day, week, month or year, where it was Once or one to four weeks in the Line's ⋮. Months and years go by the calendar, to the last day of a shorter month. A new Line starts without a reappear time and, once checked, stays struck through until one is set. Lines keep the weeks they had. Older versions of the app read a time they do not know as one week. On the desktop the AI port reads and sets the number and the unit.
- The user guide on the website starts each section with a short box: what it is for in one line, at most five steps, Phone and Desktop tabs, each with its picture in the light or dark theme, and the rest folded under "More details". The last tab picked is remembered in the browser. The guide's start page lists every box as an everyday task, such as "Import notes from Google Keep", linked to it. Getting started is the first page in this form, describing the first start with Simple or Advanced; the other pages follow.
- On the phone and the desktop, the quick note always has an alarm clock beside "To full editor", in Simple too, where it showed only once Settings ▸ Quick note switched it on: its menu offers Routine and Reminder, as the Note editor's "+" does. The Routine or Reminder editor opens over the quick note, and what is set up there is saved together with the Note; a quick note left with nothing typed saves neither. The switch in Settings ▸ Quick note is now "Frog, Session or Tally", only in Advanced: it adds Frog and Session to the clock's menu and "Make it a Tally" to ⋮, on the desktop to More.
- On the phone and the desktop, a short choice of one value is a row of choice chips with one selected, styled like the Routine editor's weekdays, where it was a column of radio buttons. This covers the Routine editor's choices, the reappear time of a Shopping List's Line, the Importance, kind and text format badges, a Tally's rule and "One bar per", and on the phone the theme and the minutes in Settings. A choice with long options or a detail under each, such as Show on the Agenda or a Session's rule, stays radio buttons. A screen reader reads the chips as one group and the chosen one as selected; on the desktop Tab and the arrow keys move between them and Space picks one.

### Fixed

- On the phone and the desktop the automatic sync with the other Devices and through the shared folder runs beside the app, on a connection of its own to the data, so the app stays usable while it merges what came in, where the phone did not answer for seconds after it started. While a sync of this Device runs, a thin line in the accent moves back and forth under the top bar, on the desktop under the menu bar; with "remove animations" on the phone it stands still, and a screen reader hears "Syncing…" once each time the app comes into view.
- On the phone and the desktop, a Line just typed into a Checklist or a Shopping List in the Note editor shows its ⋮ as soon as it holds text, where it showed only after Return and Backspace. An entry of its ⋮ stores the Line first, then acts; an empty new Line has no ⋮.
- On the phone the Agenda reads its rows once per change, so it opens and updates faster: a check-off, a sync, a calendar read or coming back to the app read them once, where each read them two or three times.
- On the phone and the desktop, a Session's picks and entries are read once per change, so the Agenda loads faster: they were read again for every run so far, several times per load. On the phone the home-screen Agenda widget takes the Agenda's rows instead of reading them a second time. Whether a Note is a Tally, and which kind each Note is, are read without going through every Tally entry.
- On the phone the announcement after an unexpected stop opens a dialog that says what happened, with Close and Report, where its tap opened the mail at once. Closed, it does not come back before the next unexpected stop. A freeze while the app was in the background, which the phone's energy management ends, no longer counts as an unexpected stop.
- On the phone a quick, short swipe between the Agenda and the levels of the browser switches as soon as the finger lets go, where a flick that passed the touch threshold only with its last move did nothing, and the pages start where the finger is. The browser comes in without the Agenda under it being built again, so the change no longer stutters as it starts.
- On the phone and the desktop the Sync page says that Simple or Advanced and the Trash setting sync to your own Devices and other settings stay on each Device, where it said that no settings are synced.
