# Changelog

All notable changes to Old Brain Fix are documented in this file.

## [0.7.2]

### Changed

- On the phone and the desktop, moving an Area to the trash and importing from Google Keep are faster when you use a Shared Area. ([obf-1092](https://github.com/paxel/oldbrainfix/issues/405))
- On the phone and the desktop, the app answers faster when you keep many Notes. ([obf-1069](https://github.com/paxel/oldbrainfix/issues/382))
- On the phone and the desktop, typing in a search field no longer waits; the results follow shortly after you stop typing. ([obf-1068](https://github.com/paxel/oldbrainfix/issues/381))
- On the desktop, pages open faster while the alarms are planned in the background. ([obf-1091](https://github.com/paxel/oldbrainfix/issues/404))
- On the phone and the desktop, the app stays responsive after importing photos or voice recordings while their text is read. ([obf-1094](https://github.com/paxel/oldbrainfix/issues/407))
- On the phone and the desktop, the app opens faster; the desktop shows its window while the Agenda is still read. ([obf-1090](https://github.com/paxel/oldbrainfix/issues/403))

### Fixed

- On the phone, More on the quick note opens the Note editor even while a tip shows, instead of crashing the app. ([obf-1058](https://github.com/paxel/oldbrainfix/issues/371))
- On the phone, restarting the app after a crash no longer makes it crash again shortly after. ([obf-1059](https://github.com/paxel/oldbrainfix/issues/372))
- On the phone, an Import no longer freezes the app: it runs beside the app on a page counting its Notes, which you can leave. ([obf-1061](https://github.com/paxel/oldbrainfix/issues/374))
- On the phone, moving an Area to the trash no longer freezes the app: its row counts the Notes moved while the app stays usable. ([obf-1093](https://github.com/paxel/oldbrainfix/issues/406))
